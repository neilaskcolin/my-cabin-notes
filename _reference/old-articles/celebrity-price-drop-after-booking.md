Yes, before final payment. Celebrity generally lets a booking move to a lower publicly advertised fare for the same ship, sailing date and cabin category, usually keeping your cabin. After final payment the answer is mostly no, apart from the odd goodwill gesture.

## How Celebrity handles a lower fare before final payment
Final payment falls 75 to 120 days before sailing depending on cruise length, and 90 days for most 5–14 night sailings; the [Celebrity cancellation policy](/articles/celebrity-cancellation-policy-final-payment/) has the full schedule. Until then, a lower fare can be applied through whoever holds the booking, Celebrity or your travel agent. Expect to give up any promotion tied to the old fare and take whatever comes with the new one.

Celebrity has also offered a short best price guarantee window right after booking, much like sister line Royal Caribbean. Treat it as a backstop; repricing before final payment is where most of the savings come from.

## Cruise-only vs All Included: compare like with like
Celebrity often lists the same cabin two ways. A drop in the cruise-only fare isn't a saving if you booked All Included and would lose the Classic drinks package and Basic wifi.

| Illustrative couple, 7 nights | Your booking | New offer |
|---|---|---|
| Fare type | All Included | Cruise-only |
| Fare for two | US$4,200 | US$3,300 |
| Drinks and wifi bought separately | Included | about US$1,640 |
| Real total | US$4,200 | about US$4,940 |

The US$1,640 assumes the Classic package at about US$85 a day each plus the 20% service charge, and one Premium wifi device at around US$30 a day. Neither fare includes daily gratuities, which Celebrity removed from All Included in late 2023, so they don't change the comparison.

> **Good to know:** it works the other way too. If the All Included fare drops further than the cruise-only fare, a cruise-only booking may be able to step up to All Included for little extra. Ask for both prices.

## How to claim a Celebrity price drop
1. Note your fare type, cabin category and any promotion attached to the booking.
2. Check the same sailing and category regularly, at the same fare type. [Setting cruise price alerts](/articles/how-to-set-cruise-price-alerts/) makes this easier.
3. Compare totals, including taxes, inclusions and any onboard credit you would lose.
4. Ask whoever holds the booking to reprice before final payment, and get a revised invoice.
5. Check your cabin number is unchanged on the new confirmation.

## Non-refundable deposit fares
Celebrity's cheaper non-refundable deposit fares come with tighter change rules. Repricing may be possible but can carry a fee, and the deposit stays non-refundable. Ask about any fee before you accept a new fare, and choose your fare type at booking with this in mind, as explained in [cruise fare classes explained](/articles/cruise-fare-classes-refundable/).

## The Canadian check
- Compare CAD with CAD. Celebrity sells to Canadian residents in Canadian dollars, and US-dollar prices on American sites or forums aren't the same offer.
- A stronger loonie doesn't lower your fare automatically; only a published CAD price change counts. See [how exchange rate swings affect cruise pricing](/articles/exchange-rate-cruise-pricing-canadians/).
- If you paid in US dollars on a Canadian card, any refund of the difference comes back at that day's exchange rate.

## After final payment
There is no cash refund of the difference. Occasionally Celebrity offers an upgrade at the price difference or a goodwill onboard credit, and one polite request costs nothing. The wider picture across lines is in [cruise price drop after final payment](/articles/price-drop-after-booking-cruise/).
