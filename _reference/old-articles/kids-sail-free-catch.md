"Kids sail free" is the most tempting phrase in family cruising, and it is a real saving. It just isn't zero. Here is what the fine print usually says and how to tell whether it beats the other offers on your dates.

## What does "kids sail free" actually mean?
On participating sailings, the third and fourth guests in a cabin pay no cruise fare when they share with two adults paying the regular fare. Those extra guests sleep on the sofa bed or pull-down bunks. For the line, it fills berths that would otherwise sail empty.

Typical conditions include:

- Children must be the third and fourth guests in the same cabin as two full-fare adults.
- An age limit applies, which varies by line and offer.
- Only select sailings and cabin categories qualify, often shoulder-season dates.
- Cabins that sleep four are limited and sell out first.
- The offer may not combine with other promotions or discounted fares.

## What does "free" still cost?
Taxes and port fees are charged per person whatever the fare, commonly around US$100–300 per child. Gratuities are charged daily, and most lines bill children at the full or near-full rate, with a few reducing or waiving them for the youngest guests. Some promotions also pair free kids with less-discounted adult fares.

## Does it beat the alternatives? A worked example
A family of four from Winnipeg looks at a 7-night Caribbean sailing from Florida. These figures are illustrative, in US dollars, with taxes and gratuities included for all four guests.

| Option | Fares | Taxes and gratuities | Cruise total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kids sail free, shoulder date | Adults US$1,100 each, kids free | about US$1,050 | about US$3,250 |
| Regular third and fourth guest pricing | Adults US$1,000 each, kids US$450 each | about US$1,050 | about US$3,950 |
| Two cabins, one adult and one child in each | Four fares at US$1,000 | about US$1,050 | about US$5,050 |

In this example, kids sail free saves about US$700, roughly C$960, against regular third and fourth guest pricing. It saves about US$1,800 against two cabins. The point is to compare totals: the adult fares on a promotion are not always the lowest available.

> **Quick math:** a "free" child still costs about US$150 in taxes plus roughly US$16 a day in gratuities, so around US$260 for a week. That's excellent value against a typical third-guest fare, but it should be in your budget.

## The Canadian catch: school calendars and flights
The promotion clusters on quieter sailings, which are the weeks Canadian families find hardest to use. March break falls on different dates by province, and Christmas and summer are peak for everyone, so qualifying sailings on those weeks are rare and go fast. [Cruising around Canadian holidays](/articles/cruise-canadian-long-weekends-holidays/) helps you find softer weeks.

Flights are the other catch. Four return seats from Winnipeg, Calgary or Halifax to Florida can easily cost more than the whole cruise saving, especially in March. Price the flights before you celebrate the fare.

## When kids sail free wins
- Families of four happy in one cabin.
- Families with some date flexibility, such as younger children not yet tied to exams.
- Anyone comparing family totals across several offers rather than reading the headline.

## When it doesn't
- You need two cabins or a connecting pair for space or sleep (see [connecting cabins vs one big cabin](/articles/connecting-cabins-one-big-cabin-families/)).
- Your only possible week is peak school holidays.
- A percentage-off or onboard-credit offer gives a lower family total on your dates.

Before you book, check that every child has a valid passport. If one parent is travelling alone with the kids, carry a consent letter from the other parent (see [passports, NEXUS and ID](/articles/passports-nexus-id-cruise-documents/)). Kids clubs are usually included in the fare, as covered in [kids clubs: ages, hours and costs](/articles/kids-clubs-cruise-ships-ages-costs/).

## The bottom line
Treat "kids sail free" as "kids sail for taxes and tips." On the right dates it is often the best family offer available.
