On Celebrity you can smoke or vape only in signed outdoor areas, usually a section of one open deck. Cabins and balconies are smoke-free on every ship, the casino is non-smoking, and breaking the rules brings a cleaning fee of several hundred dollars.

## Where can you smoke on a Celebrity ship?
Designated outdoor areas only. On most ships that means a marked section of an open deck, sometimes near an outdoor bar, with ashtrays provided. Locations are listed in the daily schedule and the app, and Guest Relations can point you to them on embarkation day. Don't assume any open deck qualifies: the main pool area, the adults-only Solarium and the jogging track are all non-smoking.

Cigars and pipes follow the same rule, and hookahs are banned outright. You must be 18 or over to use tobacco on board.

## Where smoking is banned
- All staterooms and balconies, in every category from inside cabins to Retreat suites.
- Restaurants, bars, lounges, the theatre, hallways and lifts.
- The casino, which Celebrity runs as non-smoking; check the daily schedule in case your itinerary differs.
- Children's areas and pool decks outside a marked zone.

## Vaping and e-cigarettes
Celebrity treats vapes exactly like cigarettes: designated outdoor areas only, and vaping on your balcony draws the same cleaning fee as a cigarette. You can bring your device and liquids on board. For the flight to Vancouver, Seattle or Fort Lauderdale, e-cigarettes and spare batteries must travel in your carry-on, not your checked bag.

## The cleaning fee and what happens if you're caught
Smoking in a cabin or on a balcony brings a cleaning fee charged to your onboard account, typically several hundred dollars, and repeat offences can lead to removal from the ship at your own expense. Balcony smoke drifts to neighbours, who complain, and stateroom attendants notice the smell.

## Who this affects most on Celebrity
Celebrity is one of the stricter big lines, which suits its typical guest, who often picks it partly for a calmer, cleaner atmosphere. If you smoke, Celebrity is workable but less convenient than Carnival or Royal Caribbean, where casinos have smoking sections on many ships.

### Choosing a cabin as a smoker
Ask which deck holds the smoking area on your ship before you choose a cabin, then pick one close to the lifts nearest it. On a large Edge-class ship that can save a long walk several times a day; on a smaller Millennium-class ship such as Summit, distances are shorter anyway.

### Cannabis
Cannabis is legal in Canada but banned on Celebrity and every major line, including Alaska sailings from Vancouver, because the ships call at US ports. The reasons are in [why you can't bring cannabis on a cruise from Canada](/articles/cruise-cannabis-canada-why-no/).

> **Canadian tip:** buying cigarettes in the ship's duty-free shop? After 48 hours or more away, each adult can bring 200 cigarettes and 50 cigars back into Canada within the personal exemption, but cigarettes not stamped as Canadian duty paid may still attract a special duty at the border. See [cruise duty-free limits for Canadians](/articles/cruise-shopping-onboard-duty-free/).

## How Celebrity compares
| Line | Smoking in the casino | Cabins and balconies |
|---|---|---|
| **Celebrity** | No — casinos are non-smoking | Banned |
| [Princess](/articles/princess-smoking-vaping-policy/) | Varies by ship | Banned |
| [Royal Caribbean](/articles/royal-caribbean-smoking-vaping-policy/) | Designated section on many ships | Banned |

Princess and Royal Caribbean are the lines most often weighed against Celebrity. Cabins and balconies are smoke-free on all three, so the real difference is the casino. If you like to smoke at the tables, Royal Caribbean, Celebrity's sister line, is the more relaxed choice; if you want smoke-free air throughout, Celebrity is the safer bet. ## Knowing the map before you sail
If smoking areas matter to your trip, ask where they are on your specific ship before you choose a cabin. Locations vary more from ship to ship than from line to line.
