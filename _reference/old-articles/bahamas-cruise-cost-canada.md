A short Bahamas cruise is the cheapest warm-weather sailing for most Canadians, but the fare is only part of the bill. For a couple flying from Toronto or Montreal, a 3–4 night inside cabin typically comes to about C$2,100–4,700 all-in at recent prices, and flights are often the single biggest line.

## What does a Bahamas cruise cost from Toronto or Montreal?
Figures are per couple, for a 3–4 night sailing from Florida in an inside cabin. Cruise costs are usually charged in US dollars and shown here as rough Canadian equivalents.

| Item, per couple | Lower end | Higher end |
|---|---|---|
| Cruise fare, inside | C$690 | C$1,250 |
| Taxes and port fees, if not included | C$250 | C$450 |
| Return flights to Florida | C$600 | C$1,200 |
| Hotel night before | C$200 | C$350 |
| Airport and port transfers | C$40 | C$150 |
| Gratuities | C$175 | C$225 |
| Drinks | C$100 | C$350 |
| Excursions or beach extras | C$0 | C$500 |
| Travel insurance | C$80 | C$250 |
| **Rough total** | **C$2,135** | **C$4,725** |

Gratuities run about US$16–20 per person per day on mainstream lines, or roughly US$130–160 for a couple over four nights. Taxes and fees are a bigger share of the price on short sailings, so check whether a quote includes them before comparing. The lowest fares tend to be on older ships and midweek departures.

A balcony usually adds about C$550–850 per couple for the same short sailing. From Calgary, Edmonton or Vancouver, flights to Florida cost more and take longer, so add several hundred dollars.

## Why flights change the maths
Short cruises have the lowest fares, but your flights cost the same whether you sail for three nights or seven.

> **Quick math:** take the lower-end total above, about C$2,135 for four nights, or roughly C$530 per night for two. A 7-night Caribbean sailing for the same couple might total around C$3,300 at the low end, or about C$470 per night. The longer cruise costs more overall but often less per night.

If you have the time, compare with [Caribbean cruise costs from Canada](/articles/caribbean-cruise-cost-canada-budget/). If you only have a long weekend, the Bahamas is hard to beat.

## Where do Bahamas cruises go?
Most sail from Miami, Fort Lauderdale or Port Canaveral, near Orlando. Port Canaveral sailings pair well with a few days at Orlando's theme parks, which can make the flight worthwhile for families. Itineraries usually include one or two of these:

- **Nassau:** the capital, with beaches, a busy straw market and day passes to resort water parks.
- **Freeport, Grand Bahama:** quieter, with beaches and nature parks.
- **Private islands:** run by the cruise lines, such as Royal Caribbean's Perfect Day at CocoCay, Norwegian's Great Stirrup Cay, MSC's Ocean Cay and Carnival's Celebration Key.

Private islands are free to visit, though cabanas, water parks and some activities cost extra. [Private island cruise stops explained](/articles/private-island-cruise-stops-explained/) covers what's included.

## Who a Bahamas cruise suits
- First-time cruisers testing the idea before committing to a week.
- Couples or friends wanting a quick winter escape over a long weekend.
- Groups celebrating something short and sweet.

It is less suited to anyone who wants several ports or time to settle into ship life. Short sailings also attract a party crowd, especially on weekends.

> **Canadian tip:** three- and four-night sailings often fit a long weekend. Family Day in February is popular, so book early, and see [cruising around Canadian long weekends](/articles/cruise-canadian-long-weekends-holidays/) for timing.

## How to keep the cost down
1. **Fly the day before.** Missing a 3-night cruise is as costly as missing a 7-night one, and winter storms in Toronto and Montreal are a real risk. See [snow delays and winter flights](/articles/snow-delay-winter-flights-caribbean-cruise/).
2. **Compare airports.** Fort Lauderdale and Orlando can be cheaper than Miami from Canada.
3. **Avoid March break,** when flights and fares jump.
4. **Skip the drink package** on a short sailing unless you'll clearly drink enough to justify it.
5. **Enjoy the private island beach** rather than paying for a cabana.
6. **Use a card with no foreign transaction fees** for the onboard account.

## Documents
Canadians need a valid passport to fly to the US and to board. Florida embarkation also involves US customs on the way back; [cruise check-in documents for Canadians](/articles/cruise-check-in-online-documents/) lists what to have ready.

## The bottom line
A Bahamas cruise is the lowest-cost way for Canadians to try cruising, but flights make it pricier per night than it first looks. If you can spare a week, price a 7-night sailing too. If you can't, a long-weekend Bahamas trip is a great short break.
