A 7-night Norwegian fjords cruise typically costs a Canadian couple C$6,000–10,500 all-in for an inside cabin. The cruise fare is roughly in line with the Mediterranean, but long flights, a hotel night in a pricey European city and some of the highest shore prices anywhere push the total up.

## Norway or Alaska: the cost comparison
Most Canadians weighing Norway have done, or are considering, Alaska. Here is how a 7-night inside-cabin trip compares for a Calgary couple, at recent prices.

| Per couple, from Calgary | Norwegian fjords | Alaska from Vancouver |
|---|---|---|
| Rough all-in total | C$6,000–10,500 | C$4,500–8,500 |
| Flight time each way | 9–12+ hours, often one connection | About 1.5 hours |
| Time difference | 8 hours ahead of Alberta | 1 hour behind Alberta |
| Hotel night before | London, Amsterdam or Copenhagen, C$250–450 | Vancouver, C$250–450 |
| Shore costs | Very high, in Norwegian kroner | High, in US dollars |
| Scenery | Narrow fjords, waterfalls, farms on cliffs | Glaciers, wildlife, wilderness |

## The fare itself
At recent prices, inside cabins on a 7-night fjords sailing run roughly US$750–1,150 per person, and balconies about US$1,300–2,100. That is about C$2,050–3,150 per couple for an inside cabin.

A balcony is easier to justify here than on most itineraries. Ships spend hours sailing narrow fjords such as the Geirangerfjord, with sheer walls close on both sides, and much of the best viewing happens before breakfast or late in the long northern evening.

Longer 10–14 night sailings that cross the Arctic Circle to the North Cape cost more but bring midnight-sun evenings in early summer.

## Getting to the ship from Canada
Return flights to a northern European gateway typically cost C$900–1,700 per person.

### Southampton
London Heathrow has nonstop flights from most large Canadian cities. Southampton is roughly 1.5–2 hours away by coach or car, so most people stay a night in London or near the port. Cruise-line transfers are convenient but pricier than the train.

### Amsterdam, Copenhagen and German ports
KLM flies nonstop to Amsterdam from several Canadian cities, which makes Amsterdam departures easy. Copenhagen, Hamburg and Kiel usually mean a connection. Kiel is about 90 minutes from Hamburg, so check the transfer before booking.

> **Example:** a Calgary couple flying to London on a sale fare in May, with one night near Southampton and an inside cabin, could land near C$6,500. The same trip in July with a balcony, two nights in central London and a few tours can reach C$12,000.

## What it costs ashore
This is where Norway differs from every other cruise region. Prices in Bergen, Stavanger or Ålesund are high by Canadian standards, and a beer or lunch can cost noticeably more than in Juneau or Barcelona.

The good news is that the best things are often free:
- Walking Bergen's old Bryggen wharf, then hiking up Mount Fløyen instead of paying for the funicular
- Walking the waterfall trail up from Geiranger village for a view back over the fjord
- Strolling the Art Nouveau streets of Ålesund and climbing the steps to Aksla viewpoint
- Exploring old Stavanger's white wooden houses

Paid highlights, such as the Flåm railway, fjord kayaking or glacier trips from Olden, typically run US$100–250 per person through the ship. Budget C$500–1,200 per couple for excursions.

> **Good to know:** Norway uses the Norwegian krone, not the euro, and cards are accepted almost everywhere, even for small amounts. A no-foreign-transaction-fee credit card means you barely need cash.

## Onboard spending
Gratuities run about US$16–20 per person per day, roughly C$320–420 per couple for the week. Drinks add C$300–600 without a package. Pack rain gear and layers: summer days in the fjords are often 12–20°C and Bergen is famous for rain.

## When the fjords are worth the extra
Norway earns its premium for couples who have already done Alaska and want similar drama with a very different culture, or who want to combine a cruise with time in London, Amsterdam or Copenhagen. If you have never cruised Alaska and live in the West, Alaska gives you comparable scenery for less money and no jet lag. Eastern Canadians, who face a longer flight to Vancouver, will find the gap smaller. Compare with the [Mediterranean cruise budget](/articles/mediterranean-cruise-cost-canada/) if you are choosing between European regions.
