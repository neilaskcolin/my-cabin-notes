Hawaii cruises split into two very different budgets for Canadians. Fly to Honolulu and sail a week around the islands, or board in Vancouver for a seasonal crossing with several sea days. The second can cost less, though not for the reason most people assume.

## How much does a Hawaii cruise cost from Canada?
Rough per-couple totals at recent prices, inside cabin, converted at about 1.37. Balconies add roughly 40–70% to the fare line.

| Per couple, CAD | Fly to Honolulu, 7 nights | Vancouver one-way, about 10 nights |
|---|---|---|
| Cruise fare | C$2,750–5,500 | C$2,500–4,100 |
| Taxes and port fees | C$400–700 | C$400–800 |
| Flights | C$1,200–3,200 return | C$800–1,600 one way home |
| Hotel in Honolulu | C$300–550, one night | Usually not needed |
| Gratuities | C$390–480 | C$440–500 |
| Drinks and extras | C$300–700 | C$400–900 |
| Excursions and car rental | C$800–2,000 | C$600–1,500 |
| Travel insurance | C$200–600 | C$200–600 |
| **Rough total** | **C$6,300–13,500** | **C$5,300–10,000** |

These ranges are wide because Hawaii prices swing with season, cabin and departure city. Confirm current fares for your dates. The Vancouver column assumes you live near the port; a couple from Calgary or Edmonton should add roughly C$300–700 to reach Canada Place, or the cost of driving and parking (see [cruising from Vancouver without flying](/articles/cruise-from-vancouver-without-flying/)).

## Route one: fly to Honolulu
The year-round week-long island cruise is Norwegian's Pride of America, which is US-flagged and so can sail round trip from Honolulu without a foreign port. It calls at Maui, the Big Island and Kauai, with overnights on some islands. Fares run higher per night than most Caribbean cruises, partly because there is little competition.

Other lines reach Hawaii on longer round trips from Los Angeles or San Diego, usually 14 nights or more with a stop in Ensenada, Mexico. Those add more sea days and a flight to California.

### Flights from Canada
Vancouver has the most direct service to Honolulu, and Calgary sees seasonal options. From Toronto or Montreal, expect a long day with a connection or a seasonal nonstop, which is why the flight line in the table is so wide. Fly in at least a day early, since [flying the day before a cruise](/articles/fly-day-before-cruise/) matters even more on a six-hour flight.

## Route two: sail from Vancouver
As ships move between Alaska and their winter regions, they sail from Canada Place to Hawaii in spring and fall. These voyages typically include four or five sea days crossing the Pacific, then several island calls. [Hawaii cruises from Vancouver](/articles/hawaii-cruises-from-vancouver/) covers how they work.

### Why it can cost less
Repositioning fares are usually lower per night than the same ship's Alaska or island rates (see [repositioning cruises explained](/articles/repositioning-cruises-explained/)). A one-way voyage still needs a flight home from Honolulu, but that is one flight instead of two. Round-trip versions from Vancouver remove flights entirely but run longer, often two weeks or more, so extra gratuities and onboard spending eat into the saving.

> **Example:** a Vancouver couple books a 10-night fall sailing to Honolulu at about US$1,100 each. Fare, taxes and tips come to roughly C$4,000, a one-way flight home adds about C$1,000, and modest excursions, drinks and insurance bring the trip to around C$6,800. The same couple flying to Honolulu for the 7-night island cruise would likely spend more for fewer nights.

## What you spend ashore in Hawaii
- **Car rental** is often the best value on Maui, the Big Island and Kauai. Book early and budget for parking and fuel.
- **Luaus and helicopter tours** are the big-ticket items, with helicopter flights commonly several hundred US dollars per person.
- **Pearl Harbor and beaches** are inexpensive, and many top beaches are free.
- **Prices ashore are high** for food and drinks, similar to a pricey US resort town.

All charges on board post in US dollars, so a no-foreign-transaction-fee card helps. Canadians don't need an ESTA for Hawaii, as explained in [visas and ESTA for Canadians](/articles/canadians-visa-esta-cruise-us-port/).

## When is the best time to go?
Island cruises from Honolulu run all year, with humpback whales around the islands from roughly December to April. Christmas and March break are the priciest weeks for Canadian families. Vancouver sailings only run in their spring and fall windows and sell steadily, so book early. The crossing suits people who enjoy [sea days more than port days](/articles/port-days-vs-sea-days-itinerary-balance/).

## Which route fits you?
Western Canadians with time and a liking for sea days should price the Vancouver voyage first. Eastern Canadians, families on school calendars and anyone who wants maximum island time will usually prefer flying to Honolulu. Either way, add flights, a hotel night and excursions before comparing fares.
