Celebrity allows two 750 ml bottles of wine or champagne per stateroom, carried on at embarkation. Beer, spirits and liqueurs are not allowed, and opening your own bottle in a restaurant or bar costs about US$25 in corkage at recent policy.

## How much wine can you bring on Celebrity?
The allowance is two standard bottles per stateroom, not per adult, and only on embarkation day. Pack them in your carry-on; bottles in checked bags are more likely to be pulled and held. Celebrity's wording has varied over time, so check your booking documents.

The same two bottles cover a couple or four adults sharing a Concierge Class stateroom. You also need to be of drinking age on board: 21 on North American sailings, including Alaska from Vancouver, and often 18 on sailings that start in Europe.

> **Watch out:** a 19-year-old from British Columbia, or an 18-year-old from Alberta, Manitoba or Quebec, can drink legally at home but not on a Celebrity Alaska sailing from Vancouver, where the onboard age is 21.

## What Celebrity does not allow
- Beer, spirits and liqueurs, in any quantity, in carry-on or checked bags.
- Wine or spirits bought ashore on port days, which are tagged at the gangway and returned near the end of the cruise.
- Duty-free bottles from the ship's shops, which are held and delivered at the end of the voyage.
- Alcohol decanted into water or mouthwash bottles. Every bag is scanned, and the consequences can go beyond confiscation.

## Corkage: where your own bottle costs extra
Opening your own wine in the main dining room, a specialty restaurant such as Fine Cut Steakhouse, or a bar adds a corkage fee of about US$25 per bottle at recent policy. In your stateroom there is no charge, and your attendant can usually bring glasses and an ice bucket if you ask.

### Is bringing your own worth it?
Wine-list prices sit well above shop prices, with Celebrity's 20% service charge on top, so a bottle you bought ashore is often cheaper even after corkage. The bigger saving is drinking it on your balcony, with no fee at all.

## All Included fares change the maths
Celebrity's All Included fare bundles the Classic drinks package, covering wine by the glass up to about US$12 a drink at recent terms, with 15% off bottles. Retreat suites get the Premium package, capped at about US$19. On either, save your two bottles for something special. On a cruise-only fare the allowance is real value; the [Celebrity drink package price](/articles/celebrity-drink-package-price/) page shows where a package starts to pay.

## Bringing bottles home to Canada
Wine the ship held comes back on the last night and counts towards your CBSA personal exemption. After at least 48 hours away, each adult of legal age in their province can bring back one of these duty-free: 1.5 litres of wine (two standard bottles), 1.14 litres of spirits, or 8.5 litres of beer. More in [cruise duty-free limits for Canadians](/articles/cruise-shopping-onboard-duty-free/).

> **Good to know:** boarding at Canada Place? Picking up BC wine in Vancouver the afternoon before is the easiest way to fill your allowance. See [where to stay in Vancouver the night before](/articles/where-to-stay-vancouver-night-before-cruise/).

## How Celebrity compares
| Line | Wine you can bring | Corkage (recent) |
|---|---|---|
| **Celebrity** | 2 bottles per stateroom | ~$25 in bars and restaurants |
| [Princess](/articles/princess-alcohol-policy/) | 1 free per adult; more with corkage | ~$20 for extra bottles |
| [Royal Caribbean](/articles/royal-caribbean-alcohol-policy/) | 2 bottles per stateroom | ~$15 in bars and restaurants |

Princess and Royal Caribbean are the lines most often weighed against Celebrity. Royal Caribbean, Celebrity's sister line, uses the same per-stateroom rule with lower corkage, while Princess counts its free allowance per adult, which matters more for four adults sharing a cabin. ## Sorting this before you pack
Your fare type decides if two bottles of your own are a small extra or a real saving: on All Included they are a treat, on cruise-only they cut the bar bill. Settle that at booking, then pack accordingly.
