Disney recommends about US$16 per guest per night in standard staterooms and US$27.25 in concierge, at rates in place since early 2025. It applies to every guest, babies included, and pays the people who look after you all week: your dining team and your stateroom host.

## How much are Disney gratuities per day?
The rate is per person per night, so a family pays for each child as well as each adult. Disney raised it twice in a short period around the start of 2025, so check the current figure on Disney's site or with whoever holds your booking.

| Party and length | Approx. USD | Roughly in CAD |
|---|---|---|
| Couple, standard, 7 nights | $224 | C$300–315 |
| Couple, concierge, 7 nights | about $382 | C$515–535 |
| Family of four, standard, 4 nights | $256 | C$345–360 |
| Family of four, standard, 7 nights | $448 | C$605–630 |

> **Example:** a family of four from Winnipeg on a 7-night Alaska sailing from Vancouver on the Disney Wonder would budget about US$448, or roughly C$605–630, before any bar or spa tips.

## Who your Disney gratuity goes to
Disney's rotational dining is the reason its system feels personal. You move between three themed restaurants, but your dining room server, assistant server and head server move with you. By the last night they know who wants chocolate milk and who is allergic to nuts.

The gratuity is shared among that dining team and your stateroom host. In concierge, it also covers the assistant stateroom host and the concierge lounge team, which explains the higher rate.

Near the end of the cruise you receive envelopes and receipt slips. These let you hand thanks to each crew member in person. The money itself has already been charged or prepaid.

## Can you prepay Disney gratuities?
Yes, and for most Canadians it makes sense. If your booking is priced in Canadian dollars, prepaid gratuities are added in CAD too, which fixes the exchange. If you pay on board, the charge posts in US dollars and converts at your card's rate, plus any foreign transaction fee. The pros and cons are in [paying in CAD vs USD for a cruise](/articles/paying-cad-vs-usd-cruise/).

Prepaying also locks today's rate. Disney has raised the figure several times, and a prepaid amount is generally not topped up later.

### How to prepay, step by step
1. Count every guest, including infants, and multiply by the nights and the current rate.
2. Add prepaid gratuities to your reservation online or through whoever booked you, before the cut-off shortly before sailing.
3. Check the amount on your updated invoice.
4. Once aboard, look in the Navigator app or ask Guest Services to confirm your account shows them as prepaid.

> **Canadian tip:** if you do pay on board, settle your account with a card that has no foreign transaction fee. On a family's 7-night bill, a typical 2.5% fee adds around C$15 for nothing.

## What the daily gratuity doesn't cover
An automatic 18% gratuity is added to bar and beverage purchases, wine, deck service and spa treatments. Adult dining at Palo, Remy or Enchanté has its own cover charge, so check your receipt to see whether a tip is already included before adding one. Terminal porters at Port Canaveral, Fort Lauderdale or Canada Place, and excursion guides, are tipped in cash by convention.

## Adjusting the amount
Guest Services can change the recommended amount. Reducing it for a specific, unresolved service problem is fair. Most families leave it as set and add cash for a server or stateroom host who went the extra mile.
