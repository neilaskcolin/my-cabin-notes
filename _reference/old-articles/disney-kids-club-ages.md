Disney's kids clubs start at age 3, with the Oceaneer Club for 3 to 12, Edge for 11 to 14 and Vibe for 14 to 17. All three are included in the fare. Under-3s have a paid nursery that you book ahead.

## How Disney's age groups split
| Club | Ages | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| It's a Small World Nursery | 6 months to 3 years | Paid by the hour | Reservation needed; limited spaces |
| Oceaneer Club | 3 to 12 | Included | Must be fully toilet-trained |
| Oceaneer Lab | 3 to 12 | Included | On Disney Magic, Wonder, Dream and Fantasy |
| Edge | 11 to 14 | Included | Tween space; 11- and 12-year-olds can choose |
| Vibe | 14 to 17 | Included | Teens only; 14-year-olds can choose |

The overlaps let an 11-year-old stay in the Oceaneer Club or a 14-year-old move up to Vibe.

### Differences between the ships
On the older Magic, Wonder, Dream and Fantasy, the Oceaneer Club and Oceaneer Lab are two connected spaces children move between. On the Wish-class ships (Disney Wish, Treasure and Destiny), it is one larger Oceaneer Club with themed rooms, such as a Marvel Super Hero Academy and Star Wars play spaces.

## Babies and toddlers
The It's a Small World Nursery takes babies from 6 months to 3 years, at recent prices around US$9 an hour for the first child and about US$8 for each sibling. Booking opens before sailing, earlier for concierge guests and higher Castaway Club tiers, and popular evening slots go first.

> **Watch out:** babies must be at least 6 months old to sail with Disney, and 12 months on some longer itineraries. Check the rule for your sailing before you book a cruise for a young baby. The wider picture is in [cruising with a baby or toddler](/articles/cruising-with-baby-toddler/).

## Opening hours and late-night care
The Oceaneer Club is typically open from morning until around midnight on sea days, and from later in the morning on port days, at no extra charge. That is what buys parents a dinner at Palo or a show on their own. Edge and Vibe run later starts and late finishes, sometimes past midnight for Vibe. The Navigator app lists the times each day.

Children wear a waterproof wristband so counsellors can see where they are in the club. Only adults you have named, using the security details set at registration, can sign younger children out.

## Signing up and settling in
- Register each child for the clubs during online check-in before you sail.
- Go to the open house on embarkation afternoon, when parents can tour the spaces with their children.
- Set up the Navigator app chat so counsellors can reach you, as [using the cruise line app](/articles/using-cruise-line-app-before-during-trip/) explains.

> **Good to know:** clubs are busiest on March break and summer sailings. A late-August Alaska sailing from Vancouver or a fall Bahamas cruise usually means more space and calmer sessions.

## Is Disney right for your children's ages?
For children of about 3 to 12, the clubs are the best reason to choose Disney: characters drop in and the spaces are themed around films they know. Teens may prefer the thrill rides on bigger ships. The fit by age is covered in [Is Disney Cruise Line Right for You](/articles/disney-cruise-line-honest-guide-canadians/) and, for northern itineraries, [an Alaska cruise with kids](/articles/alaska-cruise-with-kids-ages/).

## How Disney compares
| Line | Main club | Youngest age | Care for under-3s |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Disney** | Oceaneer Club and Lab | 3 | Paid nursery on every ship |
| [Royal Caribbean](/articles/royal-caribbean-kids-club-ages/) | Adventure Ocean | 3 | Paid nursery on many ships |
| [Carnival](/articles/carnival-kids-club-ages/) | Camp Ocean | 2 | None, but club starts at 2 |

Disney and Royal Caribbean both start their main clubs at 3, while Carnival starts at 2. Disney's edge is a nursery on every ship and the character content, though you pay for it in the fare. ## Matching the ship to your children's ages
The newer Wish-class ships have the biggest themed spaces, while the Magic and Wonder bring the same program to Alaska from Vancouver. Pick the ship and dates first, then book nursery hours the day the window opens.
