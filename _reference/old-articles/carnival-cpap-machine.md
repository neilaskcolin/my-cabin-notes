Yes, you can bring a CPAP on Carnival, and the line sees them on every sailing. The practical jobs are telling the Special Needs team, packing a plain extension cord without a surge protector, and sorting distilled water before the first night.

## How to set up a CPAP for a Carnival cruise
1. **Notify Carnival after booking.** Contact the Special Needs team through Carnival's accessibility page or ask whoever booked you to add it. Carnival can ask for medical equipment to be safety checked, and a note on the reservation avoids questions at the terminal.
2. **Order distilled water if you want the ship to supply it.** It is sold for a charge, either pre-ordered before sailing or bought on board.
3. **Pack a plain extension cord or power strip.** Carnival allows them as long as there is no surge protector.
4. **Charge any battery pack.** Carnival advises CPAP users to have a backup that doesn't depend on the cabin socket.
5. **Carry everything on.** Crew can't handle medical equipment during embarkation, so the machine, mask, tubing, power supply and first night's water stay with you.

## Extension cords: Carnival is more relaxed than people expect
Many first-timers assume every cruise line confiscates extension cords. On Carnival, plain cords and power strips are on the allowed list; it is surge-protected strips that are taken away and held until the end of the cruise. Damaged or frayed cords are removed too, so pack a 2–3 metre cord in good condition.

> **Good to know:** Carnival cabins have North American 110-volt sockets as well as 220-volt European ones, so a Canadian CPAP plug fits with no adapter. The question is distance, not plug type.

### Older ships vs the Excel class
Socket numbers and positions vary across the fleet. Newer ships such as Mardi Gras, Carnival Celebration and Carnival Jubilee tend to have more outlets and USB ports, while older Fantasy- and Spirit-class ships may have fewer, often clustered at the vanity. Ships also gain outlets in dry dock, so bring the cord whatever ship you are on.

## Getting distilled water on board
You have three options: pre-order through Carnival, buy it once aboard, or bring your own. If you drive to a home port such as Baltimore, New York or Seattle, a sealed jug or two in the car is the cheapest route. Tell the terminal staff it is for a medical device, since Carnival's normal drink allowance is limited to cans and cartons. If you fly from Calgary or Toronto to Florida, a litre in your hand luggage covers night one; [CPAP distilled water on cruise ships](/articles/cpap-distilled-water-cruise-ships/) covers alternatives.

> **Canadian tip:** your provincial health plan won't replace a lost or broken machine abroad, and it only partly covers medical care at sea. Check that your travel insurance covers medical equipment and declare sleep apnea if the policy asks, as covered in [cruise insurance for pre-existing conditions](/articles/cruise-insurance-seniors-canada-pre-existing/).

## Why the CPAP never goes in a checked bag
Checked bags on Carnival can reach your cabin late in the evening, and occasionally not at all. Keep the CPAP in your hand luggage. [Lost or delayed luggage on a cruise](/articles/lost-delayed-luggage-cruise/) explains what happens when bags go astray.

## How Carnival compares
| Line | Desk to contact | Distilled water |
|---|---|---|
| **Carnival** | Special Needs team | Paid; pre-order, buy on board or bring your own |
| [Royal Caribbean](/articles/royal-caribbean-cpap-machine/) | Access Department | On request, often via stateroom attendant |
| [MSC](/articles/msc-cpap-machine/) | Special-needs desk for your market | Varies by ship and market |

All three lines welcome CPAP machines. On Carnival, the main differences are that you can bring your own non-surge-protected cord and that distilled water is a paid item you can pre-order. Royal Caribbean routes requests through its Access Department, and MSC through the special-needs desk for your booking market. Rules for all eight lines are in [can you bring a CPAP machine on a cruise ship](/articles/bringing-cpap-machine-cruise-ship/).
