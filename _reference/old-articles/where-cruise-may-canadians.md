May is when cruise prices are low and the scenery is at its most dramatic. Alaska opens for the season, Europe is in bloom before the summer crowds, and ships repositioning to their summer homes offer some of the cheapest days at sea of the year.

## Alaska in May: the season opener
Alaska's season starts in late April or early May. Early sailings carry the year's lowest fares, often 25–40% below July for the same cabin. The peaks are still heavy with snow, waterfalls roar with spring melt, and ports feel uncrowded.

| | May | July |
|---|---|---|
| Fares | Often 25–40% below peak | Highest of the season |
| Daytime temperatures | Cool, often around 10–15°C | Mild, often around 15–20°C |
| Rain | Often one of the drier months | Moderate, rising later in summer |
| Crowds in port | Light | Heavy, with several ships in port at once |
| Wildlife | Whales arriving, bears foraging on shorelines | Whales settled; salmon runs and bear viewing peak later in summer |
| School holiday demand | Little | Very high |

Temperatures and rain vary by port and year, so treat these as rough guides. The [honest pros and cons of shoulder season](/articles/alaska-shoulder-season-pros-cons/) explains the trade-offs in more detail, and [packing for May vs September](/articles/alaska-packing-may-vs-september/) covers the layers you will need.

> **Good to know:** on narrow fjord routes, ice early in the season sometimes stops ships short of the glacier face, and captains may swap to an alternative route. It is a reason to keep expectations flexible, not to avoid May.

### Who May suits best
Couples, retirees and anyone flexible about dates get the most out of May. Families are usually tied to school, but a long-weekend sailing can still work. Victoria Day, on the Monday before May 25, is also Journée nationale des patriotes in Quebec, so sailings that weekend are the busiest of the month with Canadian families and working couples. See [cruising around Canadian long weekends](/articles/cruise-canadian-long-weekends-holidays/) for more ideas.

## Repositioning cruises: the hidden May bargain
In spring, ships move from their winter regions to their summer ones. On the west coast that means one-way Pacific coastal sailings from Los Angeles, San Diego or San Francisco north to Vancouver or Seattle. In the Atlantic, ships cross from Florida to Europe.

These sailings often have a low price per night, with plenty of sea days. The catch is one-way flights and time. [Repositioning cruises explained](/articles/repositioning-cruises-explained/) covers how to price them properly.

## Europe in May
Rhine and Danube river cruises are at their best: mild days, flowering riverbanks and towns before the coach-tour crowds. The tulip season in the Netherlands usually runs into early May. The Mediterranean is warm enough for sightseeing and much calmer than in summer. [Danube and Rhine cruises for Canadians](/articles/danube-rhine-river-cruises-canadians/) compares the main routes.

Flights from Toronto and Montreal to Europe are generally cheaper in May than in July and August, which helps the total.

## The Caribbean in May
May is the tail end of the Caribbean dry season. It is hot, quiet and usually good value, and hurricane season does not start until June 1. It lacks the seasonal drama of Alaska or Europe, but it is a sensible warm-weather pick.

## Booking a May Alaska cruise
1. Pick a sailing in mid or late May if you want slightly warmer weather and fully open ports; early May for the lowest fares.
2. Compare round trips from Vancouver with Seattle departures on price and flights.
3. Book during wave season, on a fare that allows repricing.
4. Watch for spring promotions on unsold May dates, explained in [when Alaska cruise prices drop](/articles/when-do-alaska-cruise-prices-drop/).
5. Book flights early if you are coming from Toronto, Montreal or the Prairies.

## Alaska or Europe this May?
Choose Alaska for scenery, value and an easy trip from Western Canada. Choose Europe for culture and spring blossoms if the transatlantic flight fits your budget. If longer evenings and warmer days matter more than the price, compare the [June guide](/articles/where-cruise-june-canadians/) before deciding.
