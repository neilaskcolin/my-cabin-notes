Formal night on a modern cruise is closer to a nice restaurant dinner than a black-tie gala. Pack one step-up outfit per person and you are covered. You will see everything from sundresses to sequins and the odd tuxedo, and all of it is fine.

## What each line calls it and expects
Every line has its own name and its own level of dressiness. These are the typical patterns at recent policy; your daily programme confirms the exact nights.

Luxury lines tend to dress up a notch every evening, while expedition ships barely notice clothes.

## What about the other nights?
Most evenings are smart casual or "cruise casual". The rules are simple: no swimwear, tank tops, ball caps or bare feet in the main dining room. Nice jeans pass on most mainstream ships. For daytime rules, see [what to wear on board during the day](/articles/cruise-ship-dress-code-daytime-wear/).

## The packing formula
### For a 7-night cruise, per person
- One step-up outfit that covers both formal nights. Nobody notices repeats.
- Two smart-casual evening outfits that mix and match.
- One pair of dressy shoes, and comfortable shoes for everything else.
- A wrap, cardigan or light jacket, because theatres and dining rooms run cold.

### Adjusting for where you sail
On Alaska sailings from Vancouver, formal nights are even more relaxed, and a warm layer matters more than a tie. The [Alaska cruise dress code](/articles/alaska-cruise-dress-code/) covers what people actually wear. Caribbean evenings are warm, so lighter fabrics and a dress or linen jacket travel well.

> **Canadian tip:** if you are flying from Winnipeg, Toronto or Calgary on a basic fare with checked-bag fees, wear the blazer on the plane and pack one versatile dark outfit. It saves space and possibly a second bag fee each way.

## If you would rather skip it
Formal night only governs the main dining room. The buffet, pizza counter, casual venues and room service all run normally. Some couples book a specialty restaurant that night to dodge the crowd, while others lean in for the photos. Ship photographers set up backdrops on formal nights, and you only pay if you buy, as explained in [cruise photo packages](/articles/cruise-photography-packages-worth-it/).

## Know before you pack
Ask at booking which nights are dressier on your ship. It saves packing a suit you will not wear, and it lets you plan specialty dinners around the gala evenings.
