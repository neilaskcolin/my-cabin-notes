Sail-away is the moment the trip officially starts: the lines are cast off, music plays on the pool deck, and the ship slides away from the pier. It costs nothing, and even people who aren't party types usually enjoy it.

## What actually happens?
Once the muster drill is finished (see [what happens at the muster drill](/articles/cruise-ship-safety-drill-what-happens/)), the crew turn up the music on the main pool deck. There's often a DJ or live band, a cocktail of the day, and sometimes dancing led by the entertainment team. The ship sounds its horn as it pulls away, and people wave to anyone on shore.

It lasts about as long as it takes to clear the harbour, often 30–60 minutes. Then the deck quietens and people head off to unpack or eat.

## Where to stand by departure port
The party is at the pool, but the best views are often elsewhere. Open decks at the very front or back of the ship give the widest outlook.

| Port | What you'll see | Where to stand |
|---|---|---|
| Vancouver, Canada Place | Stanley Park, then under the Lions Gate Bridge | Forward, port (left) side for the park |
| Seattle | The downtown skyline and Space Needle shrinking behind you | Aft open decks |
| Fort Lauderdale | A narrow channel close to the beach, with people waving from shore | Any rail near the channel side; aft is good |
| Miami | Government Cut, South Pointe Park and South Beach | Rail on the beach side; aft for skyline |
| Quebec City | The old city and Château Frontenac above the St. Lawrence | Aft, looking back at the city |

Leaving Vancouver is one of the most scenic departures anywhere. The ship hugs the shore of Stanley Park, and the bridge looks startlingly close as you slide underneath. The [Canada Place embarkation walkthrough](/articles/canada-place-embarkation-walkthrough/) explains timing on the day.

## How to get a good spot
1. Check the departure time in the daily program or app.
2. Finish the muster drill check-in early so you're free.
3. Grab a drink or a snack from the buffet on your way up.
4. Get to the rail 15–20 minutes before departure.
5. Keep your phone ready for the bridge or skyline, then put it away and watch.

> **Canadian tip:** turn off data roaming or switch to airplane mode before the ship leaves port. Once you're out of range of Canadian towers, your phone may connect to the ship's maritime network at steep rates. [Cruise wifi and roaming for Canadians](/articles/cruise-wifi-phone-roaming-canadians/) explains how to avoid a surprise bill.

## Dress for the deck
A Vancouver departure in May or September can be cool and breezy once you're moving, so bring a jacket. Florida sail-aways are warm but sunny, so a hat helps.

## Not a party person?
Watch from your balcony, or from a quiet deck at the back of the ship, away from the speakers. You still get the views without the dance music. Some people prefer sail-away from later ports too, like leaving Juneau along the Gastineau Channel, when the decks are quieter.

If you're eyeing the bar, a drink package starts working from the first day on most lines; [are drink packages worth it](/articles/drink-packages-worth-it-first-cruise/) helps you decide.
