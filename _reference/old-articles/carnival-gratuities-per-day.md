Carnival's daily gratuity is US$17 per person in interior, ocean view and balcony cabins and US$19 in suites, for sailings from April 2, 2026. It posts to your Sail & Sign account every day unless you prepay, and a separate 20% charge applies to drinks and some extras.

## How much are Carnival gratuities per day?
The charge applies to every guest in the cabin, children included, for each night of the cruise. Carnival raised it by US$1 in April 2026, the first increase in about three years, so older blog posts quoting US$16 are out of date.

Because so many Carnival sailings are short, it helps to see the totals by trip length.

| Carnival trip | Approx. USD | Roughly in CAD |
|---|---|---|
| 3-night Bahamas, couple | $102 | C$138–143 |
| 4-night Cozumel, couple | $136 | C$184–190 |
| 5-night, family of four | $340 | C$459–476 |

| 7-night, couple, suite | $266 | C$359–372 |

## Where the money goes
Carnival pools the daily gratuity across dining room teams, stateroom attendants and behind-the-scenes crew such as galley and housekeeping staff. For many of them it is a core part of pay rather than a bonus. The broader system is explained in [cruise gratuities explained for Canadians](/articles/cruise-gratuities-canadians/).

## Should you prepay Carnival gratuities?
Usually, yes. Prepaid gratuities are charged with your booking, so the exchange on that part of the trip is settled when you pay. Anything left on your Sail & Sign account posts in US dollars and converts at your card's rate on the last morning. [Paying in CAD vs USD for a cruise](/articles/paying-cad-vs-usd-cruise/) covers the trade-offs.

> **Good to know:** prepaying locks the rate in force on the day you pay. When Carnival raised gratuities in April 2026, guests who had already prepaid kept the old US$16 and US$18 rates.

### What if you don't prepay
The charge simply builds up on your Sail & Sign account each day. Check the running total in the HUB app midway through the cruise, so the final bill holds no surprises.

> **Canadian tip:** settle the onboard account with a card that charges no foreign-transaction fee. On a family's US$476 week of gratuities alone, a typical 2.5% fee adds roughly C$16–17.

## What the daily gratuity doesn't cover
Carnival adds a separate automatic charge, 20% since December 2025, to:

- bar drinks and every drink package, including CHEERS! and Bottomless Bubbles
- specialty dining such as Fahrenheit 555 and Bonsai Sushi
- in-stateroom gifts and celebration packages

Spa treatments carry their own service charge, and many room service items cost extra, as covered in [room service on cruises](/articles/room-service-cruise-free-cost/). Terminal porters and shore excursion guides are tipped in cash, so bring a few small US bills. The [Carnival drink package price](/articles/carnival-drink-package-price/) page shows how the 20% changes the package maths.

## Adjusting gratuities on board
Guest Services can lower the daily charge during the cruise if service genuinely falls short, but nothing can be changed after the sailing ends. Adjusting for a specific failure is fair; removing it wholesale shortchanges crew who did their jobs. If someone stands out, a cash tip or a named comment on the post-cruise survey goes a long way.

Gratuities also count towards Carnival Rewards, the loyalty program that replaced VIFP in 2026, which earns points on eligible onboard and pre-cruise spending.
