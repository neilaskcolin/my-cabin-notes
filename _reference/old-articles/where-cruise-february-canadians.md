February is the sweet spot of the Canadian winter cruise season. The weather down south is as good as it gets, and you are sailing between the two most expensive windows of the winter. If your dates are flexible, this is the month to escape.

## Why February beats the weeks either side
The Caribbean and Mexican Riviera are deep in their dry season: warm, sunny, low rain, no hurricane season to worry about. What changes from December to March is demand, and that is where February wins.

| Winter window | Demand | Typical pricing | Who it suits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Christmas and New Year | Very high, families | Highest of the winter | Families tied to the school break |
| Mid-January to February | Moderate | Usually the best winter value | Couples, retirees, flexible families |
| March break weeks | Very high, families | Near holiday levels | Families with school-age kids |

Wave season promotions also run through February, so you can sail this month and book next year's trip while the extras are rich. [Wave season explained](/articles/wave-season-explained-canada/) covers what those promotions actually include.

## The February long weekend
Most provinces have a holiday on the third Monday of February: Family Day in Alberta, British Columbia, Ontario, Saskatchewan and New Brunswick, Louis Riel Day in Manitoba, Islander Day in PEI and Heritage Day in Nova Scotia. Quebec has no February holiday, but many Quebec schools take their spring break, the semaine de relâche, in late February or early March.

> **Canadian tip:** a 7-night cruise that departs on the Saturday of the long weekend uses only four vacation days. That makes mid-February sailings popular with working couples, so book those specific dates a little earlier than the rest of the month.

## Where to cruise in February
### Western and eastern Caribbean
The classic choice, with sailings from Fort Lauderdale, Miami, Port Canaveral and Tampa. Direct flights from Toronto and Montreal make the eastern Caribbean easy; Calgary, Edmonton and Winnipeg travellers usually connect. [Eastern vs western vs southern Caribbean](/articles/eastern-vs-western-caribbean-first-cruise/) compares the routes.

### Mexican Riviera
Round trips from Los Angeles or San Diego suit Western Canadians, and February is prime humpback season off Cabo San Lucas and Puerto Vallarta. The [Mexican Riviera cost guide](/articles/mexican-riviera-cruise-cost-canada/) breaks down the budget from Canada.

### Southern Caribbean and Panama Canal
Longer itineraries for repeat cruisers. Ports such as Aruba and Curaçao sit outside the usual storm belt, and the canal's dry season makes February comfortable for a transit. See [why couples love Panama Canal cruises](/articles/panama-canal-cruise-couples/).

### Hawaii
Whale season peaks in winter. It is a bigger trip in time and money, set out in the [Hawaii cost guide](/articles/hawaii-cruise-cost-canada/).

## Still closed in February
- **Alaska:** the season does not start until late April or May.
- **Norway and the Baltic:** mostly closed until spring, apart from winter northern lights sailings along Norway's coast.
- **Mediterranean:** a few ships sail year round, but weather is cool and changeable.

## Mistakes to avoid
- Flying in on the morning of departure. A February storm in Toronto, Montreal or Winnipeg can wipe out a whole day of flights.
- Forgetting that provincial health plans pay very little outside Canada. Travel medical insurance is essential.
- Assuming cheap means last minute. Long-weekend and Valentine's sailings can fill early.

## Making the most of February
For flexible travellers, February offers the warmest weather of the winter at the fairest prices. Pick your region by how far you want to fly, add a buffer day for winter weather, and use the month's wave season promotions to line up your next trip too. If your only option is March break, the [March guide](/articles/where-cruise-march-canadians/) explains how to get the most from that busy week.
