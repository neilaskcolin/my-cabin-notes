Carnival's wifi costs about US$20–26 per device per day at recent pre-cruise prices, depending on which of its three plans you choose. Buying before you sail saves around 15%, and since Carnival moved its fleet to Starlink, the service is good enough for video calls on the Premium plan.

## Carnival's three wifi plans
Each plan covers one device at a time, and the difference between them is what you can open and how fast it loads.

| Plan | What works | Pre-cruise, per day | On board, approx. |
|---|---|---|---|
| Social | Messaging and social apps, airline sites | US$20.40 | US$24 |
| Value | Adds email and general browsing | US$23.80 | US$28 |
| Premium | Up to 3× faster; streaming and video calls | US$25.50 | US$30 |
| Premium Multi-Device | Premium on up to 4 devices | about US$90 | Online only |

Prices are recent figures and change. Carnival has said wifi and drink package prices will vary by sailing length, itinerary and season from December 1, 2026, so expect holiday sailings to cost more.

## Which Carnival plan do you actually need?
Social is enough for WhatsApp, iMessage and Facebook, but it won't open email or a news site. Most couples who want to check in with family and read the news do fine on Value. Choose Premium only if you need FaceTime, Zoom or streaming, or if you'll be working; [can you work remotely on a cruise ship](/articles/can-you-work-remotely-cruise-ship/) covers that.

> **Quick math:** a couple both buying Value before a 7-night cruise pays about US$333, roughly C$450–466. A family of four on Premium pays about US$714 for four single plans, or about US$630 for Multi-Device, roughly C$850–880.

## How fast is Carnival wifi now?
Carnival finished moving its fleet to Starlink satellite internet in 2024, and the improvement is real: messaging and browsing are reliable on most ships, and Premium handles video calls most of the time. Speeds still dip when thousands of guests are online at once, such as on a sea-day afternoon. The basics are in [how cruise ship internet works](/articles/cruise-ship-internet-how-it-works/).

### At Carnival's private destinations
Celebration Key on Grand Bahama supports Carnival wifi plans; Half Moon Cay, at last report, doesn't.

## What you can do without buying wifi
The Carnival HUB app runs on the ship's network for free. Without a plan you can see the daily schedule, check menus, book dining and shows, and watch your Sail & Sign balance. HUB chat, for messaging the people you're travelling with, costs a one-time US$5 per person for the cruise. [Using the cruise line app](/articles/using-cruise-line-app-before-during-trip/) explains the set-up.

## Setting up Carnival wifi without wasting money
1. Download the HUB app at home and log in before you leave.
2. Decide who really needs a plan; kids can often share a parent's device or use HUB chat.
3. Buy pre-cruise through My Cruise Manager for the 15% saving, or 25% on Multi-Device.
4. Put phones in airplane mode before sail-away, then turn wifi back on.
5. Log out of one device before logging into another, since a plan covers one at a time.

> **Canadian tip:** your Canadian carrier's US or international roaming pass usually doesn't cover the ship's own cellular network at sea, where charges can run to several dollars a minute or megabyte. Keep data roaming off on board and use Carnival wifi instead; [cruise wifi and phone roaming for Canadians](/articles/cruise-wifi-phone-roaming-canadians/) covers each carrier.

## How Carnival compares
| Line | System | Recent price per device per day | Included with |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Carnival** | Hub plans | $20–30 USD | Occasional sales and bundles |
| [Royal Caribbean](/articles/royal-caribbean-wifi-packages/) | VOOM | $20–30 USD | Some promotions; some suites |
| [MSC](/articles/msc-wifi-packages/) | Browse / Browse & Stream | About $16–20 USD prepaid; $31–33 on board | Yacht Club; some promotions |

After recent increases, Carnival's wifi now costs about the same as Royal Caribbean's per device, while MSC is cheaper only if you buy a full-cruise plan before sailing; its day passes on board cost more. Royal Caribbean includes wifi in some suites and promotions, whereas Carnival mostly bundles it in sales and packages. ## Getting wifi bundled instead of bought
For a connected couple, wifi can add more than C$400 to a week on Carnival. Ask whether a promotion or bundle for your dates includes it before buying a plan at the full daily rate.
