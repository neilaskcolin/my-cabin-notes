Holland America has been moving its final payment date earlier. Many newer bookings, and all longer voyages, are now due 120 days before sailing, while many earlier bookings of 13 nights or less still use 90 days. Which schedule applies depends on when you booked, so the date on your confirmation is the one that counts.

## Holland America final payment dates
Holland America ties the schedule to your booking date, cruise length and region. At recent policy:

- Bookings opened from late September 2026 for 2027 departures onward (Grand Voyages aside) move to a 120-day final payment.
- Alaska cruises and cruisetours departing from April 2028, booked from late August 2026, also move to 120 days.
- Cruises of 14 nights or more, Grand Voyage segments, and Asia, Australia and South America sailings already use 120 days.
- Many bookings made earlier for cruises of 13 nights or less, including Alaska cruisetours, stay on 90 days.

The general mechanics are covered in [cruise deposits and final payment dates explained](/articles/cruise-deposits-final-payment/).

## The Holland America cancellation schedule
Each schedule has a full-refund window, then a short period where you lose the deposit or a quarter of the fare, then larger penalties.

| Schedule (recent policy) | Full refund if cancelled | Partial refund | Nothing back |
|---|---|---|---|
| Newer 120-day bookings | 120+ days out | 75% at 119–91 days; 50% at 90–61 | 60 days or less |
| Earlier bookings, 13 nights or less, cruisetours | 90+ days out | Deposit lost at 89–83 days; 50% at 82–46 | 45 days or less |
| 14+ nights, Grand segments, Asia, Australia, South America | 120+ days out | Deposit lost at 119–113 days; 50% at 112–76 | 75 days or less |

Specially priced promotions can carry a non-refundable deposit, and some fares are non-refundable from the moment of payment. [Cruise fare classes explained](/articles/cruise-fare-classes-refundable/) covers how to spot them.

> **Example:** an Edmonton couple pays C$5,000 for a 7-night Vancouver to Alaska sailing and cancels 100 days out. On the newer 120-day schedule they get 75% back, about C$3,750. On the older 90-day schedule the same cancellation would be a full refund.

## What else follows the fare
Have It All is priced into the fare, so it cancels on the same schedule. Flights, transfers and hotels booked through Holland America follow the cruise schedule too, while anything booked with a third party follows that company's terms.

Name changes and date changes are treated as cancellations and may carry fees. Dropping one of two guests after the full-refund window can also leave the other paying a single rate, so get a revised quote first.

## How to cancel a Holland America cruise
1. Find which schedule your confirmation shows and work out which band today falls in.
2. Call your insurer before cancelling if you may claim. Many policies expect prompt notice.
3. Cancel through whoever holds the booking. Holland America says bookings made through an advisor are cancelled through that advisor, except within seven days of travel.
4. Ask for the refund amount and the treatment of air, Have It All and excursions in writing.
5. Watch for the refund on the original card, in the currency you paid.

> **Canadian tip:** if you paid in US dollars, a refund arrives in US dollars and converts back at the day's rate, which can leave you short. Booking in Canadian dollars avoids that, as [paying in CAD vs USD for a cruise](/articles/paying-cad-vs-usd-cruise/) explains.

## Insurance for long Holland America voyages
Holland America's long itineraries raise the stakes: bigger balances, earlier deadlines and longer trips. Many Canadian policies give full cancellation cover only if bought soon after the deposit, and some cap trip length or age. Check the maximum trip duration before booking a Grand Voyage, as covered in [longer cruises for retirees](/articles/longer-cruises-retirees-14-nights/) and [cruise insurance in Canada](/articles/travel-insurance-cruise-canadians/).

The line's own Cancellation Protection Plan works differently from a Canadian policy. [Travel insurance vs cruise line protection plans](/articles/travel-insurance-vs-cruise-protection/) sets out the trade-off.
