Yes, you can bring a CPAP on any Princess ship, and the crew sees plenty of them. The two things to sort before you sail are an approved extension cord and distilled water, both arranged through Princess's Access Office.

## How to set up a CPAP for a Princess cruise
1. Contact the Access Office once you have booked. Give the type of machine and ask for a medical extension cord and distilled water to be noted on your reservation.
2. If online check-in in the Princess app or on the website asks about special needs or medical equipment, mention the CPAP there too.
3. Pack the machine, mask and tubing in your carry-on, with enough distilled water for night one.
4. When you reach your cabin, check whether the cord has been delivered. If not, ask Guest Services that afternoon rather than at bedtime.
5. Set the machine up before dinner and run it briefly, so any problem with reach or power shows up early.

## Flying in from elsewhere in Canada
Air Canada and WestJet generally let a CPAP travel as a medical device that does not count towards your carry-on allowance, but confirm with your airline. If you are flying from Winnipeg or Toronto to sail from Vancouver, it is simpler to buy a jug of distilled water at a Vancouver pharmacy or grocery store than to fly with it. More detail is in [luggage rules when flying to your cruise](/articles/luggage-rules-flying-to-cruise/).

## The extension cord rule
Fire rules mean Princess holds guests' own extension cords and power bars at security until the last day. The medical cord it lends is designed for exactly this use. [Can you bring an extension cord on a cruise](/articles/extension-cord-cruise-ship-rules/) explains why the rule exists and where the exception sits.

## Distilled water and outlets
Princess can typically supply distilled water on request. Supply and any charge can vary by ship, so confirm when you contact the Access Office. [Distilled water by cruise line](/articles/cpap-distilled-water-cruise-ships/) compares the arrangements.

Socket position varies by ship. Newer Royal-class and Sphere-class ships such as Discovery Princess and Sun Princess have more outlets and USB ports in cabins; on older Grand-class ships the nearest socket may be at the desk, which is where the medical cord earns its keep.

> **Good to know:** cabin outlets on Princess ships are North American style, the same as at home, and so are the outlets at the Denali and Fairbanks lodges used on cruisetours. Leave the adapter at home.

## CPAP on a Princess cruisetour
On a cruisetour the lodge nights are on land, so the ship's arrangements stop at the gangway. The lodges are remote, so carry enough distilled water for those nights before you leave the ship or a city stop such as Anchorage or Fairbanks. The structure of these trips is in [Alaska cruisetours explained](/articles/alaska-cruisetours-denali-rail/).

## What to keep in your carry-on
- The machine, mask, tubing and power cord
- Spare filters and a spare mask cushion
- Distilled water for the first night
- A copy of your prescription or a short letter from your doctor
- Your travel insurance details, since provincial health plans do not cover care at sea

If a checked bag is delayed, as [lost or delayed luggage on a cruise](/articles/lost-delayed-luggage-cruise/) explains can happen, you still sleep with the machine. If you have sleep apnea, declare it accurately on your insurance application; [cruise insurance and pre-existing conditions](/articles/cruise-insurance-seniors-canada-pre-existing/) explains why that matters.

## How Princess compares
| Line | Desk to contact | Distilled water |
|---|---|---|
| **Princess** | Access Office | On request before or after boarding |
| [Holland America](/articles/holland-america-cpap-machine/) | Guest Accessibility | On request in advance |
| [Celebrity](/articles/celebrity-cpap-machine/) | Access Department | On request via attendant or Access |

All three lines welcome CPAP machines and lend an approved extension cord. Princess takes water requests before or after boarding, while Holland America prefers them in advance.
