Kids clubs are one of the best-value parts of a family cruise. On every major mainstream line, supervised programmes for children aged about 3 to 17 are included in the fare, run by trained youth staff, with activities from crafts and science to pool games and themed parties.

## Kids club ages by cruise line
Every line below includes its main kids club in the fare. The differences that matter most to parents are the youngest age accepted and what happens with babies and toddlers.

| Line | Main club | Youngest age | Care for under-3s | Full guide |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Royal Caribbean | Adventure Ocean | 3 | Paid nursery on many ships | [Royal Caribbean kids club](/articles/royal-caribbean-kids-club-ages/) |
| Carnival | Camp Ocean | 2 | None, but club starts at 2 | [Carnival kids club](/articles/carnival-kids-club-ages/) |
| Norwegian | Splash Academy | 3 | Parent must stay (Guppies) | [Norwegian kids club](/articles/norwegian-kids-club-ages/) |
| Princess | Camp Discovery | 3 | None | [Princess kids club](/articles/princess-kids-club-ages/) |
| Celebrity | Camp at Sea | 3 | None | [Celebrity kids club](/articles/celebrity-kids-club-ages/) |
| Holland America | Club HAL | 3 | None | [Holland America kids club](/articles/holland-america-kids-club-ages/) |
| MSC | Mini Club | 3 | Paid Baby Care from 6 months; Baby Time with parent | [MSC kids club](/articles/msc-kids-club-ages/) |
| Disney | Oceaneer Club and Lab | 3 | Paid nursery on every ship | [Disney kids club](/articles/disney-kids-club-ages/) |

Carnival is the only line here whose main club takes 2-year-olds. Royal Caribbean and Disney run paid nurseries for babies from 6 months on many or all ships, and MSC offers paid Baby Care drop-off from 6 months on ships that run it, which makes them strong choices for families with very young children; the [baby and toddler guide](/articles/cruising-with-baby-toddler/) covers that age group. Princess, Celebrity and Holland America suit families with children of 3 and up. For every line's rules side by side, see [cruise line policies compared](/articles/cruise-line-policies-compared/).

## How age bands work
Most lines split children into three younger bands, roughly 3–5, 6–8 and 9–11, each with its own space and schedule. Teens usually get a separate lounge, often split into about 12–14 and 15–17, with a lighter touch: drop-in rather than signed in and out.

On Glacier Bay sailings, National Park Service rangers who board for the day often run a Junior Ranger activity, which can be a highlight for school-age kids. Teens on Alaska trips have their own guide in [Alaska cruises with teenagers](/articles/alaska-cruise-teenagers/).

## Typical hours and what costs extra
Included hours vary by line and by sea or port day, but this is the usual pattern:

| Time | What usually happens | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Morning to lunch | Open sessions, crafts, games | Included |
| Lunch | Club closes or offers supervised meals on some ships | Included where offered |
| Afternoon | Activities, pool time, themed events | Included |
| Dinner to about 10pm | Evening session, often with kids' dinner on some ships | Included |
| About 10pm to late | Late-night group sessions on some lines | Paid, often around US$7–10 per child per hour |

> **Quick math:** two children in a paid late session from 10pm to 1am at about US$8 an hour each comes to around US$48, or roughly C$65–67, for a long dinner and a show. Confirm current rates on board, as they change.

## Registering on embarkation day
1. Pre-register children in the line's app or website if offered.
2. Visit the club during the open house on boarding afternoon, so kids can see the space.
3. Fill in medical, allergy and dietary details, and name who may collect each child.
4. Collect any wristband, tracking device or phone access the line uses.
5. Ask about the rules for port days, meals and swimming, and whether children can sign themselves out at older ages.

> **Canadian tip:** March break and July and August sailings are when Canadian families flood the clubs. Expect busier rooms and book any paid nursery or late sessions as soon as reservations open.

## Matching the club to your family
Programmes vary by line and even by ship within the same line. Before booking, check your exact ship's youngest age, opening hours, late-night options and port-day rules, especially if a full parents' evening out matters to you.
