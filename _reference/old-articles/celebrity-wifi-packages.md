On a cruise-only fare, getting online on Celebrity means buying Premium wifi, at about US$27–35 per device per day before you sail at recent prices, roughly C$37–49. Basic wifi only comes bundled in All Included fares, and Retreat suites get Premium included.

## Celebrity wifi plans and prices
Celebrity uses Starlink satellite internet across its ocean fleet, which made onboard speeds far better than they were a few years ago. Older guides call the service Xcelerate; today it is sold as two tiers.

| Plan | How you get it | Good for | Recent price |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | Included in All Included fares only | Messaging, email, browsing | Not sold separately |
| Premium | Bought per device, or included in Retreat fares | Video calls, streaming, photo uploads | about US$27–33 a day before sailing; often US$35+ on board |
| Basic to Premium upgrade | For All Included guests | More speed | about US$10 a day |

A second device is typically about 20% off. Prices vary by ship and sailing, with Edge-class ships at the top of the range. Captain's Club Zenith members get Premium free, and Elite levels get discounts.

## Is Basic enough?
For WhatsApp, email and browsing, usually yes. Basic is slowed for heavier use, so photo uploads and video calls can struggle. If you need to join meetings or send large files, plan on Premium; [working remotely from a cruise ship](/articles/can-you-work-remotely-cruise-ship/) covers what that is like.

## Buy before you sail or on board?
Pre-cruise prices in your Celebrity account are typically at least 10% lower than on board, and a plan for the whole voyage beats buying day by day.

### How to buy it
1. Check your fare. If it is All Included, you already have Basic for each guest.
2. Decide how many devices really need Premium. Often one upgraded phone or laptop is enough for a couple.
3. Watch your Celebrity account for sales in the months before sailing.
4. Buy before embarkation to lock the pre-cruise price.
5. Log in on day one and test it before the ship leaves port.

> **Quick math:** a couple on a 7-night cruise-only fare buying Premium for two devices at about US$30 and US$24 a day pays roughly US$378, or about C$510–530. On an All Included fare, upgrading one device from Basic for about US$10 a day costs around US$70 for the week.

## How fast is it really?
Starlink comfortably handles messaging, browsing and usually video calls on Celebrity's ocean ships, though short drop-outs still happen. Celebrity Flora in the Galapagos doesn't use Starlink and is noticeably slower. The technology is explained in [how cruise ship internet actually works](/articles/cruise-ship-internet-how-it-works/).

> **Canadian tip:** on Alaska sailings from Vancouver or Seattle, your phone is in the US when docked in Juneau, Ketchikan or Skagway. A Canadian carrier's US roaming day pass can be cheaper than a day of ship wifi on port days, but switch roaming off at sea, where the ship's cellular network bills at much higher rates. More in [cruise wifi and phone roaming for Canadians](/articles/cruise-wifi-phone-roaming-canadians/).

## How Celebrity compares
| Line | System | Recent price per device per day | Included with |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Celebrity** | Starlink (Basic and Premium) | $27–35 USD | All Included (Basic); Retreat (Premium) |
| [Princess](/articles/princess-wifi-packages/) | MedallionNet | $15–25 USD | Princess Plus and Premier |
| [Royal Caribbean](/articles/royal-caribbean-wifi-packages/) | VOOM | $20–30 USD | Some promotions; some suites |

Princess and Royal Caribbean are the lines most often weighed against Celebrity. Celebrity's standalone wifi is the priciest of the three, but most Celebrity guests get it through All Included, much as Princess guests do with Princess Plus. If you book cruise-only and need to stay connected, factor in Premium before comparing fares. ## Getting wifi bundled instead of bought
An All Included fare puts every guest online with Basic, which is enough for most messaging and browsing. Price both fares with wifi counted before you pay for Premium at list price.
