Norwegian Cruise Line is right for you if you want to eat when you like, dress how you like and have plenty to do on a big, lively ship. It is less right if you love the ritual of formal nights and a fixed table, or want one price that covers everything.

## What "freestyle cruising" means on Norwegian
Norwegian built its brand on dropping cruising's fixed structure. There are no assigned dining times or tables: you turn up at a main dining room at 6 pm or 9 pm, or book one of the specialty restaurants such as Cagney's Steakhouse or Le Bistro. There are no compulsory formal nights, and resort casual is fine in the dining rooms every evening, as the [Norwegian dress code](/articles/norwegian-dress-code-formal-nights/) page explains.

The trade-off is that the evening is yours to organize. Popular shows and specialty restaurants fill up, so book them in the Norwegian app before you sail.

## Which Norwegian ship are you on?
The fleet varies a lot by age and size, and the ship shapes the trip more than the brand does.

| Class | Ships include | What to expect |
|---|---|---|
| Prima and Prima Plus | Norwegian Prima, Viva, Aqua | Newest; more outdoor space, a large Haven, top-deck thrill rides |
| Breakaway Plus | Norwegian Escape, Joy, Bliss, Encore | Very large; go-karts on most, Observation Lounge on the Alaska regulars |
| Breakaway | Norwegian Breakaway, Getaway | Waterfront promenade, busy Caribbean and Bermuda routes |
| Epic | Norwegian Epic | The ship that introduced studio cabins |
| Older, smaller ships | Jewel class and similar | Fewer attractions, longer and more unusual itineraries |
| Pride of America | Year-round in Hawaii | U.S.-flagged, inter-island week with no sea days |

Norwegian Bliss and Norwegian Encore have been regulars on Seattle–Alaska round trips in recent seasons, but deployments change, so check your year.

## What a Norwegian cruise really costs
Norwegian's total has more layers than many lines. There is a daily service charge of roughly US$20 per person in standard cabins at recent rates, and the Free at Sea promotion, which bundles drinks, specialty dining, wifi and excursion credit, carries service charges on those perks. The [Norwegian gratuities](/articles/norwegian-gratuities-per-day/) page breaks it down.

> **Example:** an Edmonton couple on a 7-night Seattle–Alaska sailing should budget the fare, about US$280 in daily service charges (roughly C$380–390), plus the Free at Sea service charges, which can add a few hundred dollars more. Compare that total, not the headline fare, with other lines.

## Who Norwegian suits, and who it doesn't
### Good fit
- Couples and friends who want flexible dinners around port days and shows
- Families with children who like go-karts, waterslides and laser tag
- Solo travellers, thanks to studio cabins and the Studio Lounge
- First-timers who would find formal nights more stress than fun

### Less of a fit
- Cruisers who value the same table and waiter every night
- Anyone who wants a quiet, adult-feeling ship; see [quiet ships without waterslides](/articles/quiet-ships-without-waterslides/)
- Budget-watchers who dislike add-ons and want one inclusive price

## The Haven and studios: Norwegian at both ends
Norwegian covers the extremes better than most mass-market lines. The Haven is a keycard-access suite complex with its own restaurant, lounge and sundeck, plus butler and concierge service It gives you big-ship attractions without big-ship crowds.

At the other end, studio cabins are small inside rooms built and priced for one person, grouped around a Studio Lounge where solo guests can meet. Norwegian introduced them on Norwegian Epic and has added them to most newer ships, which takes much of the sting out of the single supplement covered in [solo cruising from Canada](/articles/solo-cruising-canada-supplements/).

## Norwegian from Canada: Alaska, Hawaii and the East Coast
For western Canadians, Alaska is the easy entry point. Seattle round trips usually call at Victoria, BC, while Vancouver sailings save a border crossing for many B.C. and Alberta travellers. The [Calgary to Seattle vs Vancouver comparison](/articles/calgary-vs-seattle-vs-vancouver-alaska/) weighs the drive, flights and hotel costs.

From Ontario and Quebec, Norwegian's Florida departures cover the Caribbean in winter, and its northeast sailings visit Atlantic Canada in late summer and fall. Pride of America in Hawaii is a long flight but a unique week; [Hawaii cruise costs from Canada](/articles/hawaii-cruise-cost-canada/) covers the budget.

> **Canadian tip:** Norwegian sells fares to Canadian residents in Canadian dollars, but onboard spending posts in US dollars. Settle your account with a no-foreign-transaction-fee credit card to avoid an extra 2.5% on every drink and excursion.

## Deciding if freestyle cruising suits your travel style
Ask yourself two questions: do you want dinner on your own schedule, and are you happy to plan your evenings yourself? If both answers are yes, Norwegian is a strong choice. If you would rather the ship set the rhythm, compare it with a more traditional line before you book.
