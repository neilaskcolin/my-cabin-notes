Holland America's Signature Beverage Package has recently cost about US$61 per person per day, plus the bar service charge, and pays off at roughly 5–7 drinks a day. For many couples the smarter comparison is not package versus paying by the glass, but cruise-only versus a Have It All fare that already includes it.

## Holland America drink packages and prices
Holland America sells three main packages. Prices below are recent figures per person per day; confirm them for your sailing.

| Package | Recent price (USD) | What's covered | Daily limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Signature | about $61 | Cocktails, beer, wine by the glass, specialty coffee, soda, water, up to about $11–12 a drink | 15 drinks, zero-proof included |
| Elite | about $66 | The same, up to about $15–16 a drink | 15 alcoholic drinks, zero-proof unlimited |
| Quench (no alcohol) | about $18 | Specialty coffee, mocktails, smoothies, soda, energy drinks, water | 15 drinks |

If you order something above your cap, you pay only the difference. A bar service charge is added to the package price. It was 18% for years and was reported to rise to 20% for sailings from June 2026.

> **Quick math:** Signature at about US$61 plus 20% is roughly US$73 a day. For a couple on a 7-night sailing that is about US$1,020, roughly C$1,380–1,430, before a single drink is poured.

## The catches worth knowing
The package excludes minibar items, room service and purchases in the shops, and it does not work on RelaxAway, Half Moon Cay, the line's Bahamas stop. Packages are for one person and drinks cannot be shared. And if one adult in a stateroom buys it, every adult in that stateroom must.

## Have It All vs buying the package
Have It All bundles the Signature package with specialty dining, Surf wifi and shore excursion credit. It has recently been priced around US$60–70 per person per day, though it varies by sailing, and the bar service charge on its drinks is included.

That means a couple who would buy Signature anyway often gets dinner at the Pinnacle Grill or Canaletto, wifi and US$100 each in excursion credit on a week's sailing for about the same money. Holland America's Early Booking Bonus version has gone further, adding Elite drinks, Premium wifi and prepaid crew appreciation. Read the current terms, as [Cruise Line Sales Decoded](/articles/cruise-line-sales-decoded/) explains.

## How to decide, step by step
1. Count realistic drinks per day, including coffees and sodas, for both sea days and port days.
2. Look at the itinerary. A 7-night Alaska sailing from Vancouver has long days ashore in Juneau, Skagway and Ketchikan, when you drink little on board.
3. Price the package with the service charge, then compare with a Have It All fare for the same cabin.
4. Check whether your dates carry a promotion with drinks included.
5. If you mainly drink wine with dinner, compare with bringing your own under the [Holland America alcohol policy](/articles/holland-america-alcohol-policy/).

## Is the package worth it on Holland America?
For steady drinkers on sea-day-heavy trips, such as a Panama Canal crossing from Fort Lauderdale or a transatlantic, yes. For light drinkers, often not. Holland America's guests tend to drink less than on party-focused lines, and paying by the glass, using happy hours at select bars, or a Quench package for coffee lovers is usually cheaper. ## Getting the package bundled instead
Many Holland America promotions fold the drinks into the fare. For a couple who would buy the package anyway, that can be worth over C$1,000 on a week's sailing, so check your dates first.
