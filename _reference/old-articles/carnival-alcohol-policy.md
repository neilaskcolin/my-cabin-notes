Carnival allows one sealed 750 ml bottle of wine or champagne per guest aged 21 or over, carried on at embarkation. Beer and spirits stay off the ship, opening your bottle in a restaurant or bar costs about US$15, and anything you buy in port waits for you until the last night.

## What Carnival lets you bring on embarkation day
The allowance is short and strict, and it is checked at the terminal X-ray before you reach the ship.

- **Wine or champagne:** one 750 ml bottle per guest 21 or older, unopened, in your carry-on. A couple gets two bottles; a family of four with two teenagers still gets two.
- **Non-alcoholic drinks:** up to 12 cans or cartons of soda, juice or water per guest (355 ml or smaller), also in carry-on. Carnival dropped bottled soft drinks and bottled water from the allowance years ago, so cans and cartons only.
- **Not allowed:** beer, spirits, liqueurs, hard seltzers and any opened wine.
- **Checked bags:** no drinks of any kind. Pack the bottle in your hand luggage or it may be pulled out of your suitcase.

### What happens if security finds something
Prohibited bottles are taken at screening. At best they are returned at the end of the cruise, so don't pack anything you'd be upset to lose.

## When the US$15 corkage fee applies
Drinking your bottle in your stateroom is free. Bring it to the main dining room, a specialty restaurant such as Fahrenheit 555, or a bar, and Carnival charges a corkage fee of about US$15 per bottle at recent policy, roughly C$20–21.

For a couple on a 7-night sailing, two bottles of wine from home over two dinners adds about US$30 in corkage, which is still far cheaper than ordering two bottles off the wine list. Carnival's [drink package](/articles/carnival-drink-package-price/) also gives a discount on bottles of wine, so heavy wine drinkers should compare both routes.

> **Watch out:** the drinking age on Carnival sailings from US home ports is 21, not the 18 or 19 you may be used to in Canada. Bartenders check the Sail & Sign card, and adults caught passing drinks to under-21s risk being put off the ship.

## Bottles bought in port or in the Fun Shops
Spirits and wine bought ashore in Cozumel, Nassau or Juneau are collected at the gangway and stored. The same goes for duty-free bottles from the ship's Fun Shops, which are delivered or collected on the final night. You can't open either during the cruise.

> **Canadian tip:** if you plan to bring bottles home, CBSA lets returning residents away 48 hours or more bring in duty-free one of the following: 1.5 litres of wine, 1.14 litres of spirits, or 8.5 litres of beer. Anything over that is taxed at the border, so a cheap bottle of Caribbean rum can stop being cheap. [Duty-free shopping on a cruise](/articles/cruise-shopping-onboard-duty-free/) covers the rest.

## Drink packages and the cheaper middle ground
If your bottle of wine won't cover the week, the realistic options are paying per drink, buying CHEERS!, or carrying your soft-drink allowance and buying only the occasional cocktail. Light drinkers usually do best with the last option. What a single cocktail or beer costs on board is in [what's included on a cruise drink menu](/articles/cruise-drink-menu-what-included-basics/).

## How Carnival compares
| Line | Wine you can bring | Corkage (recent) |
|---|---|---|
| **Carnival** | 1 bottle per adult (21+) | ~$15 in bars and restaurants |
| [Royal Caribbean](/articles/royal-caribbean-alcohol-policy/) | 2 bottles per stateroom | ~$15 in bars and restaurants |
| [MSC](/articles/msc-alcohol-policy/) | Generally none | Not applicable |

Carnival counts its allowance per adult, so two adults in a cabin end up level with Royal Caribbean's two bottles per stateroom, and three or four adults sharing come out ahead. MSC generally allows none. Corkage is about the same on Carnival and Royal Caribbean. All eight lines are laid out in [cruise line alcohol policies compared](/articles/cruise-line-alcohol-policies-compared/).

## Sorting this before you pack
Put one bottle per adult in the carry-on with your soft-drink cans, decide on a package before you sail, and recheck Carnival's prohibited-items list a week before embarkation.
