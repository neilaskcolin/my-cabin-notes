For most Montrealers, Fort Lauderdale is the easiest Caribbean departure port, with Miami a close second and Port Canaveral a solid third. The bigger risk isn't which port you pick but flying on sailing day from a city that gets ice storms all winter.

## The Florida ports ranked for Montreal
Flight and transfer details are typical; nonstop schedules vary by season.

| Port | Fly into | Flights from Montreal | Airport to ship | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fort Lauderdale (Port Everglades) | FLL | Frequent nonstops in winter, about 3 hours | 10–15 minutes | Easiest overall |
| Miami (PortMiami) | MIA, or FLL | Nonstops year-round on some carriers | 20–30 minutes from MIA | The widest choice of ships and sailings |
| Port Canaveral | MCO (Orlando) | Nonstops, especially in winter | 45–60 minutes | Disney and many of the newest big ships |
| Tampa | TPA | Seasonal service | About 15–20 minutes | Western Caribbean, fewer ships |

### Fort Lauderdale
The airport is small, the port is next door and taxis are cheap. If your line sails similar itineraries from both Fort Lauderdale and Miami, choose the Fort Lauderdale sailing.

### Miami
Miami has more cruise departures than any other port, so your preferred ship may only sail from here. Its airport is large and busy, and getting to the port can be slow on a busy turnaround day. Flying into Fort Lauderdale instead is a common workaround when fares or times are better.

### Port Canaveral
Royal Caribbean, Carnival, Disney and others base some of their biggest ships here. Orlando's airport is well served from Montreal, but the drive to the port is the longest of the major Florida options. Port-by-port transfer costs are compared in [cruise ship transfers explained](/articles/cruise-ship-transfers-explained/).

## How to choose your port and flights
1. **Start with the ship and itinerary.** Pick the cruise you want first; [Eastern vs Western vs Southern Caribbean](/articles/eastern-vs-western-caribbean-first-cruise/) helps narrow it down.
2. **Check nonstop flights for your exact dates.** Winter schedules from Montreal are much richer than spring and fall.
3. **Compare flying into a neighbouring airport.** Fort Lauderdale for Miami sailings, or Miami for Fort Lauderdale, can save money or a connection.
4. **Fly in the day before, or two days before in mid-winter.** Freezing rain and de-icing delays at Montréal–Trudeau are routine from December to March (see [snow delay risk on winter flights](/articles/snow-delay-winter-flights-caribbean-cruise/)).
5. **Book the hotel and transfer together.** Many port hotels run cruise-morning shuttles.

## Why connections are the real risk from Quebec
Nonstops are the safest way to Florida. If your dates force a connection, often through Toronto or a US hub, a single late inbound flight can leave you overnighting in the wrong city. Montreal's options to Port Canaveral and Tampa are thinner than to Fort Lauderdale and Miami, which makes a missed connection harder to recover from. On a connecting itinerary, cruise line air with its delay protection is worth pricing.

Some Montrealers also look at US airports such as Plattsburgh, about an hour south, for lower fares to Florida. Count the border crossing, parking and thinner schedules before assuming it is cheaper.

> **Good to know:** Quebec residents who book with a Quebec-licensed travel agency contribute to the FICAV, a provincial compensation fund that can reimburse clients when an agency or travel supplier fails to deliver, subject to its conditions. Ask your agency whether your booking is covered.

You need a valid passport for any of these ports; the rules are in [do Canadians need a visa or ESTA](/articles/canadians-visa-esta-cruise-us-port/).
