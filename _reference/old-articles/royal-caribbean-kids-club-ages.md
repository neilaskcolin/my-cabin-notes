Royal Caribbean's kids club, Adventure Ocean, is free for ages 3 to 11 and splits children into three age groups. Teens aged 12 to 17 have their own spaces, and many ships offer a paid nursery for babies from 6 months.

## Royal Caribbean kids club age groups
Children join the group that matches their age on the day of sailing. Siblings in neighbouring groups can sometimes be placed together on request, at the staff's discretion.

| Group | Ages | What it's like |
|---|---|---|
| Royal Babies and Tots (paid) | 6–36 months | Nursery with sensory play, on many ships |
| Aquanauts | 3–5 | Crafts, story time, games, with naps and snacks built in |
| Explorers | 6–8 | Science activities, scavenger hunts, sports |
| Voyagers | 9–11 | Team games, video game sessions, talent shows |
| Teens | 12–14 and 15–17 | Own lounge, late events, teen-only activities |

## Babies and toddlers on Royal Caribbean
The Royal Babies and Tots nursery runs on many of the larger ships, such as the Oasis, Quantum and Icon classes, and charges by the hour. Spaces fill quickly on sea days, so book sessions as early as the ship allows. Under-3s can also use parent-accompanied play areas, and Icon-class ships have Surfside, a neighbourhood designed around young families. For the bigger picture, see [cruising with a baby or toddler](/articles/cruising-with-baby-toddler/).

## Royal Caribbean kids club hours
Adventure Ocean typically runs morning, afternoon and evening sessions, with breaks around meals, roughly from 9 a.m. to 10 p.m. on sea days. A paid late-night session continues after regular hours, charged per child per hour. Exact times appear in the Cruise Compass in the Royal app.

On many ships, My Family Time Dining lets children eat quickly in the main dining room at the start of dinner, then go to Adventure Ocean with staff while the adults finish their meal.

## Registration and check-in
Register on embarkation day, or through the Royal app before sailing where it's offered, and drop in at the open house on the first afternoon so children can meet the staff. Bring:
- each child's full name, date of birth and any allergies or medical needs
- the names of every adult allowed to sign them out
- your phone with the Royal app, which staff may use to reach you on board
- labelled sunscreen and a refillable water bottle for younger children

> **Canadian tip:** if one parent is travelling alone with the kids, carry a signed consent letter from the other parent, as the Government of Canada recommends. Border officers and cruise staff can ask for it, and it is easy to forget when only booking the ship.

## When to sail for the best kids club experience
March break and July are the busiest weeks, with larger groups and full nurseries. March break dates differ by province, so families from Ontario and Alberta may be on different sailings. Off-peak weeks mean smaller groups and easier nursery bookings. [Kids sail free promotions](/articles/kids-sail-free-catch/) can make the fare cheaper too.

> **Watch out:** age groups follow date of birth. A child who turns 6 shortly after the cruise stays in Aquanauts, so check birthdays near the cut-offs before promising a sibling they'll be together.

## How Royal Caribbean compares
| Line | Main club | Youngest age | Care for under-3s |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Royal Caribbean** | Adventure Ocean | 3 | Paid nursery on many ships |
| [Carnival](/articles/carnival-kids-club-ages/) | Camp Ocean | 2 | None, but club starts at 2 |
| [Norwegian](/articles/norwegian-kids-club-ages/) | Splash Academy | 3 | Parent must stay (Guppies) |

Carnival and Norwegian are the lines most often weighed against Royal Caribbean. Carnival takes children a year earlier, at 2, while Royal Caribbean is the only one of the three with a staffed nursery for babies, on many ships. Norwegian's Guppies sessions need a parent present. The full side-by-side for all eight lines is in [Kids Clubs on Cruise Ships: Ages, Hours and Costs](/articles/kids-clubs-cruise-ships-ages-costs/).

## Matching the ship to your children's ages
Youth facilities differ across the fleet, from compact clubs on older ships to large complexes on the Oasis and Icon classes. Before booking, check that your ship has a nursery if you need one and the evening hours your family will use.
