Most Calgarians fly to YVR and ride the Canada Line SkyTrain downtown, and that is usually the cheapest and least stressful way to reach a ship at Canada Place. The one thing that really matters is doing it the day before you sail.

## The fly-and-SkyTrain route, leg by leg
Here is the whole journey from a Calgary front door to the gangway.

1. **Get to YYC.** Allow at least 90 minutes before a domestic departure, more on summer Fridays and long weekends. If you are leaving a car, see [park and fly options at YYC](/articles/park-and-fly-yyc-cruise-trips/).
2. **Fly to Vancouver.** WestJet and Air Canada run frequent nonstops, and lower-cost carriers serve the route too. Flight time is about 90 minutes, and you gain an hour moving to Pacific time.
3. **Collect your bags at YVR.** Follow signs to the Canada Line station, which sits between the domestic and international terminals.
4. **Ride the Canada Line to Waterfront.** Trains run every few minutes and take about 26 minutes. At mid-2026 fares, expect roughly C$11–12 per adult, which includes the C$6.50 airport AddFare charged on trips leaving YVR. Tap a credit card or buy a ticket at the machine.
5. **Walk to Canada Place.** Waterfront Station is a couple of minutes from the terminal entrance. Porters take checked bags at the curb.
6. **Check in and clear US preclearance.** Alaska is a US state, so US border officers process you in the terminal before boarding. Have passports out (see [passports for an Alaska cruise from Vancouver](/articles/passport-alaska-cruise-vancouver/)).

> **Example:** a Calgary couple on a 7:30 p.m. flight the night before lands around 8 p.m. Pacific, rides the Canada Line to a downtown hotel and walks to Canada Place for an 11:30 a.m. check-in. Total travel cost: two flights, one hotel night and about C$25 in SkyTrain fares.

## When a taxi makes more sense
The SkyTrain has no luggage racks and gets busy at rush hour. If you have big suitcases, a mobility issue, small children or a group of three or four, a taxi or rideshare for C$35–45 is easier and splits well. Every option, including private cars and hotel shuttles, is compared in [YYC to Canada Place transfer options](/articles/yyc-to-canada-place-transfer-options/).

## The alternatives to flying
- **Drive.** About 970 km, usually split over two days through Banff, Golden and Kamloops. Parking for a week adds up, so the numbers are worked through in [driving from Calgary to an Alaska cruise](/articles/drive-from-calgary-to-alaska-cruise/).
- **Rocky Mountaineer.** Two days by daylight from Banff or Lake Louise with a night in Kamloops, ending in Vancouver. Book months ahead; it is a splurge, not a transfer.
- **Coach.** Private operators run the Calgary–Vancouver corridor as a long single day. It is the cheapest option but the slowest, and schedules change.

If you want to skip planes entirely, [cruising from Vancouver without flying](/articles/cruise-from-vancouver-without-flying/) covers no-fly routes and itineraries.

## Why the day before matters
A delayed morning flight is the most common way Albertans miss a Vancouver sailing. One weather hold or mechanical issue can push you past the boarding cut-off, and the ship won't wait. A hotel night costs a fraction of catching the ship in Alaska. The full risk math is in [flying the day before vs same day](/articles/fly-day-before-cruise/), and hotel picks near the terminal are in [where to stay the night before](/articles/where-to-stay-vancouver-night-before-cruise/).

## What the trip costs, all-in
For one person, at recent prices:

- Return flights YYC–YVR: about C$150–350 if booked early
- Hotel the night before: about C$180–300 downtown in summer, shared
- Canada Line to Canada Place: about C$11–12 one way
- Back to YVR after the cruise: cheaper, since the AddFare only applies leaving the airport

A cruise package that bundles flights, a pre-night hotel and transfers can come in close to the DIY total and adds delay protection, so price both before booking. The full trip budget is in [the real cost of a Calgary to Alaska cruise](/articles/calgary-alaska-cruise-cost/).
