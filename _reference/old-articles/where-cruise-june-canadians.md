June is the month when almost everything lines up. Alaska is fully open with near-endless daylight, Europe is warm without August's crowds, and you can still beat the Canadian school-holiday rush if you sail in the first three weeks.

## Alaska in June: long days, early-summer prices
Around the summer solstice, Juneau gets roughly 18 hours of daylight, so sail-away views last late into the evening. Southeast Alaska's driest stretch of the season tends to fall in late spring and early summer, mountains still carry snow, and humpback whales have arrived for the summer. The [Alaska weather month by month](/articles/alaska-cruise-weather-by-month/) guide has port-by-port detail.

Early June still prices below July, while late June starts to climb as families join. For Western Canadians this is prime Vancouver departure season, and the [first Alaska cruise guide](/articles/first-alaska-cruise-what-to-know/) covers the basics.

> **Example:** a Winnipeg couple choosing between a mid-June and a mid-July sailing on the same ship will usually find June cheaper, with longer evenings and often less rain. They give up a little warmth on glacier days and some of the late-summer bear viewing that comes with the salmon runs.

### A cultural bonus
National Indigenous Peoples Day falls on June 21, and in even-numbered years Juneau hosts Celebration, a large gathering of Southeast Alaska Native cultures, usually in early June. Confirm dates before you plan around it. [Tlingit culture and totem poles in Alaska ports](/articles/tlingit-culture-totem-poles-alaska-ports/) is good background either way.

## Europe in June
June is one of Europe's best months. The Mediterranean is warm but not yet scorching, and the European summer holiday crush mostly arrives in July and August. Further north, Norway's coast has the midnight sun above the Arctic Circle, and the British Isles and Baltic are mild with very long evenings.

- **Mediterranean:** warm sea, busy but manageable ports. See the [best Mediterranean itinerary for couples](/articles/mediterranean-cruise-best-itinerary-couples/).
- **Norwegian fjords:** waterfalls full from snowmelt and near-constant light.
- **Rhine and Danube:** pleasant temperatures, though spring rain occasionally affects river levels.

Flights from Toronto and Montreal to Europe are cheaper and more frequent than from the Prairies, so Albertans and Manitobans should price the air before choosing between Europe and Alaska.

## The Caribbean in June
Hurricane season officially starts on June 1, but early-season storms are usually infrequent, and real risk climbs more noticeably from August. June Caribbean sailings are hot and humid, and they become noticeably more family-heavy once US schools finish. It is a reasonable choice if it fits your calendar, with insurance bought at deposit.

## How to choose your June cruise
1. **Fix your dates first.** If you have school-age children, check your board's last day. Quebec often finishes around June 20 to 23, while Ontario, Alberta and BC usually run to the end of the month.
2. **Price the flights.** A Calgary or Edmonton traveller reaches Vancouver in under two hours; Europe is a long-haul trip with a much bigger airfare.
3. **Decide what you want most.** Wilderness, wildlife and scenery point to Alaska. Food, history and city walks point to Europe.
4. **Book early for June.** Popular Alaska balconies and Mediterranean sailings sell well ahead, and [booking now vs waiting](/articles/booking-2027-now-vs-waiting/) shows why waiting rarely pays for peak months.
