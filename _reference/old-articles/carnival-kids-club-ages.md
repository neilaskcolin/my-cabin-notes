Carnival's kids club, Camp Ocean, takes children from age 2, and that single fact is why so many toddler families choose the line. Kids are split into three groups up to age 11, teens get two clubs of their own, and a paid late-night service gives parents an evening to themselves.

## How Carnival's age groups split
| Group | Ages | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Night Owls | 6 months–11 | Paid, about 10 p.m.–1 a.m. | Pack-and-plays for babies |
| Penguins | 2–5 | Included until about 10 p.m. | Diapers fine, bring supplies |
| Stingrays | 6–8 | Included | Crafts, games, deck activities |
| Sharks | 9–11 | Included | Sports, challenges, movie nights |
| Circle C | 12–14 | Included | Teens sign themselves in and out |
| Club O2 | 15–17 | Included | Own lounge, later hours |

## Babies and toddlers on Carnival
Penguins is the standout. Most big lines start their main club at 3, while Carnival takes 2-year-olds still in diapers. Staff ask you to leave diapers and wipes. For babies under 2 there's no full-time nursery, so plan on parent time during the day and Night Owls in the evening. Most Carnival sailings need babies to be at least 6 months old on embarkation day, with some longer voyages requiring 12 months.

[Cruising with a baby or toddler](/articles/cruising-with-baby-toddler/) covers cribs, food and strollers. Carnival relies on group care rather than in-cabin sitters, which [cruise ship babysitting](/articles/cruise-ship-babysitting-in-cabin-service/) explains.

## Opening hours and Night Owls
On sea days Camp Ocean typically runs morning to late afternoon and again from early evening, closing briefly around meals. On port days it opens once the ship docks, so parents can go ashore. The HUB app shows the exact schedule for your ship.

After about 10 p.m., Night Owls takes over for a fee per child per hour, recently around US$7–8 plus a service charge. There's no sibling discount.

> **Quick math:** two kids in Night Owls for three hours on two nights is 12 child-hours, about US$105 once the service charge is added, roughly C$140–150 for two adult evenings out.

## What the Carnival kids program feels like
Programming is lively rather than educational: crafts, scavenger hunts, deck games and themed parties. Many ships add Seuss at Sea activities, including story time and a paid Dr. Seuss character breakfast that young children love. Outside the club, WaterWorks slides and, on Excel-class ships such as Mardi Gras, the BOLT roller coaster keep older kids busy.

## Signing up for Camp Ocean
1. Register each child online before you sail, or at the open house on embarkation afternoon.
2. Name every adult allowed to collect younger children; only they can sign kids out.
3. Pack diapers, wipes and a change of clothes in a labelled bag for 2-year-olds.
4. Check the HUB app each evening for the next day's sessions.
5. Book Night Owls during the day at the youth centre if you want a late night.

> **Canadian tip:** if one parent is sailing alone with the kids, carry a signed consent letter from the other parent, which the Government of Canada recommends for children travelling abroad. Each child also needs their own passport, as covered in [passports, NEXUS and ID for cruises](/articles/passports-nexus-id-cruise-documents/).

March break dates differ by province, and mid-March Florida sailings also carry US spring-break crowds; see [where to cruise in March](/articles/where-cruise-march-canadians/).

## How Carnival compares
| Line | Main club | Youngest age | Care for under-3s |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Carnival** | Camp Ocean | 2 | None, but club starts at 2 |
| [Royal Caribbean](/articles/royal-caribbean-kids-club-ages/) | Adventure Ocean | 3 | Paid nursery on many ships |
| [MSC](/articles/msc-kids-club-ages/) | Mini Club | 3 | Paid Baby Care from 6 months; Baby Time with parent |

Carnival is the only one of the three whose main club takes 2-year-olds for free. Royal Caribbean starts Adventure Ocean at 3 but runs a paid nursery on many ships, and MSC pairs free parent-stay Baby Time with a paid Baby Care drop-off service from 6 months. All eight lines are in [kids clubs on cruise ships](/articles/kids-clubs-cruise-ships-ages-costs/).

## Matching the ship to your children's ages
Youth spaces differ across the fleet, from the big Excel-class clubs to smaller rooms on older ships. Check your ship's Camp Ocean layout, Night Owls availability and teen spaces before booking, especially with a 2-year-old.
