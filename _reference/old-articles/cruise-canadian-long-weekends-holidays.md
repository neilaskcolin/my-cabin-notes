Some Canadian long weekends are genuinely good value for a short cruise, and some are not. Victoria Day and Canadian Thanksgiving fall outside the big US travel weeks, while Family Day, Canada Day and Labour Day collide with American holidays and get priced that way.

## Why US holidays set the price
Most cruise passengers on mainstream lines are American, so US school breaks and holidays drive demand. A Canadian holiday only helps you if it falls in a week when Americans are not travelling in large numbers.

## Canadian long weekends in 2027, checked against US dates
| Canadian holiday | 2027 date | What the US is doing | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Family Day (and equivalents) | Mon Feb 15 | Presidents' Day, same Monday; many US winter breaks | Busy, book early |
| Good Friday and Easter Monday | Mar 26 and Mar 29 | Easter and spring break season | Busy |
| Victoria Day | Mon May 24 | Memorial Day is a week later, May 31 | Good value window |
| Canada Day | Thu Jul 1 | July 4 falls that Sunday | Busy peak summer |
| Civic Holiday (most provinces) | Mon Aug 2 | No US holiday | Fine, but summer prices |
| Labour Day | Mon Sep 6 | Labor Day, same day | Busy end-of-summer |
| Thanksgiving | Mon Oct 11 | Columbus Day, not a major travel holiday | Good value window |
| Remembrance Day (where observed) | Thu Nov 11 | Veterans Day | Modest demand |

Family Day goes by other names in some provinces, such as Louis Riel Day in Manitoba, Heritage Day in Nova Scotia and Islander Day in PEI, but it falls on the same Monday. Quebec's Fête nationale on Thursday, June 24, 2027 is another option for Quebecers, with no US overlap.

## The best windows for a short cruise
### Victoria Day
The weekend before US Memorial Day is often quieter than the one after. A three- to five-night Bahamas sailing from Florida fits well, and at the start of the Alaska season some lines run short Pacific coastal sailings out of Vancouver and Seattle. Late May weather is warm in the Bahamas and before the worst of hurricane season.

### Thanksgiving
Canadian Thanksgiving sits in a quiet patch for US travel. Short Caribbean and Bahamas sailings are common, and October is also the tail end of the Canada and New England fall season. [New England and Canada fall cruises](/articles/new-england-canada-fall-cruises/) covers those routes. The trade-off is that October is still hurricane season in the Caribbean, so read [what happens to a cruise in a hurricane](/articles/what-happens-hurricane-cruise-caribbean/).

> **Canadian tip:** add one or two vacation days to a Monday holiday and you get a four- or five-day window. A Toronto or Montreal couple can fly to Fort Lauderdale on Wednesday evening, sail a Thursday four-night Bahamas cruise, and be home on Monday night of the long weekend.

## Making a long-weekend cruise work
- **Fly the day before.** With only a few days off, a missed ship ruins the whole trip. See [flying the day before vs same day](/articles/fly-day-before-cruise/).
- **Check the return flight.** Holiday Monday flights home are busy; book early or come back Tuesday.
- **Prefer nonstops.** Toronto and Montreal have the most nonstop options to Florida. Western Canadians may find a short Vancouver sailing easier.
- **Price the holiday against the week after.** Sometimes shifting by one week saves more than the holiday weekend itself.
- **Watch school calendars.** Ontario's March Break and other provincial spring breaks are expensive weeks regardless of the US.

## What a short cruise costs
A four-night Bahamas cruise for a couple, including flights from Toronto, a hotel night, gratuities and modest spending, often costs roughly half to two-thirds of a full 7-night trip. [What a Bahamas cruise costs from Canada](/articles/bahamas-cruise-cost-canada/) and [the realistic Caribbean budget](/articles/caribbean-cruise-cost-canada-budget/) give the line-by-line numbers.

## Picking your weekend
If you want value, aim for Victoria Day or Thanksgiving and book a few months ahead. If you are tied to Family Day or March Break, treat it like peak season and book early for the best cabins.
