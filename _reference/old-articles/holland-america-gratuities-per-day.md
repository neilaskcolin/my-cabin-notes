Holland America charges a daily "crew appreciation" of about US$18 per person in standard staterooms and US$20 in suites, for sailings from June 1, 2026. It goes on your onboard account each day unless you prepay it, and the standard Have It All package does not cover it.

## How much is Holland America's crew appreciation?
The rate rose by US$1 a day in June 2026, from US$17 and US$19. It applies to every guest in the stateroom for every night of the voyage. Because Holland America sails so many long itineraries, that dollar adds up faster than on a line built around 7-night trips.

| Party and voyage | Approx. USD | Roughly in CAD |
|---|---|---|
| Couple, standard, 7-night Alaska | $252 | C$340–355 |
| Couple, suite, 7 nights | $280 | C$380–390 |
| Couple, standard, 14-night Alaska | $504 | C$680–705 |
| Couple, standard, 28-night Collectors' Voyage | $1,008 | C$1,360–1,410 |
| Couple, standard, 120-night world voyage | $4,320 | C$5,830–6,050 |

> **Quick math:** guests times nights times the daily rate, then multiply by 1.35–1.40 for Canadian dollars. Long voyages are where prepaying really earns its keep.

## Does Have It All include gratuities?
Not in its standard form. Have It All bundles the Signature drinks package, specialty dining, Surf wifi and shore excursion credit, with crew appreciation charged on top.

Holland America's Have It All Early Booking Bonus has gone further, including prepaid crew appreciation along with upgraded drinks and wifi. On a 14-night Alaska sailing that is worth about US$500 for a couple, so ask which version you are being quoted. [Cruise Line Sales Decoded](/articles/cruise-line-sales-decoded/) explains how to read these offers.

## Should Canadians prepay Holland America gratuities?
Usually, yes, for two reasons.

### Locking the exchange
On a booking priced in Canadian dollars, prepaid crew appreciation is normally added in CAD. Paid on board instead, it posts in US dollars and converts at your card's rate, often with a 2.5% foreign-transaction fee. [Paying in CAD vs USD for a cruise](/articles/paying-cad-vs-usd-cruise/) covers the trade-off.

### Locking the rate
Prepaying generally fixes the amount you pay. Whether a booking prepaid before a rate change is topped up is not always clear, so ask when you prepay.

> **Canadian tip:** if you do pay on board, settle the account with a no-foreign-transaction-fee card. On a couple's 28-night voyage, a 2.5% fee adds about US$25 to the gratuities alone.

## What the daily charge doesn't cover
- **Bar and restaurant service charge.** A separate charge is added to bar drinks, drink packages and specialty dining. It was 18% for years and was reported at 20% from June 2026.
- **Spa and salon.** These carry their own service charge.
- **Porters and tour guides.** Tip in cash. At Canada Place, Canadian dollars are generally fine; in Seattle or Fort Lauderdale, bring small US bills.

The [Holland America drink package price](/articles/holland-america-drink-package-price/) page shows how that service charge changes the maths on drinks.

## Adjusting crew appreciation on board
The Front Office can review the daily charge if you have a genuine service problem. Removing it wholesale cuts into pay for dining and housekeeping crew who did their jobs, so a reduction tied to a specific issue is fairer. A cash tip or a named comment card is a good way to recognize a standout steward.

## Longer voyages and Grand Voyages
On Holland America's long itineraries, gratuities become one of the biggest fixed extras. Some Grand Voyage fares and promotions have bundled perks such as prepaid gratuities or onboard credit, so ask before you book.
