The best-value route from Calgary to your ship is a flight to YVR, then the Canada Line SkyTrain to Waterfront Station, a short walk from the cruise terminal. A taxi makes more sense with bulky luggage or three or more people sharing. Whichever you choose, do it the day before you sail.

## The whole journey at a glance
Here's how the trip usually runs for a Calgarian on a Saturday Alaska sailing:

1. **Friday, at YYC:** arrive 90 minutes to two hours before a domestic flight. It's a Canadian flight, so there's no US preclearance on this leg.
2. **In the air:** about an hour and a half to Vancouver, and you gain an hour switching from Mountain to Pacific time.
3. **At YVR:** collect your bags in domestic arrivals and follow the signs to the Canada Line.
4. **The transfer:** about 26 minutes by SkyTrain to Waterfront, or 25–40 minutes by taxi depending on traffic.
5. **Friday night:** check in to your hotel and walk past Canada Place so you know the entrance.
6. **Saturday morning:** check out, keep your carry-on with you, and walk to the terminal for your boarding window.

## Transfer options from YVR compared
Prices are rough, at recent rates. Confirm current fares before you travel.

| Option | Rough cost | Time | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Canada Line SkyTrain | About C$10 per person | About 26 minutes, trains every few minutes | One bag each, couples, solo travellers |
| Taxi | About C$35–45 per car | 25–40 minutes | Big cases, mobility needs, three or more people |
| Rideshare | Varies with demand | 25–40 minutes | App users, off-peak times |
| Cruise line coach | About C$35–50 per person | Waits to fill, then direct | Nervous first-timers on cruise day |
| Private van | About C$90–140 for up to six | 25–40 minutes | Families and groups |

### SkyTrain tips
You can tap a contactless credit card or a Compass Card at the fare gates, and each traveller needs their own card. Leaving YVR includes an airport add-fare. There are elevators and escalators the whole way, so rolling suitcases are fine, though trains get crowded at weekday rush hours. At Waterfront, the walk to Canada Place takes a few minutes and is well signed.

### Taxi
Taxis wait at the arrivals level. For three people, a taxi costs about the same as three SkyTrain fares plus the walk, so groups often choose it for convenience.

### Cruise line coach
A coach is handy if you want someone to handle the luggage and the route. It's slower than the train, and on embarkation day it ties you to a schedule you don't control.

## If you must arrive on sailing day
Book the first flight of the morning from YYC, carry everything you need for the first night in your carry-on, and take a taxi straight to the terminal.

> **Watch out:** boarding at Canada Place closes 60–90 minutes before departure. A two-hour delay on a mid-morning flight from Calgary can leave you watching your ship from the seawall. [What happens if your flight to Vancouver is delayed](/articles/cruise-flight-delay-what-happens/) covers your options if it goes wrong.

## Coming home: from the ship to YYC
Disembarkation usually runs from early morning to mid-morning, depending on your luggage tag time or whether you carry your own bags off. Waterfront Station is right there, so the Canada Line back to YVR is the easy choice. Book a flight home no earlier than about midday to allow for the getting-off queue and airport check-in. [Disembarkation morning](/articles/disembarkation-morning-getting-off-ship/) explains how the walk-off works.
