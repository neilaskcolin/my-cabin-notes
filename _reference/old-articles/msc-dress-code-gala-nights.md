MSC runs smart-casual evenings with one or two gala nights per cruise, and those gala nights feel more like an occasion than on most North American lines. You don't need a tuxedo, but one polished outfit each will make you feel at home.

## What to wear on MSC, night by night
The code rises in the evening and relaxes again for the buffet and the late-night bars.

| Time and place | What most people wear | The minimum |
|---|---|---|
| Daytime anywhere | Shorts, sundresses, swimwear with a cover-up | Cover-up away from the pool |
| Casual evenings, main dining room | Trousers or smart jeans, collared shirts, sundresses | No swimwear, tank tops or beach shorts |
| Gala nights, main dining room | Jackets, suits, cocktail and long dresses | Collared shirt and trousers, or a dress |
| Buffet, any night | Whatever you wore that afternoon | Shirt and footwear |
| MSC Yacht Club restaurant | Smart casual every night | Tidier than the buffet |

Themed evenings vary by ship and sailing. A white-clothing party on deck is a familiar MSC tradition, so a white shirt or dress earns its suitcase space. Your daily program in the MSC app lists what's coming.

## How many gala nights does MSC have?
Usually one on a 3- or 4-night Bahamas sailing to Ocean Cay and two on a 7-night Caribbean cruise. Longer voyages, such as MSC's Grand Voyages across the Atlantic, add more. They tend to fall on sea days, when people have the afternoon to get ready.

## Why MSC feels dressier
MSC's European roots show most clearly at dinner. On a Caribbean sailing from Miami you will often share the dining room with guests from Italy, Germany, Latin America and elsewhere, and many of them treat gala night as a proper night out. Suits and long dresses are common, and the ship's photographers set up around the grand staircases for the evening.

That doesn't mean anyone checks your clothes beyond the basic minimum. A man in a crisp collared shirt and dark trousers will not stand out, and plenty of North American guests do exactly that. For the general picture, see [what to wear on formal nights](/articles/formal-nights-what-to-wear/).

### Alaska and MSC Poesia
On MSC's Seattle-based Alaska sailings, evenings are cooler and days are about layers, not sundresses. Gala nights still happen; a jacket over a sweater or a dress with a wrap works. [What people actually wear on an Alaska cruise](/articles/alaska-cruise-dress-code/) has more.

## Packing for a 7-night MSC cruise
A simple plan that covers everything without a second suitcase:
1. Pack one gala outfit each and repeat it, or bring two if you enjoy dressing up.
2. Add three or four smart-casual dinner outfits for the other nights.
3. Include one white top or dress for a possible white party.
4. Bring one pair of dress shoes and one pair of smart sandals or loafers.
5. Pack daytime and pool clothes last, because you will need fewer than you think.

> **Canadian tip:** flying to Florida in January from Calgary or Toronto, put the gala outfits in your carry-on. Winter delays and misrouted bags are common, and a dress or blazer is far harder to replace in the ship's shops than a swimsuit.

## Knowing the themes before you pack
Check the MSC app a few days before sailing for your ship's gala and themed nights. Knowing which evenings to dress for is the easiest way to enjoy MSC's European style without overpacking.
