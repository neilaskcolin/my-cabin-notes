A Tahiti cruise usually means a week or so on a small ship, looping from Papeete through the Society Islands: Moorea, Huahine, Raiatea, Taha'a and Bora Bora. It is one of cruising's most beautiful regions, and one of the most expensive to reach from Canada, so it pays to plan the timing and flights carefully.

## Which islands will you see?

| Island | What stands out | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| Tahiti (Papeete) | The capital, its market and food trucks | A pre- or post-cruise night |
| Moorea | Jagged volcanic peaks, two deep bays | Lookout drives, snorkelling, hiking |
| Huahine | Quiet villages and ancient stone temples | A slower, traditional island day |
| Raiatea | Taputapuatea, a UNESCO-listed sacred site | Culture and history |
| Taha'a | Vanilla farms, shallow coral gardens | Snorkelling and a lagoon day |
| Bora Bora | The famous lagoon and Mount Otemanu | Lagoon tours, beach time |
| Rangiroa or Fakarava | Huge coral atolls in the Tuamotus | Diving and drift snorkelling |

Bora Bora is the name everyone knows, but many travellers find quieter Huahine and Taha'a among the highlights.

## What kind of ships sail there?
French Polynesia is mostly small-ship territory. Paul Gauguin Cruises runs a ship of about 330 guests there year-round, and Windstar and Ponant also sail the islands. For something different, the Aranui, a working cargo-and-passenger ship, sails to the remote Marquesas.

Bigger lines visit occasionally on long voyages from Los Angeles, often combined with Hawaii, which suits travellers who prefer sea days to long flights. For more on small-ship options, see [quiet luxury small-ship lines](/articles/quiet-luxury-small-ship-lines-overlooked/).

## When to go
The dry season, roughly May to October, brings lower humidity and more reliable sunshine. It overlaps the Canadian summer, so you won't be escaping winter, but conditions are at their best.

November to April is warmer, wetter and more humid. Showers are often short, fares can be softer, and it is a genuine winter escape, but the risk of tropical storms is higher. If you sail in those months, choose travel insurance that covers weather disruption.

> **Watch out:** most Canadians connect through Los Angeles or San Francisco, and the flight from California to Papeete takes about eight hours. Arrive at least a day before the ship sails, as a missed connection can mean a day's wait for the next flight.

## Getting there from Canada
From Vancouver, Calgary, Edmonton, Toronto or Montreal, you will usually fly to California and connect to Papeete's Faa'a airport. Check for seasonal options too, as routes change. Jet lag is mild from the West Coast, since Tahiti is only two or three hours behind Pacific time.

Connecting through the US means clearing US customs, which Canadians do with a passport and no ESTA; see [visa and ESTA rules for Canadians](/articles/canadians-visa-esta-cruise-us-port/).

## What it costs
This is a premium region. Small-ship fares, long-haul flights and expensive island prices add up, and the local currency, the CFP franc, can make resort spending a surprise. For a line-by-line budget, see [South Pacific Tahiti cruise cost from Canada](/articles/south-pacific-tahiti-cruise-cost-canada/).

### Ways to keep it sensible
- Choose a line that includes drinks and gratuities, so fewer costs arrive later.
- Snorkel from the ship's watersports platform, where there is one, or at beach stops, rather than booking every lagoon tour.
- Bring your own mask and snorkel; see [bringing snorkel gear vs renting](/articles/bringing-snorkel-gear-vs-renting-port/).
- Use a credit card with no foreign transaction fees.

## Adding a resort stay
Many travellers add a few nights at an overwater bungalow before or after the cruise, given the effort of getting there. Bora Bora and Moorea are the usual choices. Two or three nights on land also helps you recover from the long flight before boarding.

## Who it suits
Tahiti suits couples celebrating a big milestone, snorkellers and anyone who loves water more than sightseeing. It is less suited to travellers who want busy ports, lots of onboard entertainment or a short, cheap trip. If you want the Pacific with an easier journey, compare [Hawaii cruises from Vancouver](/articles/hawaii-cruises-from-vancouver/).

## Before you book
Ask what is included in the fare, whether ports are tendered, and what the cancellation terms are. Buy medical insurance with evacuation cover, as some islands are a long way from major hospitals.
