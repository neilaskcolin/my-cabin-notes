No, and there's no workaround. Cannabis has been legal across Canada since 2018, but every major cruise line bans it on every sailing, including Alaska cruises that start and end in Vancouver. Three sets of rules all point the same way.

## Why cannabis is banned on cruises from Canada
**The ship's flag.** Once a ship leaves Canada Place it operates under the law of its flag state, usually the Bahamas, Panama, Bermuda or Malta, not Canadian law.

**The ports.** Alaska and almost every other itinerary from Canada calls at US ports, where cannabis is still illegal under federal law. Lines apply one rule fleet-wide rather than switching by port.

**The border.** Canadian law makes it illegal to take cannabis out of or into Canada in any form, even between two places where it's legal, and even with a medical document.

## What's banned, and what's not

| Product | Allowed on board? | Legal to carry across the border? |
|---|---|---|
| Dried flower, pre-rolls | No | No |
| Edibles and gummies | No | No |
| Oils, tinctures, capsules | No | No |
| THC vape cartridges | No | No |
| CBD products, any THC level | Usually no | No |
| Medical cannabis with authorization | No | No, without a Health Canada permit |
| Nicotine vapes and cigarettes | Yes, in designated areas | Yes, within duty-free limits |

Nicotine rules, including where you can smoke or vape, are in [smoking and vaping rules on cruise ships](/articles/smoking-vaping-rules-cruise-ships/), with line-specific detail such as the [Royal Caribbean smoking and vaping policy](/articles/royal-caribbean-smoking-vaping-policy/).

## What happens if it's found
Terminal security screens carry-on and checked bags. Cannabis found is confiscated, and the line can refuse boarding or remove a guest mid-cruise at their own expense, with no refund.

The border is the bigger risk. Alaska cruises from Vancouver clear US customs before boarding, at Canada Place. Carrying cannabis there means bringing it into a US federal inspection area on Canadian soil. A US border refusal can follow you on future trips. Coming home, Canadian customs treats cannabis carried back from a US port as an illegal import. Passport and preclearance basics are in [passports for an Alaska cruise from Vancouver](/articles/passport-alaska-cruise-vancouver/).

> **Watch out:** cannabis is legal under Alaska and Washington state law, and you'll pass shops in some ports. Buying in Juneau or Seattle doesn't help: it still can't come back aboard, and bringing it home to Canada is illegal.

## Before you leave home
1. **Check every bag and pocket.** A forgotten pack of gummies in a jacket is the most common way people get caught out.
2. **Clear your vape pen.** Leave THC cartridges and batteries used for cannabis at home.
3. **Check the car if you're driving to Seattle.** Crossing at the Peace Arch or any other border point with cannabis in the vehicle is illegal in both directions. [Driving from Calgary to an Alaska cruise](/articles/drive-from-calgary-to-alaska-cruise/) covers the route.
4. **Talk to your doctor** if you use cannabis for sleep, pain, anxiety or nausea. There may be legal options you can bring; for motion sickness, see [how bad is seasickness, really](/articles/seasickness-how-bad-really/).

## The simple answer
Leave it all at home: flower, edibles, CBD oil and the vape pen. This is one of the very few cruise rules with no exceptions and no line worth calling about it.
