A transatlantic crossing usually costs a Canadian couple C$5,000–10,000 for a repositioning voyage, or C$7,000–14,000 on the Queen Mary 2. Those figures cover the crossing itself, with flights, gratuities and onboard spending. The European holiday that usually follows is a separate budget.

## Two kinds of crossing, two very different bills
| Per couple, inside cabin | Repositioning crossing | Queen Mary 2 liner crossing |
|---|---|---|
| Typical route | Florida to Barcelona, Rome or Southampton, often via the Azores, Madeira or Canaries | New York to Southampton, or the reverse |
| When | April–May eastbound, October–November westbound | Scheduled through much of the year |
| Length | 12–16 nights | About 7 nights |
| Fare | US$1,400–2,800 (C$1,900–3,850) | US$3,000–6,000 (C$4,100–8,300) |
| Flights | Short flight to Florida plus a one-way home from Europe | Short flight to New York plus a one-way home from the UK |
| Ports | Several Atlantic islands and European stops | None, ocean all the way |
| Rough total | C$5,000–10,000 | C$7,000–14,000 |

Fares are at recent prices and vary by season and promotion. Cunard's Queen Mary 2 is the only ship running a regular scheduled liner service across the Atlantic, with formal evenings, a planetarium and a strong tradition. Repositioning crossings are mainstream ships moving between the Caribbean and European seasons, priced to fill.

## Budgeting a repositioning crossing
For a Toronto couple on a 14-night Fort Lauderdale to Barcelona sailing in an inside cabin:

- **Fare with taxes:** about C$1,900–3,850
- **Flight to Florida:** C$300–700 for two
- **One-way flight home from Europe:** C$1,200–2,400 for two
- **Hotel in Florida the night before:** C$250–400
- **Gratuities for 14 nights:** about C$615–770
- **Drinks:** C$300–800
- **Port excursions:** C$200–600
- **Travel insurance:** C$300–700

That lands between roughly C$5,000 and C$10,000, before any time in Europe. Lighter drinkers who walk the ports themselves will sit near the bottom.

> **Quick math:** 14 nights of gratuities at US$16–20 per person per day is US$450–560 for a couple, about C$615–770. On a long crossing that one line can cost more than the flight to Florida.

## Budgeting a Queen Mary 2 crossing
New York to Southampton takes about seven nights. A Montreal couple might fly to New York, spend a night there, sail, then fly home from London. New York hotels are expensive, often C$350–600 a night, and the one-way flight home from London is the second-biggest cost after the fare. Gratuities for a week are about C$300–350.

## Why one-way flights cost more than you'd expect
A one-way ticket from Europe to Canada is sometimes priced close to a return. Try these in order:
1. Search a multi-city ticket with the same airline, for example Toronto to Fort Lauderdale and Barcelona to Toronto.
2. Compare that with a separate one-way on a carrier with genuine one-way pricing.
3. Check whether the cruise line's air program offers a reasonable one-way fare, as discussed in [cruise line air vs booking your own flights](/articles/cruise-line-air-vs-own-flights/).
4. Allow at least a night in the arrival city before flying home, in case disembarkation runs late.

> **Good to know:** sailing eastbound means no checked-bag rush to make a flight on the way out. Pack for both the crossing and your European land days, then fly home from wherever your trip ends.

## Building the Europe half
Most crossings turn into a crossing-plus-Europe holiday. Sail over, spend a week or two on land, and fly home. Priced that way, a two-week crossing and a week in Europe for a couple typically lands around C$8,000–15,000 all-in, with the land portion often the bigger share once hotels, trains and meals are added. The [Mediterranean cruise budget](/articles/mediterranean-cruise-cost-canada/) gives a sense of European costs ashore.

## Who each crossing suits
Repositioning crossings suit budget-minded retirees, sea-day lovers and anyone combining a cruise with a long European trip. The Queen Mary 2 suits travellers who want the liner tradition, dress-up evenings and a shorter, port-free voyage. Read [what a transatlantic crossing is like](/articles/transatlantic-crossings-what-like/) before booking, since a week of ocean is not for everyone, and see [repositioning cruises explained](/articles/repositioning-cruises-explained/) for the wider picture.

## Pricing your crossing
Start with your preferred season and direction, then price the crossing and both flights together. A crossing that looks cheap can become expensive if the flight home is booked late.
