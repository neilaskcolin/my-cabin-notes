Norwegian's kids club, Splash Academy, takes children from 3 to 12 in three age groups, and teens 13 to 17 have their own program called Entourage. Babies and toddlers can join Guppies sessions, but a parent stays with them. Daytime programs are included in your fare.

## Norwegian kids club age groups
| Program | Ages | How it works |
|---|---|---|
| Guppies | 6 months to under 3 | Play sessions; a parent stays |
| Splash Academy: Turtles | 3–5 | Drop-off; toilet-trained |
| Splash Academy: Seals | 6–9 | Drop-off, signed in and out |
| Splash Academy: Dolphins | 10–12 | Drop-off, more independent activities |
| Entourage | 13–17 | Teen lounge and program |

Children are placed by age on the day of sailing. Ask on embarkation day if a child is right on the boundary.

## Babies and toddlers on Norwegian
Guppies is a parent-and-child program, not childcare. Sessions offer toys and play activities for children from 6 months to under 3, with you there throughout. Most ships have no drop-off nursery, which is a real difference from some rival lines.

That suits parents who want to spend the trip with a young child. If you were counting on a few child-free hours with a toddler, Norwegian is the wrong fit. [Cruising with a baby or toddler](/articles/cruising-with-baby-toddler/) covers which lines do offer a nursery.

## Teens: Entourage
Entourage is Norwegian's space for 13 to 17 year olds, separate from Splash Academy, with later hours, games, music and organized activities. Teens usually come and go rather than being signed in, so agree meeting times and a way to message each other in the Norwegian app.

## Hours and the paid late-night option
Splash Academy runs sessions through the day and into the evening, with breaks around lunch and dinner. Times are in the daily program and the app. After standard hours, a paid late-night fun zone typically runs for a per-hour fee, which is what lets parents book a late specialty dinner or a show.

> **Good to know:** freestyle dining helps families on Norwegian. With no fixed dinner time, you can eat at 5:30 pm when the children are hungry, then drop them at Splash Academy for the evening session.

## Registering on embarkation day
- Register children on the first afternoon, or online beforehand where the option is offered
- Note any allergies, medical needs or toilet-training details on the form
- Agree who can collect each child, since only named adults can sign them out
- Set up the Norwegian app so the club staff can reach you

## Is Norwegian right for your children's ages?
Norwegian works best for families with children aged about 5 to 15. The Breakaway Plus and Prima class ships add attractions older children love: go-kart tracks (a fee and height minimums apply), laser tag and aqua parks with big waterslides. Parents of under-3s get less help than on some lines.

### Canadian family timing
March Break fills Norwegian's Florida departures, while July and August are peak season for Alaska from Seattle and Vancouver. For the Alaska side, see [Alaska cruise with kids](/articles/alaska-cruise-with-kids-ages/).

> **Canadian tip:** if one parent is travelling with a child alone, carry a signed consent letter from the other parent. The Government of Canada recommends one for international travel, and it can be asked for at the border or the pier.

## Matching the ship to your children's ages
Youth spaces and attractions vary a lot between Norwegian's newest ships and its older ones. Confirm your ship's facilities and evening hours before booking, and check whether a [kids sail free offer](/articles/kids-sail-free-catch/) is really the better deal once fees are added.
