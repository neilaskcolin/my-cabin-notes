Online check-in is the homework that turns embarkation day into a quick scan and walk-on. It takes about 20 minutes per person if you have everything to hand, and doing it early is the best way to catch a passport problem while there's still time to fix it.

## How to check in online, step by step
1. **Download the line's app** and create an account, or use the website. Link your booking number and every guest in the cabin.
2. **Enter passport details** for each traveller: number, nationality, issue and expiry dates, and the name exactly as printed.
3. **Upload a photo.** A plain, well-lit face shot with no hat or sunglasses. Some lines take it at the terminal instead.
4. **Add an emergency contact** who isn't on the cruise, with a full phone number including the +1.
5. **Register a credit card** for the onboard account, or choose to pay by cash deposit on board. [How cruise cards work](/articles/cruise-cards-how-you-pay-onboard/) explains the account.
6. **Add travel details:** your flight times, or that you're arriving by car or transfer.
7. **Flag anything special:** dietary needs, accessibility, a CPAP, or a birthday or anniversary.
8. **Answer the health questions** if your line asks them online (see [the cruise medical questionnaire](/articles/cruise-ship-medical-form-health-questionnaire/)).
9. **Pick an arrival time** at the terminal, then download or print your boarding pass and luggage tags.

## Which documents do Canadians need?

| Sailing | Main document | Also check |
|---|---|---|
| Alaska from Vancouver | Valid passport | US preclearance at Canada Place |
| Alaska from Seattle | Valid passport | Land or air entry to the US first |
| Caribbean from Florida | Valid passport | Nothing extra for most islands |
| British Isles or Northern Europe | Valid passport | UK ETA; any EU travel authorization in force |
| Asia, South America, Australia | Valid passport | Country visas or e-visas |

A passport is the safe choice for every sailing, even where other documents are sometimes accepted. Many lines recommend at least six months of validity beyond the end of the cruise. [Passports, NEXUS and ID for cruises](/articles/passports-nexus-id-cruise-documents/) explains why a NEXUS card isn't enough, and [ESTA and visas for Canadians at US ports](/articles/canadians-visa-esta-cruise-us-port/) covers US departures.

> **Watch out:** the name on your booking must match your passport. A missing middle name, a married name the passport doesn't show yet, or "Bill" instead of "William" can cause delays at the terminal. Fix it with the line before check-in, not on the day.

## When does online check-in open and close?
Most lines open it 60–90 days before departure, though some open earlier or later. The deadline is usually a few days before sailing, and it's firm. Earlier is better, because terminal arrival times are first come, first served, and the popular late-morning slots go quickly.

### Travelling with children
Each child needs their own passport. If a child is travelling without both parents, such as with grandparents or one parent, the Government of Canada recommends carrying a signed consent letter from the absent parent or parents. Cruise lines may ask for one too.

### Canadian addresses and phone numbers
Some check-in forms default to US formats. Canadian postal codes and provinces normally work, but if a field rejects yours, try removing the space in the postal code, and double-check the province hasn't defaulted to a US state.

## What check-in gives you
Once everyone in the cabin is done, you'll get a boarding pass, often a QR code in the app, an arrival time window and printable luggage tags. Save a screenshot and keep a printed copy as backup, because terminal wifi and phone batteries both fail on busy mornings. [What to do in your first hour aboard](/articles/what-to-do-first-hour-aboard-cruise/) covers what to keep in your hand luggage for boarding day.

## If you miss the deadline
Most lines will still let you check in at the terminal, but expect a longer queue and more forms. Some lines warn that an incomplete check-in can lead to a cancelled booking, so treat the deadline as real.

## Do it the week it opens
Set a reminder for the day check-in opens, gather everyone's passports, and finish in one sitting. You'll get a better arrival time, and any name or passport problem surfaces with weeks to spare.
