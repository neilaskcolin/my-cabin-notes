MSC does not publish a price drop guarantee, but a lower fare found before final payment can often be applied to your booking. The catch is that MSC's fares come bundled with promotions, and switching to the cheaper one can mean giving up something you already had.

## What happens when an MSC fare drops
MSC reprices sailings often, especially on its big Florida ships, where a 7-night World America or Grandiosa sailing can move more than once between booking and final payment. Whether you benefit depends on timing and on how closely the new fare matches yours.

| Situation | What to expect |
|---|---|
| Before final payment, same ship, date, cabin category and experience | Repricing often possible on request |
| Before final payment, but the lower fare is a different experience (Bella vs Fantastica) | Treated as a change; Bella's rules are tighter |
| Lower fare comes with fewer extras than your original promotion | You may lose the extras if you switch |
| After final payment | No refund of the difference |
| Lower price on a European MSC website | Usually not bookable by Canadian residents |

## Why the fare type matters on MSC
MSC sells most cabins under different experiences. Bella is the cheapest and usually means MSC assigns your cabin; Fantastica lets you choose it and comes with a free date change. A Bella fare that drops is still worth asking about, but there is less room to move it around. The general trade-off is covered in [cruise fare classes explained](/articles/cruise-fare-classes-refundable/).

> **Watch out:** many MSC promotions bundle extras such as onboard credit, reduced deposits or kids-sail-free pricing. A lower "new" fare may not include them. Add up what you would lose before you accept the reprice.

## Canadian bookings and currency
MSC prices Canadian bookings in Canadian dollars. A drop on the US site, quoted in US dollars, is not necessarily a drop for you, because MSC's CAD and USD prices are set separately and may not track the day's exchange rate. Compare your CAD fare with the current CAD fare for the same cabin. [Paying in CAD vs USD for a cruise](/articles/paying-cad-vs-usd-cruise/) explains why the two can move separately.

## How to claim an MSC price drop, step by step
1. **Note your final payment date.** For most 5–14 night sailings it is about 90 days out; repricing is realistic only before then.
2. **Track your exact sailing.** Watch the same ship, date, cabin category and experience in Canadian dollars. [Setting cruise price alerts](/articles/how-to-set-cruise-price-alerts/) shows how.
3. **Screenshot the lower fare** with the date and time showing, because MSC fares can change within hours.
4. **List what your current booking includes**, such as onboard credit, a promotion code or a cabin you chose.
5. **Ask whoever holds your booking** to compare the two and apply the lower fare if you come out ahead.
6. **Check the revised invoice**, especially that your cabin number and any prepaid gratuities or packages are still attached.

## When MSC prices tend to move
Wave season, from January to March, is when most lines, MSC included, run their biggest promotions, so Canadians booking Caribbean sailings for the next winter should watch fares closely then. Prices can also fall closer to sailing on short Bahamas cruises with unsold cabins, though by then you are usually past final payment. [Wave season explained](/articles/wave-season-explained-canada/) has more on timing.

## Choosing the right MSC fare from the start
Repricing is easier when you have room to move. A Fantastica fare with a cabin you chose, booked well before final payment, gives you the most flexibility if MSC's price drops. Ask about it when you book, not after.
