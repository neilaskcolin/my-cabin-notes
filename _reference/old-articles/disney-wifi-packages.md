Disney's onboard internet, called Connect@Sea, costs about US$30 per device per day for the standard plan and about US$49 for premium streaming, at prices raised in January 2026. It is not included in the fare. For many families the free Navigator app chat is enough.

## Disney's Connect@Sea plans
| Plan | Good for | Approx. price (USD) | Roughly in CAD |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard Internet | Social media, messaging, email, video calls | about $30 per day | C$40–42 |
| Premium Surfing and Streaming | Everything above plus Netflix, Disney+ and music streaming | about $49 per day | C$66–69 |
| Navigator app only | Onboard chat, schedules, menus | Free | Free |

Each plan covers one device at a time. You can switch a plan between devices, but only one can be online. Disney has not offered discounts for several devices in the same stateroom, so a connected family pays per device.

> **Quick math:** two teens on the standard plan for a 7-night sailing would cost about US$420 (2 × US$30 × 7), or roughly C$565–590. One shared plan that they take turns on costs half that.

## Should you buy Disney wifi before you sail?
Unlike some lines, Disney has not made pre-cruise wifi discounts a regular feature. Most guests buy on board through the Navigator app, often on the first afternoon. It is still worth checking the add-ons in your online reservation before you sail in case an offer appears.

The advantage of buying on board is that you can wait. Try the free app chat for a day, see who really needs to be online, and buy only for them.

## What you can do free with the Navigator app
The Disney Cruise Line Navigator app connects to the ship's network without a plan. It lets you message others in your party, check the day's schedule, see menus and character greeting times, and hear from the kids clubs. Download it and set up chat at home over your own wifi, as [using the cruise line app](/articles/using-cruise-line-app-before-during-trip/) explains.

## How to choose, step by step
1. Set up the Navigator app chat for everyone before you leave home.
2. Decide who must be reachable from home, such as a parent with work or elderly relatives.
3. Give that person the standard plan; it handles calls and messaging.
4. Buy premium only if someone has to stream, or work with large files.
5. Let everyone else use the app chat and catch up on free wifi in port.

## How fast is it?
Speeds are usually fine for messaging and video calls, with slower spells at busy times when thousands of guests are online. On an Alaska sailing from Vancouver, steep fjord walls near the glaciers can briefly interrupt satellite signal. The technology is explained in [how cruise ship internet works](/articles/cruise-ship-internet-how-it-works/).

> **Canadian tip:** your Canadian carrier's US roaming add-on generally works in Ketchikan, Juneau and Skagway. At sea, a phone can quietly switch to the ship's expensive marine network, and Castaway Cay, Lookout Cay and Nassau are Bahamian, not US, coverage. Use airplane mode with wifi on unless you have checked your plan. See [cruise wifi and roaming for Canadians](/articles/cruise-wifi-phone-roaming-canadians/).

## How Disney compares
| Line | System | Recent price per device per day | Included with |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Disney** | Connect@Sea | $30–49 USD | Not included; app messaging is free |
| [Royal Caribbean](/articles/royal-caribbean-wifi-packages/) | VOOM | $20–30 USD | Some promotions; some suites |
| [Carnival](/articles/carnival-wifi-packages/) | Hub plans | $20–30 USD | Occasional sales and bundles |

Disney's wifi is the priciest of the three at recent rates and is rarely bundled, while Royal Caribbean includes it in some promotions and suites. Disney's free app messaging softens that for families who mostly need to find each other on board. ## Keeping the wifi bill in check
Start with the free app, buy one standard plan for the person who must stay reachable, and add more only once you are on board and know you need it. Onboard credit can also go towards it, as [onboard credit explained](/articles/onboard-credit-explained/) shows.
