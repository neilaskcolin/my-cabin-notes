CHEERS!, Carnival's drink package, has recently cost about US$84 per person per day bought before sailing, once the 20% service charge is added. It pays off if you'll have roughly six alcoholic drinks a day, every day, and it rarely pays off on short port-heavy cruises or for light drinkers.

## What CHEERS! costs per day
At recent pricing, CHEERS! is US$69.95 per person per day bought before you sail, or US$74.95 on board, plus a 20% service charge. That makes it about US$84 pre-cruise or US$90 on board, roughly C$115–125 a day per person.

Carnival has said that from December 1, 2026, drink package and wifi prices will vary by sailing length, itinerary and season instead of being one fleet-wide price. Expect higher figures on holiday sailings, and check the price shown for your specific booking.

> **Quick math:** a couple from Toronto on a 7-night Eastern Caribbean sailing from Port Canaveral, both buying before the cruise: US$84 × 2 × 7 = about US$1,175, roughly C$1,585–1,645. On a 4-night Bahamas run, the same couple pays about US$670, roughly C$905–940.

## What's included, and what isn't
CHEERS! covers cocktails, spirits, beer and wine by the glass with a menu price up to US$20, plus sodas, juices, specialty coffees, tea, energy drinks and 500 ml bottled water. Drinks over US$20 and bottles of wine get 25% off instead.

It does not cover room service, the minibar, souvenir glasses, self-serve wine machines or bars ashore at Half Moon Cay and Princess Cays. Alcoholic drinks are capped at 15 per day, counted from 6 a.m. to 6 a.m., and non-alcoholic drinks are unlimited.

### The Texas and New York quirk
On sailings from Galveston or New York, the package starts at 6 a.m. on day two and you aren't charged for embarkation day. That matters for Canadians driving down from Ontario or Quebec to New York: budget for paying per drink on the first evening.

## Is the Carnival drink package worth it?
Pay-as-you-go cocktails on Carnival run roughly US$11–15 once the 20% charge is added, and beer and wine a little less. Here's how typical days stack up against a pre-cruise package at about US$84.

| Typical day | Rough pay-as-you-go cost | Better choice |
|---|---|---|
| 2 cocktails, 1 coffee | US$30–40 | Pay as you go |
| 4 drinks, 2 coffees, 1 soda | US$60–75 | Usually pay as you go |
| 6 drinks, 2 coffees, water | US$85–105 | CHEERS! |
| Sea day by the pool, 8+ drinks | US$110+ | CHEERS! |

The maths changes with your itinerary. A 3- or 4-night cruise with a port day and a Half Moon Cay stop rarely justifies it. An 8-night sailing from Baltimore with several sea days often does, for steady drinkers.

## How to decide in five minutes
1. Count the sea days on your itinerary; that's where packages earn their money.
2. Estimate honest drinks per day, including coffees and bottled water.
3. Price it for every adult 21+ in the cabin, since all must buy.
4. Check whether you leave from Galveston or New York, where day one is excluded.
5. If you buy, do it before sailing. Pre-cruise purchases can be refunded until 9 p.m. Eastern the evening before departure.

## Cheaper ways to drink on Carnival
- **CHEERS! Zero Proof:** about US$30 a day pre-cruise plus the service charge, with mocktails, specialty coffees and alcohol-free beer.
- **Bottomless Bubbles:** soda and juice for about US$12 a day for adults and US$7 for kids, plus the charge.
- **Your wine allowance:** one bottle per adult, covered in [Carnival alcohol policy](/articles/carnival-alcohol-policy/).
- **Bundles:** Carnival has sold limited-time FunPacks, the top tier of which wrapped CHEERS!, premium wifi and some specialty dining into one daily price.

## How Carnival compares
| Line | Package | Recent price per person per day | Catch |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Carnival** | Cheers! | $70–75 USD + 20% | 15 alcoholic drinks a day |
| [Royal Caribbean](/articles/royal-caribbean-drink-package-price/) | Deluxe Beverage | $70–105 USD + 18% | All adults in cabin must buy |
| [MSC](/articles/msc-drink-package-price/) | Premium Extra | About $85–100 USD incl. 18% | Only alcohol package on North American sailings; 15-drink daily cap |

CHEERS! sits at the lower end of Royal Caribbean's range but now carries a higher 20% service charge, and its 15-drink cap is the rule that sets it apart. MSC's only alcohol package in North America, Premium Extra, now costs a little more, at about US$85–100 with its 18% charge included. ## Getting the package bundled instead
Some Carnival promotions and bundles fold CHEERS! into the price. For a couple who would buy it anyway, that's worth over C$1,000 on a week-long cruise, so ask what's available for your dates before paying separately.
