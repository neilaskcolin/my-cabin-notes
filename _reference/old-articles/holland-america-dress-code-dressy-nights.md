Holland America is smart casual most evenings, with a set number of "Dressy" nights that depends on how long you sail. Dressy means a step up, a jacket or a collared shirt with dress trousers, or a dress or smart separates, not black tie. The line renamed "formal" to "dressy" partly to make that clear.

## Smart casual: most evenings on Holland America
Most nights, the main dining room and specialty restaurants such as the Pinnacle Grill run on smart casual. Think collared shirts, blouses, trousers, skirts, dresses and neat jeans.

In the table-service restaurants, Holland America does not allow:
- shorts
- swimwear and beach cover-ups
- distressed jeans
- men's tank tops

By day, casual clothes are fine everywhere, as long as you wear a shirt or cover-up and shoes indoors. For ideas, see [what to wear during the day on a cruise](/articles/cruise-ship-dress-code-daytime-wear/).

## How many dressy nights on a Holland America cruise?
The count scales with cruise length.

| Cruise length | Dressy nights (recent policy) |
|---|---|
| 4–6 nights | 1 |
| 7–13 nights | 2 |
| 14–20 nights | 3 |
| Grand Voyages | Varies with the itinerary |

On a typical 7-night cruise, dressy nights usually fall on the first full day and the second-to-last evening. They are never on embarkation night or the final night, and the ship can move them, so check the daily program or the Navigator app.

## What "dressy" means on Holland America
### For men
A jacket earns its place here more than on mass-market lines, though it is not compulsory. A collared shirt with dress trousers and proper shoes is the practical minimum. Suits and tuxedos appear but are a minority.

### For women
A cocktail dress, a smart jumpsuit or dressy separates. Long gowns show up on longer voyages but are entirely optional.

Holland America does not rent formal wear, so whatever you want to wear has to come in your suitcase. The bigger picture is in [formal nights on cruises: what you actually wear](/articles/formal-nights-what-to-wear/).

## Skipping dressy night
The Lido Market buffet stays casual every evening, and room service is another option. Nobody checks a dress code at the buffet, which makes it the easy alternative for anyone who would rather not change after a long port day.

## Alaska and long voyages
Alaska sailings from Vancouver and Seattle are noticeably more relaxed than the line's longer voyages. Most people wear fleece and waterproofs by day and change into one smart outfit for dinner, as the [Alaska cruise dress code](/articles/alaska-cruise-dress-code/) page describes.

Grand Voyages and long Panama Canal or transatlantic sailings are where Holland America's traditional streak shows most, with more dressy nights and a guest mix that enjoys them.

> **Example:** a couple from Winnipeg on a 7-night Alaska sailing could pack one blazer, two collared shirts, dress trousers, a dress and a smart cardigan. That covers both dressy nights and every smart casual evening in a single carry-on's worth of space.

> **Canadian tip:** on a basic WestJet or Air Canada fare without a checked bag, wear the blazer on the plane. It saves space and creases less than folding it.
