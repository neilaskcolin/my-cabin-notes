Norwegian charges gratuities in two layers, and most first-timers only expect one. The daily service charge is roughly US$20 per person in standard cabins and about US$25 in The Haven at recent rates. On top of that, the Free at Sea perks carry their own service charges.

## How much is the Norwegian service charge per day?
The daily charge applies to every guest in the cabin for every night of the cruise. The higher rate covers The Haven, Norwegian's suite complex with butler and concierge service, and at recent policy Concierge-level suites as well. Norwegian revises these figures from time to time, so confirm the current amount for your sailing.

It is posted to your onboard account each day unless you prepay. Like most lines, Norwegian pools it across the crew who serve you, from stateroom stewards and dining staff to galley and support teams. The background is in [cruise gratuities explained for Canadians](/articles/cruise-gratuities-canadians/).

## The second layer: service charges on Free at Sea
Free at Sea bundles extras such as the Unlimited Open Bar package, specialty dining, wifi and shore excursion credit into many Norwegian fares. The perks are free; the gratuities on them are not. A service charge of roughly 20% of each perk's retail value is added to your booking.

On a couple's drinks-and-dining bundle that can come to US$250–350 for a week. It surprises many Norwegian first-timers because it sits on the invoice, not in the advertised fare. The [Norwegian drink package price](/articles/norwegian-drink-package-price/) page shows how the drinks part is worked out.

## What a week of NCL gratuities adds up to
| Charge, 7 nights | Approx. USD | Roughly in CAD |
|---|---|---|
| Daily service charge, couple, standard cabin | $280 | C$380–390 |
| Daily service charge, couple, The Haven | $350 | C$475–490 |
| Daily service charge, family of four, standard cabin | $560 | C$755–785 |
| Free at Sea perk charges, couple (drinks and dining) | $250–350 | C$340–490 |

> **Example:** a Winnipeg couple on a 7-night Seattle–Alaska sailing in a balcony cabin, with drinks and dining through Free at Sea, would pay about US$280 in daily charges plus US$250–350 in perk charges. That is roughly US$530–630, or C$715–880, before a single extra drink.

## Should Canadians prepay NCL's service charge?
Usually, yes. Prepaying through your booking locks in today's rate if Norwegian raises it before you sail, and on a booking priced in Canadian dollars it fixes the exchange too. Left until the cruise, the charge posts in US dollars and converts at your card's rate, often with a 2.5% foreign-transaction fee on Canadian cards.

1. Check the current daily rate for your cabin type and multiply by guests and nights.
2. Ask for prepaid service charges to be added to your reservation before final payment, which on Norwegian falls about 120 days out.
3. Confirm the Free at Sea perk charges are on the invoice too, so nothing surprises you later.
4. On board, check the Norwegian app shows the service charge as prepaid.

[Paying in CAD vs USD for a cruise](/articles/paying-cad-vs-usd-cruise/) explains the exchange side in more detail.

## The 20% on everything else
Norwegian adds a 20% service charge to bar drinks bought outside a package, specialty dining not covered by Free at Sea, and spa treatments. A US$14 cocktail is really about US$16.80. Porters at the terminal and shore excursion guides are tipped in cash by convention, so bring some small US bills.

> **Watch out:** Free at Sea perk charges are billed with your booking, not on board, so they won't show in the onboard account you check on the last night.

## Adjusting the service charge on board
Guest Services can reduce the daily charge for a specific, documented service problem. Removing it entirely is discouraged, because it forms a real part of crew pay. If one person made your trip, name them in a note too.
