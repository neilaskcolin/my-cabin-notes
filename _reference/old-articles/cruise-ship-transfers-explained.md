A transfer is simply the ride between the airport, or your hotel, and the ship. You can buy it from the cruise line as a coach seat, or sort it yourself by taxi, rideshare, transit or hotel shuttle. At most ports Canadians use, your own ride is cheaper for two or more people.

## Transfer costs at the ports Canadians use most
Rough one-way costs at recent prices. The taxi or rideshare figure is for the whole car; the line's transfer is per person.

| Port | Airport | Drive time | Taxi or rideshare | Other options |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Vancouver, Canada Place | YVR | 25–40 min | About C$35–45 | Canada Line SkyTrain, about C$11–12 each |
| Seattle, Piers 66 and 91 | SEA | 30–45 min | About US$50–75 | Light rail to downtown, then a short ride |
| Fort Lauderdale, Port Everglades | FLL | 10–15 min | About US$20–30 | Hotel shuttles |
| Miami, PortMiami | MIA | 20–30 min | About US$30–45 | Hotel shuttles |
| Port Canaveral | MCO (Orlando) | 45–60 min | About US$70–110 | Shared shuttle companies, line coaches |

Port Canaveral is the outlier: it is far enough from Orlando's airport that the line's coach or a shared shuttle often beats a private car for a couple.

## The cruise line's transfer
Coaches meet flights the line knows about and take you straight to the terminal. Your checked bags are handled, and there is nothing to arrange. The downsides are waiting for the coach to fill, a fixed schedule, and a price per person that adds up for a family of four.

Its real advantage is accountability. The line knows exactly where its coaches are and is far more likely to hold boarding for a delayed one than for a passenger in a taxi.

## Taxis, rideshares, transit and hotel shuttles
At Fort Lauderdale and Miami, a taxi is cheap and quick. In Vancouver, the SkyTrain is cheaper still and drops you a short walk from the terminal; the full step-by-step is in [Calgary to the Vancouver cruise terminal](/articles/calgary-to-vancouver-cruise-terminal/), and every Vancouver option is compared in [YYC to Canada Place transfer options](/articles/yyc-to-canada-place-transfer-options/).

Many hotels near cruise ports run free or cheap shuttles on sailing mornings. If you have flown in the day before, as recommended in [flying the day before your cruise](/articles/fly-day-before-cruise/), a hotel shuttle is often the cheapest transfer of all.

> **Canadian tip:** flights from Calgary and Edmonton to Seattle clear US preclearance before departure, so you land as a domestic passenger and walk straight out to ground transport. Allow for that queue at YYC or YEG, not at SEA.

## When the line's transfer is worth paying for
- You are flying in on sailing day, despite the risks.
- You are travelling alone with heavy luggage, so there is no one to split a taxi with.
- The port is far from the airport, as at Port Canaveral.
- You are at an unfamiliar foreign port where the taxi system is uncertain.
- You booked cruise line air, which often bundles transfers (see [cruise line air vs your own flights](/articles/cruise-line-air-vs-own-flights/)).

## After the cruise: getting back to the airport
Disembarkation usually starts early in the morning and finishes by mid-morning, with self-carry passengers off first. Book domestic flights for around noon or later. After an Alaska round trip, you clear Canadian customs at Canada Place before leaving the terminal, and if you are connecting to a US flight at YVR, allow extra time for US preclearance at the airport. How the morning works is covered in [disembarkation morning](/articles/disembarkation-morning-getting-off-ship/).

Florida ports are busy on turnaround days, with several ships unloading at once. For Toronto-specific timing, see [Toronto to Fort Lauderdale](/articles/toronto-fort-lauderdale-cruise-flights/) and [Toronto to Miami](/articles/toronto-miami-caribbean-cruise/).

## Sorting transfers with the rest of the trip
Once your flights are set, the transfer is a quick decision: the line's coach if you want guaranteed accountability, your own ride if you want speed and savings. Line transfers can usually be added at booking or later, and they need your flight details.
