If Alaska is the trip, Princess is the line most Canadians should look at first. It has sailed the route for decades, owns its own lodges and rail cars for land tours, and runs frequent sailings from Vancouver. Outside Alaska it is a comfortable, middle-of-the-road line that suits couples and mixed-age groups better than thrill-seekers.

## Why is Princess so strong in Alaska?
Princess has built up infrastructure in Alaska that most rivals do not have. It owns wilderness lodges near Denali and Fairbanks, and runs glass-domed rail cars on the Alaska Railroad for its cruisetours, which combine a cruise with several nights on land. [Alaska cruisetours explained](/articles/alaska-cruisetours-denali-rail/) covers how those packages work.

Itineraries are a strength too. Princess sells Inside Passage round trips from Vancouver and Seattle as well as one-way sailings between Vancouver and Whittier, its Gulf of Alaska port near Anchorage. Glacier Bay National Park appears on many of its sailings. If you are weighing the route, [round-trip Vancouver vs one-way to Anchorage](/articles/roundtrip-vs-oneway-alaska/) sets out the trade-offs.

On board, Princess's Alaska programme brings local guides, naturalists and cultural presenters onto the ship, so sea days feel connected to the places you are passing.

Guest numbers are approximate. Which ships sail from Vancouver changes year to year, so check your season.

### Beyond Alaska
In winter Princess sails the Caribbean from Fort Lauderdale, with calls at Princess Cays, its private beach in the Bahamas. The smaller Coral-class ships run full [Panama Canal transits](/articles/panama-canal-cruise-couples/), and Princess keeps a ship based in Japan for much of the year, which suits Canadians flying from Vancouver.

## MedallionClass and Princess Plus
Every guest gets a free Medallion, a small disc worn on a lanyard or wristband. It unlocks your cabin, pays for drinks and lets you order food and drinks to wherever you are sitting through the OceanNow service. It also tends to speed up boarding.

Princess also sells bundled fares. Princess Plus adds a drinks package, MedallionNet wifi and crew appreciation, and Princess Premier adds more on top, such as a higher drink limit and extra wifi devices. For a couple who would buy drinks and wifi anyway, these bundles often beat buying the pieces. See the [Princess drink package price](/articles/princess-drink-package-price/) for the maths.

## What the onboard atmosphere is like
### Dining and evenings
Expect reliable main dining rooms with set or flexible dining times, plus specialty restaurants such as Crown Grill and Sabatini's. Evenings centre on the Piazza, the atrium with live music, and Movies Under the Stars on the top deck.

### Pace and crowd
The guest mix leans towards couples over 50, with families on summer and school-holiday sailings. There are no headline thrill rides. Adults who want a quieter corner can pay for The Sanctuary, an adults-only deck retreat on many ships. Repeat guests earn status in the Captain's Circle loyalty programme.

## Who Princess suits, and who it doesn't
Princess is a good fit for:
- First-time Alaska cruisers, especially from B.C. and Alberta
- Couples over 50 who want comfort without formality
- Multi-generation groups, since the ships cater to every age without catering to extremes
- Anyone who wants Denali on land as well as glaciers at sea

You may prefer another line if you want:
- Waterslides, surf simulators or the biggest possible ship
- A design-forward, younger-feeling ship, which Celebrity does better
- An adults-only ship
- The cheapest possible mainstream fare

For a wider view, see [best cruise lines for couples over 50](/articles/best-cruise-lines-couples-over-50/).

## The Canadian angle
Princess quotes fares in Canadian dollars to Canadian residents, but everything on board is charged in US dollars. Gratuities, drinks and wifi are the three biggest extras, and Princess Plus or prepaid crew appreciation locks them in CAD before you sail.

> **Canadian tip:** cruisetours with Denali lodge nights sell out first for July and August. If you want one, book 12 to 18 months out on a refundable deposit and reprice if a sale comes along.

For British Columbians, a Vancouver round trip means no flight at all. Albertans can fly or drive, as covered in [cruising from Vancouver without flying](/articles/cruise-from-vancouver-without-flying/).
