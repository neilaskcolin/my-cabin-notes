For most cruise trips from Calgary, the cheapest sensible choice is an economy or off-site lot with a shuttle, booked online. The number that drives the cost is days parked, and that's always more than your cruise nights.

## How many days will you actually park?
If you fly to Vancouver or Florida the day before you sail, which you should, your car sits at YYC from the day you fly out until the day you fly home. On a 7-night cruise that's nine calendar days. On a 10-night sailing it's twelve.

> **Quick math:** 9 days at C$18 a day in an economy lot is C$162. The same 9 days in the terminal parkade at C$32 a day is C$288. That C$126 difference covers a good dinner in Vancouver the night before you sail.

## YYC parking options compared
Rates below are rough, at recent prices, and change by season and demand. Confirm current rates when you book.

| Option | Rough daily rate | Getting to the terminal | 9-day total | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Terminal parkade | C$25–40 | Walk across | C$225–360 | Winter trips, mobility needs, short stays |
| Economy lot on airport land | C$15–25 | Short shuttle | C$135–225 | Most cruise trips |
| Off-site private lot | C$15–25 | Shuttle, 5–15 minutes | C$135–225 | Price hunters who book ahead |
| Hotel park-sleep-fly package | Varies | Hotel shuttle | Often similar to economy, with a hotel night included | Early-morning flights |

Off-site lots and economy parking often come out about the same. The difference shows up in shuttle frequency and in whether the lot is covered or fenced, so read recent reviews before you choose.

## Parking vs taxi, rideshare, transit or a lift
- **Taxi or rideshare:** roughly C$40–70 each way depending on where you live in the city, so C$80–140 for the return trip. That beats parking for a solo traveller on a long trip, and it's about even for a couple on a week-long cruise.
- **Calgary Transit:** the Route 300 bus runs between downtown and the airport for a regular transit fare. It's cheap, but awkward with big suitcases.
- **A lift from family or friends:** free, and the classic Calgary solution. Offer to bring back a bottle of duty-free.
- **Park-sleep-fly:** some airport hotels bundle one night's stay with parking for the length of your trip. It's handy if your flight leaves at 6 a.m. and you live in Okotoks, Airdrie or Cochrane.

## Winter trips vs summer trips
For an Alaska sailing in July, an open economy lot is fine. For a Caribbean cruise in January or February, it's worth thinking about what you'll come home to: a car under 20 cm of snow at midnight, in -25 weather.

> **Canadian tip:** if you park, leave your winter coats, boots and toques in the car. That's one less bulky layer to haul to Florida, and everything's waiting when you land. A covered parkade spot also means no digging out after a February return.

Keep a brush and scraper in the car, and make sure the battery is healthy before a two-week absence in deep cold.

## How to book parking well
### Book when you book flights
Most cruises are booked months ahead, so there's rarely a reason to pay the drive-up rate. Prebooking also guarantees a space over busy weeks like March break and Christmas.

### Pad the return
Reserve until the day after your scheduled flight home if the lot allows free changes. Flight delays happen, and some lots charge more for overstays than for the original booking.

### Record where you parked
Take a photo of the row sign and save the booking confirmation in your phone. Two weeks at sea is plenty of time to forget which lot you used.
