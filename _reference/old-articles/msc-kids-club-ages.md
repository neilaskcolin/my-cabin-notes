MSC splits its youth programme into five age bands, from a Baby Club for under-3s to a Teen Club for 15–17s. Drop-off clubs start at age 3 and are included in the fare, and MSC is one of the few big lines with a paid drop-off option for babies.

## MSC kids club age groups
| Club | Ages | How it works | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Baby Club | Under 3 | Baby Time: parent stays and plays; Baby Care: drop-off from 6 months | Baby Time free; Baby Care charged |
| Mini Club | 3–6 | Drop-off, with LEGO-branded play | Included |
| Junior Club | 7–11 | Drop-off, games, crafts and shows | Included |
| Young Club | 12–14 | Own space, sports, gaming, evening events | Included |
| Teen Club | 15–17 | Own space and later hours, discos and karaoke | Included |

Children usually go by their age on the day of sailing. Ask about moving up or down a band for siblings close in age; it is at the youth staff's discretion.

## What MSC offers babies and toddlers
MSC's Baby Club areas on many ships are equipped by Chicco, the Italian baby brand, which fits MSC's Italian roots. There are two ways to use them.

### Baby Time
Set daily slots when parents bring their child in to play with the toys and equipment. Free, and handy on sea days.

### Baby Care
A drop-off service for babies over 6 months, run by youth staff in set slots for up to eight hours a day. Places are limited, and charges apply. Book on embarkation day, especially on busy holiday sailings.

> **Good to know:** a toddler who isn't toilet-trained typically can't join Mini Club at 3, so Baby Care is the only drop-off option until they are. Read [cruising with a baby or toddler](/articles/cruising-with-baby-toddler/) before you pick a sailing.

## Hours and evenings
MSC's clubs open daily, with sessions from morning into the evening and a break for family meals. Times change by ship and day, so check the MSC app on board. The Teen Club runs the latest, with evening discos and karaoke. If you want a late adults' dinner, ask whether late-evening sessions run on your ship and whether they carry a charge. In-cabin babysitting is a different matter on most lines, explained in [cruise ship babysitting](/articles/cruise-ship-babysitting-in-cabin-service/).

## What makes MSC's clubs different
- **Multilingual groups.** MSC's passengers come from many countries, so staff run activities in several languages. Children often meet kids from Europe and Latin America, even on a Miami sailing.
- **Big promotions for families.** MSC often runs offers where children sail free or at reduced fares, though the daily service charge still applies to every child aged 2 and over.
- **Ocean Cay days.** Most of MSC's Florida itineraries include its private island, where families usually spend the day on the beach together; check whether the club runs on board that day.
- **A minors drink package.** Guests under 21 can't share a parent's alcohol package; the minors package covers sodas and juices.

## Busy weeks for Canadian families
March Break and the Christmas holidays are peak weeks on MSC's Florida sailings, and clubs are fullest then. Families from Ontario and Quebec often share the ship with US spring breakers in March. ## How MSC compares

## Matching the ship to your children's ages
Club spaces and Baby Care availability vary across MSC's fleet, from the World and Meraviglia classes to older ships. Confirm what your ship offers for each child's age before you book, especially if you have a baby.
