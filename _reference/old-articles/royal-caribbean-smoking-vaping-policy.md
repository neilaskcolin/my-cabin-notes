On Royal Caribbean you can smoke or vape only in signed outdoor smoking areas and, on many ships, part of the casino. Cabins and balconies are off limits everywhere in the fleet, and the cleaning fee for breaking that rule runs to several hundred dollars.

## Where can you smoke on Royal Caribbean?
Each ship has one or more designated outdoor smoking areas, usually a section of an open deck, often on one side of the pool deck or near an outdoor bar. The locations are listed in the Cruise Compass in the Royal app and marked with signs. Don't assume any open deck qualifies.

On the Oasis- and Icon-class ships, the smoking area can be a long walk from a cabin at the far end of the ship. If you smoke, look at your ship's deck plan before choosing a cabin; one close to the lifts on the smoking side saves a lot of walking over a week. [How to read a deck plan](/articles/reading-cruise-ship-deck-plans/) shows how.

### Casino Royale
On many ships, a designated section of Casino Royale allows smoking for guests actively playing. Some ships and itineraries have tightened this, so check the Cruise Compass on board. Non-smokers who mind the smell can usually find the casino's smoke-free side.

## Where smoking and vaping are banned
- All staterooms and all balconies, fleet-wide, with no exceptions
- Restaurants, bars, theatres, lounges and other indoor public spaces
- Pool decks outside the designated zone, and children's and teen areas
- Outside the marked areas at Perfect Day at CocoCay

The balcony ban is a fire-safety rule shared across the industry, as explained in [smoking and vaping rules on cruise ships](/articles/smoking-vaping-rules-cruise-ships/).

## Vaping on Royal Caribbean
E-cigarettes and vapes are treated exactly like cigarettes: designated outdoor areas only, and the same cleaning fee if used in a cabin or on a balcony. Pack vapes and spare batteries in your carry-on, since lithium batteries shouldn't go in checked bags.

> **Watch out:** crew notice balcony smoking quickly, because smoke drifts to neighbouring balconies and those guests complain. The fee lands on your SeaPass account, and a second offence can end your cruise early.

> **Canadian tip:** cannabis is legal in Canada but banned on Royal Caribbean ships, including medical cannabis and sailings from Vancouver, because ships and US ports fall under US federal law. Leave it at home; [why the answer is no](/articles/cruise-cannabis-canada-why-no/) explains the risk at the border too.

## How Royal Caribbean compares
| Line | Smoking in the casino | Cabins and balconies |
|---|---|---|
| **Royal Caribbean** | Designated section on many ships | Banned |
| [Carnival](/articles/carnival-smoking-vaping-policy/) | Designated section on most ships | Banned |
| [Norwegian](/articles/norwegian-smoking-vaping-policy/) | Designated section on most ships; tighter on newer ships | Banned |

Carnival and Norwegian are the lines most often weighed against Royal Caribbean. Cabins and balconies are smoke-free on all three, so the real difference is the casino and how far you'll walk to the outdoor area. On Royal Caribbean's largest ships, the walk is the bigger factor. ## Knowing the map before you sail
Smoking areas and casino rules vary more by ship than by line. If they matter to your trip, check the deck plan and recent guidance for your specific ship before you choose a cabin, and look up the locations in the Royal app on your first afternoon.
