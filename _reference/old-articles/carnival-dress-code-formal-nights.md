Carnival has only two evening dress codes, and both are relaxed. Cruise casual covers most nights, including jeans and tidy shorts, and cruise elegant is Carnival's softer take on formal night: one evening on cruises up to 5 nights, two on longer sailings.

## Cruise casual: what most Carnival nights look like
Cruise casual starts at dinner and is basically what you'd wear to a nice chain restaurant at home. In the main dining room that means:

- jeans, casual trousers, capris or skirts
- tidy dress shorts (gym and basketball shorts are out)
- polo shirts, collared shirts, blouses, sundresses or neat T-shirts
- sandals are fine; flip-flops and swimwear aren't
- no baseball caps or men's sleeveless shirts at the table

Embarkation night is the most casual of all, because many guests are still waiting for their checked bags.

## Cruise elegant: Carnival's version of formal night
On a 3-, 4- or 5-night sailing from Miami or Port Canaveral you'll get one cruise elegant night. On 6 nights or more you'll get two, usually on sea days. The HUB app's daily schedule confirms which nights.

For men that means dress trousers and a dress shirt. A sport coat or tie is optional, and you'll see a few suits and tuxedos but they are the minority. For women, a cocktail dress, dressy separates or a pantsuit works, and evening gowns appear on some tables. Jeans, shorts, T-shirts and caps aren't allowed in the dining room that night.

> **Good to know:** elegant night is when the ship's photographers set up backdrops around the atrium, so it's also the evening most families get their dressed-up photo taken.

## Where the dress code applies, venue by venue
| Venue | Cruise casual night | Cruise elegant night |
|---|---|---|
| Main dining room | Jeans and dress shorts fine | Dress trousers, collared shirt or dress |
| Fahrenheit 555 steakhouse | Smart casual, no shorts | Elegant |
| Lido Marketplace buffet, Guy's Burger Joint, BlueIguana Cantina | Casual, cover up swimwear | Casual |
| Bars, theatre, Punchliner Comedy Club | No set code | No set code; most people stay dressed |

### The buffet escape hatch
If packing a dress shirt feels like a chore, eat at the Lido on elegant night. Plenty of guests do, especially families with small children or anyone coming back late from a long port day.

## What to pack for a 7-night Carnival cruise
One step-up outfit per person covers both elegant nights; nobody tracks repeats. Add a light sweater or wrap, because dining rooms and theatres run cold. The rest of the suitcase can be genuinely casual. [Formal nights on cruises](/articles/formal-nights-what-to-wear/) has the general formula and [daytime dress codes](/articles/cruise-ship-dress-code-daytime-wear/) covers the rest of the day.

On Carnival's Alaska sailings from Seattle, elegant night looks the same but daytime packing doesn't; [what people actually wear in Alaska](/articles/alaska-cruise-dress-code/) covers the layers.

> **Canadian tip:** flying to Florida from Winnipeg or Calgary in February means a parka you won't need for a week. Leave it in the car at a park-and-fly lot, or wear a packable down jacket that squashes into your carry-on, so the elegant outfit has room in the suitcase.

## Themed nights and deck parties
Many Carnival sailings add a themed deck party, often built around a colour or a music era. They're optional, they're listed in the HUB app's daily schedule, and a single themed T-shirt is all most people bring.
