faq1q: What time should you actually arrive for cruise embarkation?
faq1a: Arrive within your assigned check-in window, but not right at its start, when the early crowd lands at once. The middle of the window, often late morning to early afternoon, usually moves faster, but never arrive later than the line's cut-off.
faq2q: Can you skip the muster drill on a cruise?
faq2a: No. The safety briefing is compulsory under international maritime rules and lines check attendance, usually by scanning your card at your station. Guests who miss it are tracked down and can face consequences, including being removed from the cruise.
faq3q: Can I bring cannabis to a cruise from Vancouver?
faq3a: No. Even though it is legal in Canada, Alaska sailings from Canada Place include US preclearance, and cannabis remains illegal under US federal law. Cruise lines also ban it on board, so leave it at home.

Most boarding-day stress comes from a few small, predictable slips rather than anything complicated. Here are the ones that catch first-timers, grouped by when they happen, with the fix for each.

## Mistakes before you leave home
### Flying in the same day
A delayed morning flight from Toronto to Fort Lauderdale, or a cancelled Calgary to Vancouver hop, is the most common way people miss a ship. The ship will not wait. Fly in the day before, as explained in [flying the day before vs same day](/articles/fly-day-before-cruise/).

### Skipping online check-in
Online check-in, done in the line's app or website, gives you your boarding time and boarding pass. Skip it and you may wait at a desk to fill in the same forms, or be refused if the line's deadline has passed. Upload a passport photo and your arrival time early, using [online check-in and documents](/articles/cruise-check-in-online-documents/).

### Bringing the wrong ID or a soon-to-expire passport
Canadians should sail on a valid passport, ideally with six months left. It is required for flights to US ports and for US preclearance on Alaska sailings from Vancouver.

> **Watch out:** cannabis is legal at home but illegal under US federal law and banned by every major line. Bringing it to Canada Place, where US preclearance happens, is a serious mistake. See [why cannabis cannot come on a cruise](/articles/cruise-cannabis-canada-why-no/).

## Mistakes at the terminal
### Arriving the minute check-in opens
Everyone with an early slot shows up at once. Arriving mid-window usually means a shorter line and cabins that are nearly ready.

### Packing essentials in checked bags
Bags handed to porters can take until dinner, or later, to reach your cabin. Your carry-on should hold:
- Passports, boarding documents and the credit card linked to your account
- Prescription medication, and CPAP equipment if you use it
- Phone, charger and any lithium battery packs
- A swimsuit, a warm layer and a change of clothes

### Packing banned items
Irons, extension cords with surge protection, candles and liquor beyond the allowance are commonly flagged at security. Bags with banned items can be held for a search, so your suitcase arrives even later. The [extension cord rules](/articles/extension-cord-cruise-ship-rules/) and [what not to pack](/articles/what-not-to-pack-for-a-cruise/) pages list the usual suspects.

### Forgetting to tip porters
Porters at Florida and Seattle terminals expect around US$2–5 a bag. At Canada Place, a few dollars a bag is also customary. Have small bills ready.

## Mistakes once you are aboard
### Waiting around for your cabin
Cabins often open around 1pm. Head straight for lunch, then explore, rather than queuing in a hallway.

### Treating the muster drill as optional
It is not. On most big lines you watch a video in the app or on your cabin TV, then check in at your muster station before sailing. Missing it is noticed.

### Leaving phone data on at sail-away
Once the ship leaves port, your phone can connect to the ship's maritime network, which bills far above Canadian travel passes. Switch to airplane mode before departure, as covered in [cruise wifi and roaming for Canadians](/articles/cruise-wifi-phone-roaming-canadians/).

### Not booking what sells out
Specialty dinners, shows and spa slots fill on day one. Book them in the first afternoon.

> **Canadian tip:** link a no-foreign-transaction-fee credit card to your onboard account at check-in. Every charge is in US dollars, and the default card on many Canadian wallets adds about 2.5%.

## A calmer boarding day
Fly in early, check in online, pack a smart carry-on and arrive mid-window, and the rest takes care of itself.
