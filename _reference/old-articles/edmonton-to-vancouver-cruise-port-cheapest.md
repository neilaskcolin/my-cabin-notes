Fly, and book early. An economy flight from YEG to YVR plus the SkyTrain to Canada Place is almost always the cheapest way for one or two people to reach a Vancouver ship. The traps are bag fees on bargain fares and booking a thin-schedule airline on sailing day.

## Cheapest ways from Edmonton, ranked
Rough return costs per person at recent prices, for a 7-night sailing.

| Option | Rough cost per person | Time each way | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discount flight plus SkyTrain | C$100–220 plus bag fees | About 4–5 hours door to ship | Cheapest if you travel light and fly the day before |
| Mainline flight plus SkyTrain | C$150–300 | About 4–5 hours | The best mix of price and reliability |
| Coach | Often the lowest fare | A very long day | For travellers who won't fly |
| Drive, cost split between two | About C$450–700 incl. parking and hotels | Two days | Only wins with four in the car |
| VIA Rail's Canadian | Varies widely by seat or sleeper | Roughly a day, often late | An experience, not a saving |

All flight options need the SkyTrain from YVR to Waterfront, about C$11–12 including the airport AddFare, and a hotel the night before sailing.

## Why a cheap flight usually wins
YEG to YVR takes about 90 minutes, with frequent service from WestJet and Air Canada and seasonal competition from lower-cost carriers. Book three to six months out and returns regularly land between C$120 and C$250. At YVR, skip the C$40 taxi: the Canada Line runs to Waterfront Station, a couple of minutes' walk from Canada Place, in about 26 minutes.

## The bag-fee trap
The cheapest fares rarely include a checked bag. At recent prices, a first checked bag costs roughly C$35–60 each way depending on the airline, fare and whether you prepay, and airport rates are higher. On a bargain ticket, two checked bags return can cost as much as the seats.

> **Canadian tip:** Alaska packing is bulky, so plan for it. Share one checked bag between two people, prepay it online, and wear your heaviest layers on the plane. That usually keeps the discount fare cheaper than a mainline ticket with bags included.

## Ultra-low-cost carriers on cruise day
A rock-bottom fare is real money saved, but discount airlines run thin schedules. If your flight cancels, the next one might be tomorrow, which is fatal for a same-day sailing. They are fine if you fly in a day early; for same-day arrival they are a false economy. [What happens if your flight is delayed](/articles/cruise-flight-delay-what-happens/) explains why.

## Driving: the maths most people skip
Edmonton to Vancouver is about 1,160 km, usually via the Yellowhead Highway to Jasper, south past Valemount to Kamloops, then over the Coquihalla. That is 12 hours or more of driving. Count it properly:

- fuel in both directions through the mountains
- a hotel night each way for most drivers
- C$25–40 per day to park for the whole cruise, or C$175–280 for 7 nights
- a Parks Canada pass if you stop in Jasper National Park

Two people almost always fly cheaper. Four people sharing one vehicle is where driving genuinely competes. Parking alternatives are in [Vancouver cruise ship parking](/articles/vancouver-cruise-terminal-parking/), and train and coach details are in [cruising from Vancouver without flying](/articles/cruise-from-vancouver-without-flying/).

## Getting to YEG without paying for a week of parking
Edmonton's airport is about 30 km south of downtown, so getting there matters on a budget. Edmonton Transit's airport bus from Century Park LRT station is the cheapest route. If you drive, off-site park-and-fly lots near Leduc can undercut the airport's long-term rates for a week or more. A drop-off from a friend beats both.

## Where the bundled option sneaks ahead
Fly-cruise packages price the flight, pre-night hotel and transfers with the cruise. Package air sometimes books below walk-up fares in peak Alaska season, and it comes with delay protection: the line rebooks you and gets you to the ship if a flight goes wrong. Price both ways before buying a DIY fare; the trade-offs are in [cruise line air vs your own flights](/articles/cruise-line-air-vs-own-flights/), and timing is in [flying the day before your cruise](/articles/fly-day-before-cruise/).
