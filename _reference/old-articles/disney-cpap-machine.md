Yes, you can bring a CPAP on any Disney ship, and the line has a simple process for it. The two things to sort out are distilled water and a safe way to reach the socket, and both are handled through Disney's Special Services team.

## How to set up a CPAP on a Disney cruise, step by step
1. Once you have a reservation number, log in to your Disney Cruise Line booking online and open the Special Services page.
2. Under Medical Equipment, tick the option for travelling with a CPAP and needing distilled water. Guests report it arriving in a bottle in the stateroom, typically daily.
3. If you think the bed is far from a socket, phone Disney Cruise Line to have an extension cord request added to your reservation.
4. On embarkation day, keep the machine with you. Never hand it to a porter with your checked bags.
5. Once in the stateroom, check where the sockets are, then tell your stateroom host or Guest Services what is still missing.

## Distilled water on Disney ships
Disney supplies distilled water on request, but a first-night gap is common if the request did not reach the ship. Buy a small jug near the port the night before: there are pharmacies and supermarkets near Port Canaveral and Fort Lauderdale, and plenty in downtown Vancouver for Alaska sailings. The wider options, including using a machine without a humidifier, are in [CPAP distilled water on cruise ships](/articles/cpap-distilled-water-cruise-ships/).

> **Canadian tip:** airport security limits liquids in your hand luggage, so buying distilled water at your destination is easier than flying it from Toronto, Calgary or Winnipeg.

## Extension cords and power strips
Guest Services lends approved extension cords for medical devices. Your own cord or power strip may be held at terminal security, especially if it has a surge protector, and returned at the end of the cruise. The rules behind that are in [extension cords on a cruise ship](/articles/extension-cord-cruise-ship-rules/).

## Fitting a CPAP into a Disney family stateroom
Disney designs its staterooms for families. Many have a split bathroom, a sofa bed and sometimes a pull-down bunk, so the CPAP user may not be sleeping in the bed nearest a socket. Layouts also differ between the older Disney Magic and Disney Wonder, the Dream and Fantasy, and the newer Wish-class ships (Disney Wish, Treasure and Destiny). Look at the deck plan for your exact category, or ask when booking.

If children share the room, set the machine on the side of the bed away from the sofa bed. Modern machines are quiet, but a hose leak at 2 a.m. will wake a light sleeper.

## What to pack in your carry-on
- The CPAP, mask, hose and power supply, in their case.
- A spare mask cushion and filter.
- A copy of your prescription or a letter from your doctor.
- Distilled water for the first night, bought near the port.
- Any lithium battery pack, which must travel in hand luggage on the plane.

Canadian airlines usually let a CPAP travel as a medical item on top of your carry-on allowance, but confirm with your airline before you fly. A lost or delayed checked bag is common enough, as [lost or delayed luggage on a cruise](/articles/lost-delayed-luggage-cruise/) explains, that the machine should never go in the hold.

## How Disney compares
| Line | Desk to contact | Distilled water |
|---|---|---|
| **Disney** | Special Services | On request via Special Services |
| [Royal Caribbean](/articles/royal-caribbean-cpap-machine/) | Access Department | On request, often via stateroom attendant |
| [Carnival](/articles/carnival-cpap-machine/) | Special Needs team | Paid; pre-order, buy on board or bring your own |

All three lines welcome CPAP machines. Disney and Royal Caribbean lend approved cords, while Carnival lets you bring your own plain cord without a surge protector. Disney's advantage is that the request is a tick box in your online booking rather than a separate form. ## Getting it noted on the booking properly
Tick the Special Services box the week you book, not the week you sail. A request made early has time to reach the ship, which is what makes night one uneventful.
