Celebrity doesn't hold traditional formal nights. Most evenings are smart casual, and one or two nights per cruise are "evening chic", which means cocktail-party smart rather than black tie.

## What does evening chic mean on Celebrity?
Celebrity replaced formal night with evening chic to keep some polish without the tuxedo. For men, dress trousers and a collared shirt are the baseline, and a blazer or sports jacket is common but optional. For women, a cocktail dress, a dressy jumpsuit, or an elegant top with trousers or a skirt all fit in. Full formal wear is welcome and you will see some, but it is a minority.

Evening chic nights usually fall on sea days and are flagged in the daily schedule and the app. Expect two on a 7-night cruise and one on shorter sailings.

## Smart casual: the rest of the week
Most nights call for smart casual: collared or tidy shirts, blouses, trousers, skirts, dresses. Swimwear, tank tops, bare feet and baseball caps don't belong in the evening dining rooms, and Celebrity asks that shorts stay out at dinner.

## Dress code by venue
| Where | Smart casual nights | Evening chic nights |
|---|---|---|
| Main dining room | Collared shirt and trousers, or a dress | Blazer optional, cocktail dress or dressy separates |
| Specialty restaurants such as Fine Cut Steakhouse | Same as the main dining room | Same as the main dining room |
| Luminae, for Retreat guests | Smart casual | Evening chic |
| Oceanview Café buffet | Relaxed, no wet swimwear | Relaxed, no wet swimwear |
| Bars and the theatre after dinner | Smart casual | Most guests stay dressed up |

On Edge-class ships the main dining room is four complimentary restaurants, Cosmopolitan, Cyprus, Normandie and Tuscan, and all four follow the same code. The buffet is the easy option for anyone who would rather skip dressing up on a chic night.

Daytime is casual everywhere. Around the pools and the adults-only Solarium, throw on a cover-up when you walk indoors. [What to wear during the day](/articles/cruise-ship-dress-code-daytime-wear/) covers the rest.

## Dressing for Celebrity's Canadian itineraries
**Alaska from Vancouver or Seattle.** The code doesn't change in Alaska, but the weather shapes what works. Choose an evening chic outfit that layers, such as a blazer over a knit or a wrap over a dress, with closed shoes. Days are about waterproofs and fleece, which is set out in the [Alaska cruise dress code](/articles/alaska-cruise-dress-code/) guide.

**Caribbean from Fort Lauderdale or Miami.** Lighter fabrics and sandals are fine for smart casual, but bring a layer for the dining room air conditioning. A linen blazer covers evening chic without overheating on deck.

> **Canadian tip:** flying from Calgary, Winnipeg or Toronto on a carry-on-only fare? One dark blazer or a dressy jumpsuit worn on both evening chic nights can save a checked bag fee each way.

## Packing list for a 7-night Celebrity cruise
- One evening chic outfit per person, worn twice, or two if you like variety.
- Mix-and-match smart casual pieces for the other five nights.
- Dress shoes or smart flats that grip on a wet deck.
- A light layer, since dining rooms can be cool.
- Fabrics that don't crease, because pressing on board is a paid service on most fares.

## Booking specialty dining around chic nights
Many couples book a specialty restaurant on one evening chic night to make an occasion of it. Reserve it before sailing, since the popular slots on those nights go first.
