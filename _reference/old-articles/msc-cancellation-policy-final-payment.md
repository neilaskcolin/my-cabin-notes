MSC's cancellation rules are built around cruise length and fare type, and the deposit is the first thing you put at risk. On a typical 7-night Caribbean sailing booked in North America, final payment falls about 90 days out, and penalties climb in steps after that until the whole fare is gone in the last two weeks.

## When is MSC final payment due?
At recent policy for North American bookings, roughly:
- **4 nights or less** (the short Bahamas runs to Ocean Cay from Miami and Port Canaveral): about 75 days before sailing.
- **5 to 14 nights**: about 90 days before sailing.
- **15 nights or more** (Grand Voyages from Europe, Panama Canal repositioning): about 110 days before sailing.

Your confirmation shows the governing date. [Cruise deposits and final payment dates explained](/articles/cruise-deposits-final-payment/) covers how deposits work across lines.

## MSC cancellation penalties by days before sailing
This is the published schedule for standard cabins on a 5–14 night cruise. Shorter and longer cruises use the same percentages with different day bands.

| Days before sailing | What you lose |
|---|---|
| 90 or more | Deposit generally refundable, unless your fare says otherwise |
| 89 to 61 | The deposit |
| 60 to 46 | 50% of the fare |
| 45 to 16 | 75% of the fare |
| 15 or fewer, or no-show | 100% of the fare |

Taxes, fees and port charges are generally refunded, except for no-shows and guests who leave a cruise early.

> **Example:** a Winnipeg couple books a 7-night MSC Seashore sailing from Port Canaveral for about C$3,200 including taxes. If a family emergency hits 50 days out, they lose 50% of the cruise fare. Seventeen days later, at 33 days out, it would be 75%. That gap is exactly what [travel insurance for Canadian cruisers](/articles/travel-insurance-cruise-canadians/) is for.

## How MSC's fare types change the picture
MSC sells the same ship under different "experiences", and they behave differently when plans move.

### Bella
The entry-level experience, where MSC usually assigns your cabin. At recent policy, one date change is allowed for a fee of about US$50 per person if made after penalties start and at least 30 days before sailing, with the new date within 90 days of the original. Anything else counts as a cancellation.

### Fantastica, Aurea and Yacht Club
The same one-time date change is free under the same timing rules. MSC Yacht Club suites have their own, stricter schedule: the deposit is treated as non-refundable well ahead of sailing, so ask before you pay it.

## Canadians and market-specific terms
MSC sells in many markets, and a sailing booked through a European site can carry different cancellation rules and a different currency. MSC has a Canadian office and prices Canadian bookings in Canadian dollars, so book through the Canadian or North American channel and read those terms. Mixing markets also complicates [paying in CAD vs USD](/articles/paying-cad-vs-usd-cruise/).

## If you need to cancel, in order
1. Check today's date against your penalty bands before you call, because waiting even a day can move you into the next band.
2. Ask whether a date change fits your plans better than a full cancellation; on Fantastica it may cost nothing.
3. Confirm in writing what you will get back and whether any part comes as [future cruise credit](/articles/future-cruise-credit-explained/) rather than cash.
4. Start your insurance claim with the cancellation invoice, not before.

### Cancelling just one guest
Dropping one person after final payment can leave the other paying a single supplement. Ask for the repriced total first.
