Royal Caribbean charges an automatic gratuity of roughly US$18–19 per person per day in standard cabins and about US$21 in suites, at recent rates. It is added to your onboard account every day unless you prepay it, and prepaying is usually the smarter move for Canadians.

## How much are Royal Caribbean gratuities per day?
The charge applies to every guest in the cabin, children included, for every night of the sailing. Royal Caribbean revises the amount from time to time, so check the current figure on its site or with whoever booked you before you set a budget.

Suite guests pay the higher rate because suites come with extra service, such as a concierge or suite lounge on many ships.

| Cabin and party, 7 nights | Approx. USD | Roughly in CAD |
|---|---|---|
| Couple, standard cabin | $252–266 | C$340–375 |
| Couple, suite | about $294 | C$395–410 |
| Family of four, standard cabin | $504–532 | C$680–745 |
| Family of four, 10 nights | $720–760 | C$970–1,065 |

> **Quick math:** take the daily rate, multiply by the number of guests and the number of nights, then multiply by about 1.35–1.40 for a rough Canadian-dollar figure.

## What the service charge pays for
The money is pooled across dining room staff, stateroom attendants and behind-the-scenes crew such as galley teams. For many of them it is a real part of their pay, not a bonus. The wider picture is in [cruise gratuities explained for Canadians](/articles/cruise-gratuities-canadians/).

## Should Canadians prepay Royal Caribbean gratuities?
Usually, yes. Royal Caribbean sells fares in Canadian dollars for Canadian residents, and prepaid gratuities added to a CAD booking are priced in CAD too. Onboard, the daily charge posts in US dollars and converts at your card's rate, plus any foreign-transaction fee (often around 2.5% on Canadian cards). The trade-offs are covered in [paying in CAD vs USD for a cruise](/articles/paying-cad-vs-usd-cruise/).

Prepaying also locks the rate in force on the day you pay. If the line raises gratuities before you sail, you keep the lower figure.

### How to prepay, step by step
1. Find the current daily rate for your cabin type and count every guest, including kids.
2. Ask to add prepaid gratuities to your reservation, either at booking or before the cut-off a few days before sailing.
3. Check the amount on your next invoice matches guests times nights times rate.
4. Once on board, confirm in the app or at Guest Services that your account shows gratuities as prepaid, so you are not charged twice.

> **Canadian tip:** if you are not prepaying, settle the onboard account with a no-foreign-transaction-fee credit card. On a family's 7-night gratuity bill alone, a 2.5% fee adds roughly C$17–19 for nothing.

## Can you adjust gratuities on board?
Yes. Guest Services can lower the charge for a genuine service problem, and adjusting for a specific failure is fair. Removing it wholesale shortchanges crew who did their jobs well. If one person stands out, a cash tip or a note naming them is a good addition.

## What the daily gratuity doesn't cover
An automatic service charge, 18% at recent rates, is added separately to bar drinks, drink packages, specialty dining and spa services, so the menu price is not the final price. Confirm the current percentage for your sailing. Terminal porters and shore excursion guides are tipped in cash by convention, so bring some small US bills. If you are weighing a package, the [Royal Caribbean drink package price](/articles/royal-caribbean-drink-package-price/) page shows how that surcharge changes the maths.

## Promotions that include gratuities
Royal Caribbean periodically bundles prepaid gratuities or onboard credit into fares. For a family that can be worth several hundred dollars, so ask whether your dates carry a promotion before you pay the deposit. ## Locking the tipping budget before you sail
Prepaying, ideally on a sailing where gratuities are bundled into the fare, turns one of the least predictable parts of a Royal Caribbean bill into a fixed cost in Canadian dollars. It takes five minutes to ask about at booking.
