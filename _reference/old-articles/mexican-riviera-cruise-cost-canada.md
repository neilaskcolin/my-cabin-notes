For a couple flying from western Canada, a 7-night Mexican Riviera cruise usually lands between C$3,500 and C$6,500 all-in in an inside cabin. It is one of the best-value warm-weather cruises for Canadians west of Winnipeg, mainly because the flight is short.

## Why the departure port changes the maths
Caribbean sailings start in Florida, which is a long trip from Alberta or BC. Mexican Riviera ships leave from Los Angeles or San Diego instead, so the cheaper side of the country flips. Compare it with our [Caribbean cruise budget from Canada](/articles/caribbean-cruise-cost-canada-budget/) using your own home airport.

| Flying from | To LA or San Diego | To Fort Lauderdale or Miami |
|---|---|---|
| Vancouver | About 3 hours, often C$250–550 return | Usually a connection or a long nonstop |
| Calgary or Edmonton | About 3 hours, often C$300–600 return | 4.5–5+ hours, often more expensive |
| Winnipeg | Often a connection | Often a connection |
| Toronto or Montreal | 5+ hours, often C$400–800 return | About 3 hours, often cheaper |

Flight prices are per person at recent prices and swing a lot around March break and Christmas. For eastern Canadians the Caribbean usually wins on flights alone.

## What a 7-night sailing costs, per couple
- **Fare:** inside cabins run roughly US$600–900 per person and balconies US$1,000–1,600, or about C$1,650–2,450 per couple for an inside cabin.
- **Flights from the West:** C$600–1,200 for two.
- **Hotel the night before:** C$250–400 near the port or airport.
- **Transfers:** C$100–200 in total. The LA cruise port is about half an hour or more from LAX, depending on traffic.
- **Gratuities:** about C$310–350 for the week.
- **Drinks and extras:** C$270–560 without a package.
- **Excursions:** C$300–700 for a couple of paid tours.
- **Travel insurance:** C$150–400.

> **Quick math:** add the low ends and a Calgary couple lands near C$3,600; take the high ends and it is about C$6,250. A balcony adds roughly C$1,000–1,900 on top.

## The ports and what they cost ashore
Each port has a different character, so the excursion budget depends on what you want from the day.

### Cabo San Lucas
Usually a tender port, with the famous rock arch reachable by water taxi or glass-bottom boat for modest prices. Snorkelling, sailing and whale watching in winter are the popular paid trips, typically US$60–120 per person.

### Mazatlán
A walkable historic centre and a long seafront promenade make this an easy do-it-yourself day. A taxi or open-air pulmonía ride into the old town costs little.

### Puerto Vallarta
The Malecón boardwalk is free and pleasant. Zip-lining, Banderas Bay boat trips and tequila tastings fill the paid side, again roughly US$60–150 per person.

Our guide to [shore excursions through the ship versus independent](/articles/cruise-excursion-through-ship-vs-independent/) helps decide which way to book. Remember that the all-aboard time is firm.

## Spending on board and ashore
Onboard accounts are in US dollars, and US cash is widely accepted in these ports, though you will often get change in pesos. A credit card with no foreign transaction fee saves about 2.5% on everything.

> **Canadian tip:** after 48 hours or more away, each adult can bring back goods worth up to C$800 duty-free, including 1.14 litres of spirits or 1.5 litres of wine. That bottle of tequila from Puerto Vallarta fits; a case does not.

## Timing your sailing
The main season runs from late fall to spring, when Mexico's Pacific coast is dry and warm. That suits a February escape from Alberta. Summer and early fall are hotter and fall inside the Pacific hurricane season, though fares can be lower. Our page on [where to cruise in February](/articles/where-cruise-february-canadians/) compares other winter options.

## Who this route suits
The Mexican Riviera works best for western Canadians, first-timers who want an easy warm week, and anyone who prefers walkable mainland towns to beach-and-island hopping. It suits eastern Canadians less, unless you find a West Coast flight deal or want to pair the cruise with time in California.

If a full week is too much, 3- and 4-night sailings from Los Angeles or Long Beach to Ensenada cost far less, sometimes a few hundred US dollars per person. They make a cheap trial cruise for a Vancouver or Calgary couple, though you see only one Mexican port and spend much of the time at sea. ## Comparing it against the Caribbean
Price both routes from your own airport, including a hotel night and transfers, before deciding. From Vancouver or Calgary the Mexican Riviera often comes out ahead; from Toronto or Montreal the Caribbean usually does.
