Norwegian doesn't have formal nights. The rule every evening is resort casual in the main dining rooms and smart casual in the specialty restaurants, with one optional night to dress up if you want to. "No formal nights" is not quite "anything goes", though, so it helps to know where the lines are.

## What is the dress code on Norwegian Cruise Line?
In the main dining rooms, resort casual means trousers, jeans or smart shorts with a collared shirt or nice top for men, and a dress, skirt, trousers or smart top for women. The exclusions are swimwear, tank tops and bare feet at dinner, and some venues ask for long trousers rather than shorts in the evening.

During the day it is relaxed everywhere except the dining rooms, where a cover-up over swimwear is expected.

## Does Norwegian have formal nights?
No. Norwegian dropped compulsory formal nights as part of freestyle cruising. Instead, most sailings have one optional dress-up evening, which the line has billed as a "Dress Up or Not" night. Guests who enjoy formal wear put on a suit or cocktail dress and the photographers set up backdrops; everyone else dresses as usual and nobody is turned away.

## Norwegian's dress code by venue
| Venue | Evening dress | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Main dining rooms | Resort casual | Jeans fine; no swimwear or tank tops |
| Specialty restaurants (Cagney's, Le Bistro) | Smart casual | Collared shirt, long trousers, no flip-flops |
| Buffet (Garden Café on many ships) | Casual | Cover-up over swimwear |
| The Haven Restaurant | Resort casual | Suite guests only |
| Bars, theatre, casino | Casual | Same as daytime, within reason |

> **Watch out:** if you have booked several specialty dinners through Free at Sea, pack enough smart-casual outfits for those nights. One pair of long trousers and two collared shirts covers most men on a 7-night sailing.

## Dressing for an Alaska sailing on Norwegian
On Norwegian's Alaska cruises from Seattle and Vancouver, practicality wins. Expect dinners in jeans and a sweater, and early mornings on deck in a toque and fleece while the ship approaches a glacier. On Norwegian Bliss and Norwegian Encore, the forward Observation Lounge lets you watch the scenery indoors without bundling up. The [Alaska cruise dress code](/articles/alaska-cruise-dress-code/) page covers what people actually wear ashore.

## What to pack for a week on Norwegian
- One smart outfit for the optional dress-up night and specialty dinners
- Two or three resort-casual evening outfits you can mix
- Daytime clothes, swimwear and a cover-up
- Comfortable shoes for port days, plus one pair that works at dinner
- For Alaska: a waterproof shell, warm layers, gloves and a toque

That is a genuinely lighter suitcase than you would need on a line with two or three formal nights, which helps if you are flying from Toronto or Winnipeg on a fare with a checked-bag fee. ## Who Norwegian's dress code suits
It suits guests who find formal nights a chore and want to pack light, as covered in [Is Norwegian Cruise Line right for you](/articles/norwegian-cruise-line-honest-guide-canadians/). Traditionalists who enjoy the ritual of a gala evening sometimes miss it. If that is you, the optional night helps, but expect only some of the room to join in.
