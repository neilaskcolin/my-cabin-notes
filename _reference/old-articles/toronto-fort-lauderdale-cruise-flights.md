Toronto to Fort Lauderdale is about as easy as fly-cruise travel gets: a three-hour nonstop, US customs done before you leave Pearson, and a ship roughly 15 minutes from the arrivals hall. The main decisions are when to fly, whether a Buffalo fare is worth the drive, and how much the whole trip really costs.

## How long is the flight and what does it cost?
Nonstop flights from Pearson to FLL take about three hours, with several carriers flying the route daily in winter. Return economy fares run roughly C$300–550 at recent prices. March break and the Christmas weeks often sit higher, and seat sales in the fall can dip lower.

> **Canadian tip:** Pearson's US preclearance means customs and immigration happen in Toronto. Budget extra time for that line at the airport, but once you land in Florida you can walk straight out to baggage claim and taxis.

Booking six to nine months ahead for a winter sailing gives the best mix of price and schedule. For a deeper look at timing, see [the best day of the week to fly for a cruise connection](/articles/best-day-week-fly-cruise-connection/).

## Fort Lauderdale or Buffalo?
### The case for Buffalo
Many Ontario travellers check fares from Buffalo, about 1.5–2 hours' drive from Toronto, where US low-cost carriers sometimes undercut Pearson. For a family of four, a big enough gap can pay for the trouble.

### The hidden costs
The drive adds a land border crossing (bring passports, and NEXUS helps), fuel, a week of airport parking, and winter roads on the QEW. Bag fees on low-cost carriers can wipe out the saving. Compare the full door-to-door cost, not just the fares.

## Which cruise lines sail from Port Everglades?
Port Everglades is one of the world's busiest cruise ports. It's a main Caribbean base for lines such as Celebrity, Princess and Holland America, with ships from Royal Caribbean, Carnival and others also sailing from here. Check the port on your booking confirmation before buying flights, because some lines sail from both Fort Lauderdale and Miami.

## The 15-minute transfer
A taxi or rideshare from FLL to the port usually runs about US$15–25. Some hotels near the port run cruise shuttles, and cruise lines sell coach transfers too. Because the distance is so short, even heavy traffic rarely adds much time. See [cruise ship transfers explained](/articles/cruise-ship-transfers-explained/) for the options at both Florida ports.

## What the travel part of the trip costs from Toronto
Here's the getting-there portion for a couple, at recent prices. Your cruise fare and onboard spending sit on top.

| Item, per couple | Rough cost |
|---|---|
| Return flights Pearson–FLL | C$600–1,100 |
| One hotel night near the port or airport | US$150–280 (roughly C$210–390) |
| Taxis: airport to hotel, hotel to port, port to airport | US$50–80 (roughly C$70–110) |
| Getting to Pearson, or a week of parking | C$80–250 |
| **Rough total** | **C$960–1,850** |

> **Example:** a Mississauga couple on a 7-night Western Caribbean sailing in early February would fly Friday morning, sleep near the port Friday night, board Saturday, and fly home the following Saturday afternoon. They'd be back in Toronto for dinner, having taken just one extra day off work for the flight down.

## Coming home through customs
On the way back you clear Canadian customs at Pearson. After more than 48 hours away, each person can bring back up to C$800 of goods duty-free, including limited alcohol. [Is cruise duty-free actually cheaper](/articles/cruise-shopping-onboard-duty-free/) covers the limits.

## Fort Lauderdale vs Miami
Both ports serve the Caribbean all winter. Fort Lauderdale's smaller airport and very short transfer make it the easier trip for most Ontario travellers. Miami is the right choice when your ship sails from there, and the details are in [Toronto to Miami for a Caribbean cruise](/articles/toronto-miami-caribbean-cruise/).

## Can you sail from Toronto to Florida instead of flying?
Not on a regular cruise: no cruise line runs Toronto–Florida sailings, and the Great Lakes-to-ocean route isn't served by cruise ships. The realistic alternatives are a repositioning cruise from a Northeast US port (New York or Boston to Florida in autumn), reached by a short flight or train, or for most people, the three-hour flight this article covers. See [what a repositioning cruise is](/articles/repositioning-cruises-explained/), and for the no-fly options in detail, [can you cruise from Toronto to Florida](/articles/cruise-toronto-to-florida-options/).

## Booking the flights with the cruise
Flights, a pre-cruise hotel and the cruise can be booked as one package through the cruise line. That isn't always cheaper, but if a line-booked flight is delayed, the line takes on responsibility for getting you to the ship. Compare both before you book flights yourself, using [cruise line air vs your own flights](/articles/cruise-line-air-vs-own-flights/).
