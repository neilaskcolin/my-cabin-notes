Cruising suits many solo women over 50 very well: you unpack once, dinner is taken care of, and company is there when you want it and easy to avoid when you don't. The best experience comes from three choices: the right cabin, the right ship and a few smart habits.

## Which cruise lines have solo cabins?
Dedicated solo cabins avoid most of the single supplement and, on some ships, come with a lounge where solo guests naturally meet. Availability varies by ship within each line, so check the specific vessel.

| Line | Solo cabin option | Social extras |
|---|---|---|
| Norwegian | Studio cabins on many newer ships, with a keycard-access Studio Lounge | Daily solo meetups in the lounge |
| Royal Caribbean | Studio cabins on some ships | Solo get-togethers listed in the daily programme |
| Holland America | Single oceanview cabins on some newer ships | Strong enrichment and small-group activities |
| Cunard | Single cabins on some ships | Hosted events, dance hosts on some voyages |
| Celebrity | Single cabins on some newer ships | Solo meetups on many sailings |

No solo cabin on your preferred ship? Watch for promotions with reduced or waived supplements, which some premium and river lines run. Our guide to [dodging the single supplement](/articles/single-supplement-how-to-dodge/) covers the tactics, and [solo cruising from Canada](/articles/solo-cruising-canada-supplements/) explains how supplements are priced.

## Choosing a ship where you'll feel at home
### The crowd
Premium lines, which attract a calmer and more mature guest mix, suit many solo women in this age group. Lecture programmes, cooking demonstrations and trivia give you easy conversation starters. See [cruise ship book clubs, trivia and social groups](/articles/cruise-ship-book-clubs-trivia-social-groups/) for what's typically on offer, and the [best cruise lines for couples over 50](/articles/best-cruise-lines-couples-over-50/) for how the lines compare in style.

### Dining
Ask for a shared table in the main dining room. Many lines seat solo travellers together on request, which turns dinner into the social anchor of the day. If you'd rather eat alone some nights, specialty restaurants and room service are always there.

### The itinerary
Ports with walkable, well-established centres, such as Juneau, Barcelona, Bergen or Halifax, are easy to explore on your own. Elsewhere, a ship's tour gives you company and guarantees the ship waits if the tour runs late. [Ship excursions vs booking independently](/articles/cruise-excursion-through-ship-vs-independent/) weighs the trade-offs.

> **Good to know:** many women join the online roll call for their sailing before departure. It's an easy way to arrange a meetup on the first evening or share a private tour in port.

## Staying safe on board and ashore
Cruise ships are among the more controlled travel environments, but the usual habits apply. Our honest look at [cruise ship safety](/articles/whats-safe-cruise-ships-honest-answer/) covers the overall picture.

- Check the peephole or call Guest Services before opening your door to anyone unexpected.
- Don't share your cabin number with people you've just met.
- Use the in-cabin safe for passports, cash and jewellery.
- Watch your drink, and pace yourself if alcohol is part of the evening.
- In port, use licensed taxis, tell someone your plans, and know the all-aboard time.
- Report anything that worries you to the ship's security team immediately.

> **Canadian tip:** register your trip with Global Affairs Canada's free Registration of Canadians Abroad service, so you can be reached in an emergency. Also check that your travel insurance includes medical evacuation, as your provincial plan covers very little abroad.

## Finding your solo style
Some women want a cabin, a book and total freedom; others want a full social calendar. Decide which you are, then choose the ship to match. For a first trip, a solo cabin on a ship with a lounge and daily meetups is a gentle start.
