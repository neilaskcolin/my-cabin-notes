If you are driving to an Alaska cruise, parking is a real line in the budget: roughly C$210–280 for a week at the terminal itself. You can usually cut that significantly by parking further out or bundling parking with a hotel night.

## What does parking at Canada Place cost?
Canada Place has parking right at the terminal, so you can roll your bags to check-in without a shuttle. At recent prices, pre-booked cruise parking runs roughly C$30–40 per day, or about C$210–280 for a typical 7-night round trip. Confirm the current rate when you book, and check whether the quoted price includes taxes.

The value is convenience. On disembarkation morning you walk off the ship, collect your bags and drive away, which matters if you have a long drive home to the Interior or Alberta.

### The hotel stay-and-park option
Several Vancouver-area hotels sell a pre-cruise night with parking for the length of your sailing. Compare that with a hotel booked separately plus a week of terminal parking, and the package often wins. Downtown hotels near Canada Place charge more per night, while Richmond hotels near Canada Line stations are often cheaper and still about 25–30 minutes from Waterfront station. See [where to stay in Vancouver the night before your cruise](/articles/where-to-stay-vancouver-night-before-cruise/) for areas to consider.

> **Quick math:** a downtown hotel at C$350 plus seven nights of terminal parking at C$35 comes to C$595. A stay-and-park package at C$400 covers both, saving about C$195 for a couple.

## Should you drive to a Vancouver cruise at all?
From Calgary the drive is roughly 10–11 hours each way, and Edmonton is a little further, so most Albertans need an overnight stop on the way. Add fuel, a roadside hotel and parking, and driving is often similar in cost to flying for a couple. It can win for families, or if you want stops in the Okanagan or the Rockies. The trade-offs are covered in [driving from Calgary to an Alaska cruise](/articles/drive-from-calgary-to-alaska-cruise/) and [cruising from Vancouver without flying](/articles/cruise-from-vancouver-without-flying/).

Drivers from the Interior, such as Kelowna or Kamloops, are better placed: a same-day drive is possible, but a highway closure on the Coquihalla or heavy traffic on Highway 1 into the city can still cost you the ship.

> **Watch out:** summer Saturdays and Sundays can bring several ships into Canada Place on the same day. Terminal parking and nearby lots can sell out, so book parking when you book the cruise, not the week before.

## How to make driving day go smoothly
1. Book parking or your stay-and-park package as soon as your cruise is confirmed.
2. Drive in the day before if you are coming from Alberta or the Interior.
3. On cruise morning, drop bags and passengers at the terminal curb first.
4. Park, then walk back with only your carry-on.
5. Keep your parking confirmation and car keys in your carry-on, not your checked bag.
6. Note the parking level and the shuttle pickup point for disembarkation day.

The [Canada Place embarkation walkthrough](/articles/canada-place-embarkation-walkthrough/) covers what happens once you are inside the terminal.

## Which option suits you
If you are staying overnight anyway, a hotel package is usually the best value. If you are on a tight budget, an off-site lot booked early saves the most. If anyone in your group has limited mobility or you are carrying a lot of luggage, pay for Canada Place parking and enjoy the shortest walk to the ship.
