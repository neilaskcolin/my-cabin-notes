Holland America allows smoking and vaping only in marked outdoor areas and, on most ships, in a designated part of the casino. Staterooms and verandahs are smoke-free on every ship, and the penalty for getting it wrong is a US$250 cleaning fee for each day of violation.

## Where you can smoke on Holland America
Every Holland America ship has a designated smoking section at the Sea View Bar, an outdoor bar on an open deck. Some ships have other outdoor spots too. Locations vary by ship and are marked on board, in the daily program and in the Navigator app.

### The casino
This is the one indoor exception, and it depends on the ship. At recent policy:
- **Smoking allowed in designated areas, for active players only:** Eurodam, Koningsdam, Nieuw Amsterdam, Nieuw Statendam, Oosterdam, Rotterdam, Volendam, Westerdam and Zaandam.
- **Non-smoking casino:** Noordam and Zuiderdam.

If a smoke-free casino matters to you, that list is worth checking before you pick a ship. Holland America can change locations at any time, so confirm on board.

## Where you can't smoke
- Staterooms, on every ship.
- Verandahs and balconies, on every ship. Holland America was one of the last big lines to ban balcony smoking, and the ban is now firm.
- Restaurants, bars, lounges, theatres and other indoor spaces, apart from the casino areas above.
- Cigars and pipes anywhere inside the ship. They are allowed only on outside decks where smoking is permitted.

The fire-safety reasoning behind the balcony ban is covered in [smoking and vaping rules on cruise ships](/articles/smoking-vaping-rules-cruise-ships/).

## Vaping and e-cigarettes
Vapes follow the same boundaries as cigarettes. They are not allowed in staterooms, and in public areas only in designated smoking zones. Discreetly vaping on a verandah counts as a violation, with the same fee.

## What getting caught costs
Holland America's policy sets a US$250 cleaning fee per day of violation, charged to your onboard account. That is roughly C$340–350 a day. Repeat violations can lead to removal from the ship, at your own cost from whatever port you are in.

> **Watch out:** cannabis is not allowed on board, even on a sailing from Vancouver where it is legal ashore. Ships operate under rules that treat it as prohibited, and US ports apply federal law. [Why cannabis can't come on a cruise from Canada](/articles/cruise-cannabis-canada-why-no/) explains.

## Smoking on an Alaska or long voyage
On a 7-night Alaska sailing from Vancouver or Seattle, the Sea View Bar can be cold, wet and windy, especially in May and September. Pack a warm layer you do not mind smelling of smoke. On long Holland America voyages, the same outdoor spot becomes your regular haunt, so it is worth finding on the deck plan before you board.

Bringing tobacco home is covered by the CBSA personal exemption after more than 48 hours away: up to 200 cigarettes, 50 cigars and 200 grams of tobacco, with duty on anything more. [Is cruise duty-free actually cheaper](/articles/cruise-shopping-onboard-duty-free/) has the full limits.

## How Holland America compares
| Line | Smoking in the casino | Cabins and balconies |
|---|---|---|
| **Holland America** | Designated areas on most ships; not Noordam or Zuiderdam | Banned |
| [Princess](/articles/princess-smoking-vaping-policy/) | Varies by ship | Banned |
| [Celebrity](/articles/celebrity-smoking-vaping-policy/) | No — casinos are non-smoking | Banned |

Cabins and balconies are smoke-free on all three lines, so the real difference is the casino. Holland America and Princess both vary by ship, while Celebrity keeps its casinos smoke-free.
