Holland America's wifi comes in three tiers, Surf, Premium and Stream, now powered by Starlink across the fleet. At recent prices, Premium has run about US$26 a day and Stream about US$38, with Surf the cheapest. Buying before you sail has saved 20%, and Have It All fares include Surf.

## Holland America wifi packages and prices
Each plan covers one device at a time. Prices are recent figures in US dollars, based on a full-voyage package; confirm them for your sailing.

| Plan | What it handles | Recent price per day |
|---|---|---|
| Surf | Browsing, email, news, messaging apps; no calls | Lowest tier; included in Have It All |
| Premium | Everything in Surf, plus basic audio and video calls | about $26 |
| Stream | Everything above, plus Netflix-style streaming and wifi calling | about $38 |

Longer sailings can earn a lower daily rate, and packages run for up to 135 days, long enough for a Grand World Voyage. Day passes are sold only on board.

> **Quick math:** Premium for a 7-night Alaska sailing at about US$26 a day is roughly US$182 on board. Bought before sailing at 20% off, it is about US$146, roughly C$200–205.

## Is wifi included on Holland America?
A cruise-only fare includes no wifi. Have It All includes one Surf package, and Holland America's Early Booking Bonus version has upgraded that to Premium. If you would buy Premium anyway, that upgrade alone is worth around US$180 a week, as the [Holland America drink package price](/articles/holland-america-drink-package-price/) page explains alongside the other inclusions.

## What speeds to expect
Starlink has transformed connections on Holland America's ships, including the older Vista-class and R-class ships that used to struggle. Messaging and browsing now work well on most days, and video calls on Premium are generally usable. Steep fjords and busy evenings can still slow things briefly. [How cruise ship internet actually works](/articles/cruise-ship-internet-how-it-works/) explains why.

## How to buy Holland America wifi
1. Choose the tier by need: messages and email on Surf, video calls with family on Premium, streaming or heavy work on Stream.
2. Check whether your fare already includes Surf or Premium.
3. Buy before online sales close, typically three days before sailing, to get the 20% saving.
4. Buy a second plan if two of you want to be online at once. One plan means one connected device.
5. Switch devices by logging out of one and into another when you need to.

Working on board? [Can you work remotely from a cruise ship](/articles/can-you-work-remotely-cruise-ship/) covers whether Premium or Stream suits video meetings.

## Phones, roaming and Alaska ports
Put your phone in airplane mode at sea, or it may connect to the ship's own cellular network at high rates. In Juneau, Ketchikan and Skagway you are in the US, so a Canadian carrier's US roaming pass covers port days. On a Seattle round trip, your home plan works normally in Victoria. The full approach is in [cruise wifi and phone roaming for Canadians](/articles/cruise-wifi-phone-roaming-canadians/).

## Getting wifi bundled instead of bought
On a long Holland America voyage, wifi can cost more than a night in a good hotel every week. Checking whether your dates carry Have It All or an Early Booking Bonus is a quick question before you pay list price.
