Holland America's youth program runs from age 3 to 17. Younger children and tweens use Club HAL, and teens have The Loft on many ships. Daytime activities are included in the fare, but there is no care for under-3s, and the clubs are smaller and quieter than on lines built around families.

## Holland America kids club age groups
Holland America describes its program as supervised activities for ages 3 to 17, led by trained youth staff. On most sailings it divides roughly like this.

| Group | Approx. ages | What it's like |
|---|---|---|
| Club HAL, younger kids | 3 to about 7 | Crafts, games, story time, themed parties |
| Club HAL, tweens | about 8 to 12 | Sports, video games, team challenges |
| The Loft, teens | 13 to 17 | Teen-only lounge, evening events, on many ships |

The exact split between the two Club HAL groups has varied by ship and year, so confirm it for your sailing. Siblings close in age can sometimes stay together if you ask.

### Sign-in rules
Children aged 3 to 11 are signed in and out by a parent or a named adult. From 12, tweens and teens can usually come and go on their own. Children joining the youngest group generally need to be toilet-trained.

## Babies and toddlers on Holland America
There is no nursery or drop-off care for children under 3. Babysitting, where it exists, has been informal and not guaranteed, so plan to look after toddlers yourself. Infants generally need to be at least 6 months old to sail, and older on some long voyages.

If you are travelling with a little one, [cruising with a baby or toddler](/articles/cruising-with-baby-toddler/) covers what is realistic, and [cruise babysitting options](/articles/cruise-ship-babysitting-in-cabin-service/) compares the lines that do offer care.

## When the Holland America kids club is open
Club HAL typically runs morning, afternoon and evening sessions, closing around mealtimes so families can eat together. Hours are shorter than on family-focused lines, and late-night care has been offered only on some sailings, sometimes for a small fee.

Staffing scales with how many children are aboard. On a quiet sailing, groups may be combined; in July and August, the clubs fill up.

> **Good to know:** on Glacier Bay days, National Park Service rangers who board the ship have run Junior Ranger activities for children. It is one of the best kid-friendly moments on a Holland America Alaska cruise.

## Your first day with Club HAL
1. Register your children in the app or at the club on embarkation afternoon.
2. Name every adult allowed to collect younger children.
3. Ask for the week's schedule and the evening session times.
4. Check which sessions, if any, carry a charge.
5. Walk your teen past The Loft so they know where to find other teens early.

## Is Holland America right for your children's ages?
Holland America's guests skew older, and most sailings carry few children outside school holidays, as [Is Holland America Right for You](/articles/holland-america-honest-guide-canadians/) explains. That suits some families well: calm ships, long port days and fewer queues.

It is a natural fit for grandparents taking grandchildren to Alaska from Vancouver or Seattle, and for families who would rather hike in Juneau than ride a waterslide. [Alaska cruise with kids](/articles/alaska-cruise-with-kids-ages/) looks at which ages get the most out of it.

> **Canadian tip:** if grandparents are sailing with grandchildren without the parents, carry a signed consent letter from the parents, as the Government of Canada recommends. [Grandparents cruising with grandkids](/articles/grandparents-cruising-grandkids-without-parents/) covers the details.

## Matching the ship to your children's ages
Youth spaces differ from ship to ship across Holland America's fleet, from the Pinnacle-class ships to the older Volendam and Zaandam. Confirm your ship's club spaces, teen area and evening hours before you book.
