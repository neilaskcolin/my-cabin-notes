Smoking and vaping on a Disney ship are limited to a handful of marked outdoor areas, kept well away from the pools and kids clubs. Staterooms and verandahs are smoke-free, and Disney charges a US$250 fee if you smoke in either.

## Where are the smoking areas on Disney ships?
Each ship has two or three designated outdoor spots, and e-cigarettes follow the same rules as cigarettes. At the time of writing, Disney lists these areas:

| Ships | Designated outdoor areas |
|---|---|
| Disney Wish, Treasure, Destiny | Deck 14 forward, port side; Deck 14 aft, port side, through the Cove Bar on Deck 13; Deck 4 aft, port side. All open any time. |
| Disney Dream, Fantasy | Deck 4 aft on the promenade, port side (6 p.m. to 6 a.m.); Deck 12 outdoor area through the Meridian Lounge; Deck 13 forward near the Currents bar |
| Disney Magic, Wonder | Deck 4 starboard outdoors (6 p.m. to 6 a.m.); Deck 9 forward, port side |

Locations can change after refits, so check the Navigator app on the first day. On the Singapore-based Disney Adventure, vapes are banned outright because they are illegal in Singapore.

### What this means on an Alaska sailing
The Disney Magic and Disney Wonder sail Alaska from Vancouver. On those ships, the Deck 4 promenade spot only opens in the evening, leaving the Deck 9 area as the daytime option. On glacier-viewing days it can be cold and windy out there, so bring a warm layer.

## Smoke-free areas
Smoking and vaping are not allowed:

- in any stateroom or on any verandah
- anywhere indoors, including bars, lounges and restaurants
- on the pool decks and near the kids clubs
- on open decks outside the marked areas

At Castaway Cay and Lookout Cay, Disney's islands in the Bahamas, smoking is limited to marked areas too, set away from the family beaches.

## The US$250 stateroom recovery fee
Disney charges this to your onboard account if you smoke or vape in your stateroom or on your verandah. It pays for deep cleaning, including new air filters, carpet extraction and cleaning or replacing drapes and bedding. Smoke detectors and neighbouring guests both report it.

> **Watch out:** a family stateroom with a sofa bed and drapes holds smell for days. Even one cigarette on the verandah can trigger the fee, and the US dollar charge becomes roughly C$340–350 on a Canadian card.

## No casino, so no casino question
Most big lines allow smoking in part of the casino. Disney has no casino at all, so there is no indoor exception. For smokers, that means fewer places to go than on most big lines, but for families it means smoke-free air almost everywhere.

## Cannabis and Canadian guests
> **Canadian tip:** cannabis is legal in Canada but banned on Disney ships, including Alaska sailings that begin and end at Canada Place. Disney's rule applies regardless, and cannabis remains illegal under US federal law in Alaska ports. See [why you can't take cannabis on a cruise](/articles/cruise-cannabis-canada-why-no/).

Coming home through CBSA after a cruise, your tobacco exemption covers 200 cigarettes, 50 cigars or 200 grams of tobacco, among other limits. Duty applies to anything over that. More on shopping limits is in [onboard and duty-free shopping](/articles/cruise-shopping-onboard-duty-free/).

## How Disney compares
| Line | Smoking in the casino | Cabins and balconies |
|---|---|---|
| **Disney** | No casino on board | Banned |
| [Royal Caribbean](/articles/royal-caribbean-smoking-vaping-policy/) | Designated section on many ships | Banned |
| [Carnival](/articles/carnival-smoking-vaping-policy/) | Designated section on most ships | Banned |

Cabins and balconies are smoke-free on all three lines. The difference is indoors: Royal Caribbean and Carnival keep a casino smoking section, while Disney has no casino and no indoor smoking at all. ## Knowing the map before you sail
If smoking areas matter to you, the ship you choose makes a difference. The Wish-class ships have spots open all day, while the Magic and Wonder have fewer. Check the current list for your ship before you book.
