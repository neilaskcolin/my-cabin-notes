Choose the western Mediterranean if your dream trip is Rome, Florence, Barcelona and long lunches in famous cities. Choose the eastern Mediterranean if it's whitewashed islands, ancient ruins and sunsets over the Aegean. Either makes an excellent first cruise together.

## The three main Mediterranean routes
| | Western | Eastern and Greek Isles | Adriatic |
|---|---|---|---|
| Common start and end | Barcelona, Rome (Civitavecchia) | Athens (Piraeus), Rome, Istanbul | Venice area (Trieste, Ravenna or Monfalcone) |
| Highlights | Barcelona, Provence, Monaco, Florence, Rome, Naples and Amalfi | Santorini, Mykonos, Athens, Ephesus, Crete, Rhodes | Dubrovnik, Kotor, Split, Corfu |
| Pace | Busy, port almost every day | Mix of islands, gentler | Compact, walkable old towns |
| Best for couples who love | Art, food, big-name cities | Beaches, sunsets, ancient history | Medieval towns, coastal scenery |

Large cruise ships are no longer allowed into central Venice, so Adriatic itineraries now usually start at another port in the region. If history is your main draw, [the best Mediterranean ports for history lovers](/articles/best-mediterranean-ports-history-lovers/) goes deeper.

## What makes a sailing romantic
A few itinerary details make a big difference for couples:

- **Late departures and overnights.** A ship that stays until 10 or 11 p.m., or overnight, lets you have dinner ashore. Mykonos and Santorini evening departures are a highlight.
- **A sea day in the middle.** Seven port days in a row is tiring. One sea day gives you a spa morning and a long lunch on board (see [port days vs sea days](/articles/port-days-vs-sea-days-itinerary-balance/)).
- **A balcony.** In the Mediterranean you'll use it for sail-aways and coastal views almost every evening.
- **A food thread.** A cooking class ashore in Tuscany or a market tour in Provence, as in [cruise cooking classes](/articles/cruise-ship-culinary-classes-cooking-demos/).

For milestone touches like a private dinner or vow renewal, see [romantic cruise ideas for a big anniversary](/articles/romantic-anniversary-cruise-ideas/).

## Ports that need planning
### Rome and Florence aren't on the coast
Civitavecchia is roughly an hour and a half from Rome, and Livorno is about the same from Florence. That leaves a few hours in each city. Pick one or two sights, or spend extra nights in Rome before or after the cruise.

### Santorini can be crowded
Ships anchor and tender passengers to the old port, then everyone queues for the cable car or climbs several hundred steps. Go ashore early, or book a tour that lands at another part of the island. [Cruise ship tenders explained](/articles/cruise-ship-tenders-explained/) covers how tendering works.

> **Watch out:** July and August bring temperatures often in the mid-30s °C in Athens and Rome, plus the biggest crowds and highest fares. Couples over 50 usually enjoy late May or late September far more.

## Getting there from Canada
Toronto and Montreal have nonstop flights to Barcelona, Rome and Athens, some seasonal, on carriers including Air Canada and Air Transat. From Calgary, Edmonton, Winnipeg or Vancouver, expect one connection. Rome is six hours ahead of Toronto and nine ahead of Vancouver, so arrive at least a day or two early.

> **Canadian tip:** book an open-jaw flight, into the start city and home from the end city, and add two nights at one end. Canadians can visit the Schengen area visa-free for up to 90 days; check whether the EU's new travel authorisation applies by your dates, and check separately if your cruise visits Turkey.

> **Example:** a Vancouver couple celebrating 30 years might fly to Barcelona two days early, sail a 7-night western Mediterranean route ending near Rome, then spend two nights in Rome before flying home.

## Longer or repeat trips
Ten to twelve nights lets you combine regions, such as Rome to Athens via Sicily and the Greek islands, or focus on one country in depth. For a calmer alternative through the heart of Europe, compare a [Danube or Rhine river cruise](/articles/danube-rhine-river-cruises-canadians/). ## Choosing your first Mediterranean route
List the three places you'd most hate to miss, then pick the route that includes them. Add a sea day, a late departure and a couple of nights ashore at one end, and sail in shoulder season.
