Disney does not sell an unlimited drink package like most big lines do. Instead it offers multi-bottle wine packages and beer bundles, and it includes soft drinks with meals and at the pool-deck beverage stations, which takes care of most of what children drink.

## What Disney's drink packages cost
These are the main options at recent prices, before the 18% gratuity added to alcohol purchases. Prices change, so check the current list for your sailing.

| Package | What you get | Recent price (USD) |
|---|---|---|
| Classic wine package | 3, 4, 5 or 7 bottles | about $123–259 |
| Premium wine package | 3, 4, 5 or 7 bottles from a higher list | about $183–388 |
| Beer package | 6 bottles, with an insulated bag | about $49–65 |
| Beer 6-pack to your stateroom | 6 cans or bottles, delivered | about $36 |
| Refillable beer mug | Souvenir mug, larger pour at the standard price | about $15 |

Wine packages are usually bought on the first night at dinner. Your serving team moves with you through the rotational restaurants, so they can bring your next bottle wherever you eat. The stateroom six-pack can often be ordered online before you sail.

## Is Disney's wine package worth it for a couple?
It is worth it if you would order a bottle with dinner most nights anyway. It is not worth it if you are happy with a glass or two, because there is no refund for bottles you never open.

> **Quick math:** a couple sharing a bottle every night of a 7-night sailing would pay about US$259 for the classic 7-bottle package, plus 18%, so roughly US$306, or about C$415–430. That works out to around US$44 a bottle with gratuity.

On a 4-night Bahamas sailing with a day at Castaway Cay, you may only manage three dinners with wine. The 3-bottle package fits better than the 7.

## What's included on Disney without a package
- Fountain soft drinks, juice, milk, tea and coffee at the beverage stations and with meals.
- Water in the dining rooms.

Sodas ordered at a bar, bottled water, specialty coffees and all alcohol are extra. Because the basics are free, Disney sells no soda package and children rarely need anything paid. More on this in [what's included on a cruise drink menu](/articles/cruise-drink-menu-what-included-basics/).

## Ways to spend less
- **Use your carry-on allowance.** Each adult 21 or over can bring one bottle of wine or six beers at embarkation, under the rules introduced in June 2026. See the [Disney Cruise Line alcohol policy](/articles/disney-alcohol-policy/).
- **Pay by the drink on light nights.** A glass at dinner and a beer by the pool will not justify a package.
- **Watch the exchange rate.** Onboard charges post in US dollars, so a card with no foreign transaction fee saves about 2.5% on every drink. [How onboard payments work](/articles/how-onboard-payments-work-cruise/) explains the details.
- **Put onboard credit towards drinks.** Credit from a booking offer can pay your bar bill, as [onboard credit explained](/articles/onboard-credit-explained/) shows.

## How Disney compares
| Line | Package | Recent price per person per day | Catch |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Disney** | Beer and wine bundles | Varies by quantity | No unlimited package |
| [Royal Caribbean](/articles/royal-caribbean-drink-package-price/) | Deluxe Beverage | $70–105 USD + 18% | All adults in cabin must buy |
| [Carnival](/articles/carnival-drink-package-price/) | Cheers! | $70–75 USD + 20% | 15 alcoholic drinks a day |

Disney is cheaper than its rivals for light and moderate drinkers, because you only buy what you will drink and soft drinks are already free. Heavy cocktail drinkers pay more on Disney, with no daily cap to hide behind. ## Deciding on night one
Count the dinners where you would really order a bottle, pick the wine package that matches, and pay for everything else by the glass. On Disney, that simple rule usually beats any bundle.
