On a Princess ship you can smoke or vape only in marked outdoor areas, plus a designated part of the casino on some ships. Cabins and balconies are smoke-free across the whole fleet, and breaking that rule brings a cleaning fee of several hundred US dollars.

## Where can you smoke on a Princess ship?
- **Outdoor smoking areas:** a small number of marked spots on open decks, shown on the deck plans and in the daily programme in the Princess app.
- **The casino, on some ships:** a designated smoking section during play. Other ships have made their casinos fully non-smoking.
- **Vaping:** e-cigarettes and vapes follow the same rules as cigarettes. Use them only where smoking is allowed.

Locations differ between ships, so check your ship's deck plan before you sail.

## Where smoking and vaping are banned
- Every cabin and every balcony, on every ship
- All indoor public areas, including bars, restaurants, lounges and the theatre
- Most open decks outside the marked areas, including pool decks

The balcony ban is the one that catches people. Princess treats it as a fire-safety rule, because a dropped ember can travel down the ship's side. Neighbours and smoke detectors both report it.

## Who this affects most on Princess
Princess's guests skew towards couples over 50, and many book balconies specifically for fresh air and scenery. Smoke drifting from a neighbouring balcony is a common complaint, and the crew take it seriously.

On Alaska sailings from Vancouver, the smoking area will be outside, and open decks get cold and windy, especially in Glacier Bay or on a May or September sailing. Pack a warm layer you do not mind wearing out there. The [Alaska temperature guide by port](/articles/alaska-cruise-temperature-guide-by-port/) shows what to expect.

## The penalty
A cabin or balcony violation means a cleaning fee of several hundred US dollars added to your onboard account, which is a good deal more in Canadian dollars once converted. Repeat offences can lead to being put off the ship at the next port at your own expense.

> **Watch out:** cannabis is not allowed on Princess ships, even on a sailing from Vancouver where it is legal on shore, and that includes medical cannabis. Ships call at US ports, and taking cannabis across the border is illegal in both directions. [Why the answer is no](/articles/cruise-cannabis-canada-why-no/) explains the detail.

## Flying in with a vape
If you are flying from Calgary, Toronto or elsewhere to meet the ship, vapes and e-cigarettes must travel in your carry-on, not your checked bag, under Canadian airline rules. Spare batteries go in your carry-on too.

> **Good to know:** the ship's shops sell cigarettes at duty-free prices, but anything you bring home still counts against your CBSA allowance of 200 cigarettes. See [cruise duty-free limits for Canadians](/articles/cruise-shopping-onboard-duty-free/).

## How Princess compares
| Line | Smoking in the casino | Cabins and balconies |
|---|---|---|
| **Princess** | Varies by ship | Banned |
| [Holland America](/articles/holland-america-smoking-vaping-policy/) | Designated areas on most ships; not Noordam or Zuiderdam | Banned |
| [Celebrity](/articles/celebrity-smoking-vaping-policy/) | No — casinos are non-smoking | Banned |

All three lines ban smoking in cabins and on balconies. The real difference is the casino: on Princess it depends on the ship, Holland America keeps a smoking area for active players on most ships but not Noordam or Zuiderdam, and Celebrity's casinos are non-smoking. If a smoking section in the casino matters to you, check your specific Princess ship. All eight lines are compared in [Smoking and Vaping Rules on Cruise Ships](/articles/smoking-vaping-rules-cruise-ships/).
