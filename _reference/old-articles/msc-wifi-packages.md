MSC's internet comes in two tiers, Browse and Browse & Stream, sold per device. Bought before you sail for the whole cruise, they have recently cost roughly US$16–20 a day per device. Buy by the day on board and you will pay closer to US$31–33, so this is one extra worth sorting before you leave home.

## MSC wifi packages and prices
| Plan | What it handles | 7-night plan bought in advance | Daily plan bought on board |
|---|---|---|---|
| Browse | Email, web, social media and messaging apps; no video calls | About $112 (roughly C$150–160) | About $31 a day |
| Browse & Stream | Everything in Browse plus streaming and video calls | About $140 (roughly C$190–195) | About $33 a day |

Prices are per device, in US dollars, at recent rates. They vary by ship, sailing length and booking market, so confirm for your cruise. Extra devices can usually be added at a lower per-device price.

> **Quick math:** a Toronto couple on a 7-night MSC World America sailing, one on Browse & Stream and one on Browse, pays about US$252 bought in advance, or roughly C$340–355. Buying daily plans on board for the same week would be around US$448.

## Which MSC plan do you need?
Browse is enough for messages, email, maps and social media. Choose Browse & Stream if you need video calls home, plan to stream, or have to join a work meeting. Working from an MSC ship is possible, but [working remotely on a cruise ship](/articles/can-you-work-remotely-cruise-ship/) explains why a backup plan matters.

### Watch the device lock
At recent policy, the first device you connect is linked to the plan for the whole cruise. Log in first with the phone or laptop you actually want online.

## When wifi is included on MSC
- **MSC Yacht Club:** suite guests get an internet package as part of the ship-within-a-ship product.
- **Promotions:** MSC occasionally bundles or discounts wifi, so ask what your dates carry before buying.
- **Standard fares:** not included, whether you book Bella, Fantastica or Aurea.

## Buying MSC wifi, step by step
1. Decide how many devices need to be online and which tier each needs.
2. Buy full-cruise plans before sailing, through MSC or whoever holds your booking, when the price is lowest.
3. On board, connect to the ship's network and sign in through the MSC app or login page.
4. Connect your chosen device first, then add any extras.
5. Turn off automatic photo backups to save bandwidth.

## Speed and coverage
Speeds on MSC's newer ships have improved a lot with low-orbit satellite systems, and Caribbean sailings from Florida generally connect well. Coverage can slow in remote areas, during long ocean crossings such as MSC's Grand Voyages, and in the deepest fjords on the Alaska run. [How cruise ship internet works](/articles/cruise-ship-internet-how-it-works/) explains why.

## Phones, roaming and Canadian plans
> **Canadian tip:** put your phone in airplane mode with wifi on as soon as you board. The ship's own cellular network is billed at premium maritime roaming rates and is generally not covered by Canadian daily roaming plans. In port, your normal US or Caribbean roaming plan applies, and on MSC Poesia's Alaska sailings from Seattle, the call at Victoria puts you back on your home network.

[Cruise wifi and phone roaming for Canadians](/articles/cruise-wifi-phone-roaming-canadians/) covers each carrier's options.

## Getting wifi bundled instead of bought
Before paying, check whether your sailing carries a wifi promotion, then buy full-cruise plans in advance for each device that needs one. On MSC, timing makes a bigger difference to the price than the plan you choose.
