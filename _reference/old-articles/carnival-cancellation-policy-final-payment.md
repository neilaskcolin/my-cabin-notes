Carnival's final payment falls due about 76 days before sailing on 2–5 night cruises and about 91 days before on longer ones. Before that date a standard fare can be cancelled for a full refund. After it, the penalty grows in steps until the whole fare is gone, and Carnival's popular Early Saver fare plays by tougher rules from day one.

## When is Carnival's final payment due?
Carnival puts every sailing on one of two schedules:

- **Short schedule:** cruises of 2–5 nights, such as the 3- and 4-night Bahamas runs from Miami and Port Canaveral. Final payment is roughly 76 days out.
- **Long schedule:** cruises of 6 nights or more, plus every Alaska, European, Panama Canal and transatlantic sailing regardless of length. Final payment is roughly 91 days out.

That second rule catches people: a short Alaska or Europe sailing still uses the longer window. Your confirmation shows the exact date, and the general mechanics are in [cruise deposits and final payment dates explained](/articles/cruise-deposits-final-payment/).

## Carnival's cancellation penalties after final payment
Penalties are charged per guest, as the deposit or a share of the total fare, whichever is greater.

| Days before sailing (6+ nights, Alaska, Europe) | Days before sailing (2–5 nights) | Penalty per guest |
|---|---|---|
| 91 or more | 76 or more | None on standard fares |
| 90–56 | 75–56 | The deposit |
| 55–30 | 55–30 | Deposit or 50% of fare |
| 29–15 | 29–15 | Deposit or 75% of fare |
| 14 or fewer | 14 or fewer | 100% |

"Total fare" includes cruise charges, transfers, Carnival-booked hotel packages and air supplements. Government taxes and fees and prepaid gratuities are refunded. Restricted air booked through Carnival is non-refundable from booking.

> **Example:** a couple from Edmonton on a 7-night Western Caribbean sailing from Galveston, paying US$1,100 each, cancels 40 days out. They are in the 50% band, so they lose US$550 each, roughly C$1,485–1,540 for the pair, plus any non-refundable flights to Houston.

## How Early Saver changes the picture
Early Saver is Carnival's discounted early-booking fare with built-in price protection, covered in [Carnival price drop after booking](/articles/carnival-price-drop-after-booking/). The catch is the deposit. Cancel before final payment and you get a future cruise credit equal to the deposit minus US$50 per person, valid for a new booking within 12 months, rather than cash. Carnival's terms describe it as usable on US-dollar Carnival sailings. After final payment, the normal penalty table applies.

Choose Early Saver when your dates are firm. How credits like this work, and expire, is in [future cruise credit explained](/articles/future-cruise-credit-explained/).

## How to cancel a Carnival cruise without losing extra
1. Check your confirmation for the fare type and the exact final payment date.
2. Count back from departure to see which penalty band you are in today.
3. Cancel through whoever holds the booking, Carnival or your agency. The penalty is based on the day Carnival receives the cancellation, so don't wait for the weekend.
4. Ask for written confirmation showing the refund, any credit, and the refunded taxes and gratuities.
5. Send that invoice to your insurer straight away if you are claiming.

> **Watch out:** dropping one guest from a two-person cabin can leave the remaining traveller paying a single rate that costs nearly as much as two. Ask for a quote first; [single supplements](/articles/single-supplement-how-to-dodge/) explains the pricing.

## Insurance for Canadians cancelling a Carnival cruise
Provincial health plans don't cover cancellation, and only partly cover medical care outside Canada. Carnival sells its own protection plan, but Canadian third-party policies usually carry higher medical limits and pay cash rather than credit for covered reasons. Some must be bought within days of your deposit to cover pre-existing conditions. The trade-offs are in [travel insurance vs cruise line protection plans](/articles/travel-insurance-vs-cruise-protection/), and [cancel for any reason cover](/articles/cancel-for-any-reason-cruise-insurance-explained/) is the option for uncertain plans.

## Picking the right Carnival fare for your certainty level
Early Saver rewards people whose dates won't move; a standard fare protects everyone else until final payment. Decide which you are before you pay the deposit, and put the final payment date in your calendar the day you book.
