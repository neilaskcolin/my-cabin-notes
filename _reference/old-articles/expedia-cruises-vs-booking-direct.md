Canadians searching "Expedia Cruises" are usually weighing one question: is there any point going through a consultant when the cruise line's website is right there? The fare is the same either way, so the real comparison is about extras, service and who handles the booking when something changes.

## Is the price the same?
Yes. Cruise lines control their pricing and don't allow agents to sell below it. Book on the line's site or through an Expedia Cruises consultant and the fare for a given cabin on a given sailing is identical. The mechanics are in [is it cheaper to book through a travel agent](/articles/cheaper-book-cruise-travel-agent/).

What can differ is what comes attached to that fare, and whether a fare in Canadian dollars or US dollars works out cheaper on the day you book.

## Booking direct vs through a consultant

| | Booking direct | Through a consultant |
|---|---|---|
| Fare | Line's published price | Same published price |
| Extras | Line's current promotion only | Same promotion, often plus onboard credit or similar |
| Price drops | You watch and ask yourself | Consultant can watch and request a reprice |
| Changes and problems | You call the line | Your consultant deals with the line |
| Comparing lines | One website and account per line | One contact for every major line |
| Flights, hotels, insurance | Line's air program or book separately | Can be packaged around the cruise |
| Loyalty credit | Earned | Earned, once your number is on the booking |

## What the consultant adds
Extras attached to the booking, commonly onboard credit and sometimes prepaid gratuities or other perks, explained in [what are Expedia Extras](/articles/what-are-expedia-extras/). A named person who watches your sailing for price drops and reprices it, as in [cruise price drop after final payment](/articles/price-drop-after-booking-cruise/). One contact for every cruise line, so comparing Princess against Holland America for Alaska doesn't mean two websites and two accounts. And someone to call when a flight is cancelled or a port is dropped.

## What Expedia Cruises actually is
A franchise network of local consultant offices across Canada and the US, not the Expedia.com booking site. Consultants work with every major line and can also book flights, hotels and insurance around the cruise. Most work by phone, email and video as much as in person, so where your consultant sits matters less than that they are Canadian-based and can quote in CAD.

> **Good to know:** Ontario, Quebec and British Columbia license travel agencies through their provincial regulators, and Ontario and Quebec run compensation funds that can help if a registered agency or supplier fails. Whoever you book with in those provinces, it is reasonable to ask for their registration number.

## When booking direct makes sense
- A line-exclusive offer that hasn't been released to agents. It is rare, but it happens.
- A casino offer tied to your player account, as covered in [casino offers and free cruises](/articles/casino-offers-free-cruises-explained/).
- You enjoy managing everything yourself in the line's website and app.
- You booked a future cruise on board during a sailing. Many lines let you move that booking to an agent within a set window afterwards (see [transferring a cruise booking to a travel agent](/articles/transfer-cruise-booking-to-travel-agent/)).

For a first cruise, a complex fly-cruise or a group, those cases are the minority.

## What doesn't change either way
Your cabin, the onboard experience, the line's cancellation and final payment rules, and your loyalty status are the same however you book. You can still use the line's app for check-in, dining reservations and planner purchases with an agent booking. If you are weighing the line's air program against your own flights, [cruise line air vs your own flights](/articles/cruise-line-air-vs-own-flights/) applies to both routes.

## The honest trade-off
You give up nothing on price. You take on a relationship, which is the point for most people and a mild nuisance for a few who would rather click through it all themselves.

## Trying it without committing
Asking a consultant to quote a sailing you're already looking at costs nothing and shows you exactly which extras apply. If the quote matches the line's price with nothing added, you have lost two minutes; if extras appear, compare them with the line's own offer and choose.
