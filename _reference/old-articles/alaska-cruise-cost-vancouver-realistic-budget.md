For a couple who live in Metro Vancouver, a 7-night Alaska cruise from Canada Place typically costs about C$3,500–7,200 all-in for an inside cabin, or roughly C$5,000–9,700 with a balcony, at recent prices. The big advantage is what you skip: no flights and no hotel night.

## What's in a realistic Vancouver budget?
Figures are per couple for a 7-night round trip from Canada Place. Cruise charges are usually in US dollars, so they're shown as approximate Canadian equivalents.

| Item, per couple | Inside cabin | Balcony cabin |
|---|---|---|
| Cruise fare | C$1,900–3,100 | C$3,250–5,600 |
| Taxes and port fees, if not included | C$540–840 | C$540–840 |
| Getting to Canada Place and back | C$10–160 | C$10–160 |
| Gratuities | C$340–490 | C$340–490 |
| Drinks, without a package | C$150–560 | C$150–560 |
| Wifi, if needed | C$0–280 | C$0–280 |
| Excursions, one or two each | C$400–1,400 | C$400–1,400 |
| Travel medical insurance | C$150–500 | C$150–500 |
| **Rough total** | **C$3,500–7,200** | **C$5,000–9,700** |

Fares run roughly US$700–1,100 per person for an inside cabin in May or September, and US$1,200–2,000 for a balcony, with July and August higher. [When Alaska cruise prices drop](/articles/when-do-alaska-cruise-prices-drop/) covers timing.

## What Vancouver residents don't pay
Compared with the [Calgary to Alaska cost breakdown](/articles/calgary-alaska-cruise-cost/), a Lower Mainland couple saves the return flight (C$300–700) and the pre-cruise hotel (C$250–450), and usually spends less getting to the terminal. That's roughly C$550–1,200 back in your pocket.

You also save time. Most locals can sleep at home, board around midday and be home by late morning on disembarkation day, usually needing a vacation day or two fewer than travellers flying in. Still leave a buffer: summer weekends can bring several ships, and heavy traffic, into downtown at once.

### Getting to the ship
- **SkyTrain:** the Expo and Canada lines both reach Waterfront station, a short walk from Canada Place. At the time of writing, TransLink charges a one-zone fare for all zones on weekends and weekday evenings.
- **Taxi or rideshare:** roughly C$40–80 each way from Burnaby, Richmond or Surrey.
- **Driving:** terminal parking runs about C$210–280 for a week. [Vancouver cruise parking options](/articles/vancouver-cruise-terminal-parking/) compares cheaper lots.
- **From Victoria or the Sunshine Coast:** add a BC Ferries crossing and allow a buffer for sailing waits.

> **Watch out:** many Vancouverites assume an Alaska cruise is a "domestic" trip. It isn't. Every Alaska port is in the US, and MSP covers very little outside Canada, so buy travel medical insurance and bring valid passports. See [passports for an Alaska cruise from Vancouver](/articles/passport-alaska-cruise-vancouver/).

## Where to put the money you save
With no airfare, many local couples upgrade in one of two places.

The first is the balcony. Alaska is the itinerary where outdoor space pays off most, with hours of scenic cruising and a glacier day. The premium over an inside cabin, often C$1,300–2,500 per couple, is easier to justify when the trip has no flight costs.

The second is one standout excursion, such as Juneau whale watching (about US$150–250 per person) or a glacier flightseeing trip (US$400 and up). See [are Alaska shore excursions worth it](/articles/alaska-shore-excursions-worth-it/) to choose.

> **Example:** a Burnaby couple who take SkyTrain on a Saturday, book a May inside cabin at US$800 each, skip wifi and the drink package, and choose one modest excursion each would land near C$3,800. The same couple in a July balcony with a helicopter tour and a few drinks a day could pass C$9,000.

## Other Vancouver options worth pricing
### One-way to Whittier or Seward
One-way Gulf of Alaska sailings reach Glacier Bay or Hubbard Glacier and finish near Anchorage. You'll need a flight home, often C$400–900 per person, but you see more of Alaska. [Round-trip vs one-way Alaska](/articles/roundtrip-vs-oneway-alaska/) compares the two.

### Seattle departures
Seattle sometimes has lower fares or a ship you want, about three hours' drive away. Add the border crossing, fuel and US parking or transit, and compare the total.

## Habits that save Vancouver cruisers money
- Prepay gratuities in Canadian dollars at booking, if your line allows it.
- Bring wine on board within the line's allowance.
- Use a credit card with no foreign transaction fees; the onboard account bills in US dollars.
- Walk Ketchikan and Skagway on your own rather than booking tours in every port.
- Sail in May or September for lower fares and fewer families.

## Comparing your options
Compare the full cost, not the headline fare. Ask whether a quote includes taxes, gratuities or onboard credit. Two cabins at similar fares can differ by several hundred dollars once those extras are counted.
