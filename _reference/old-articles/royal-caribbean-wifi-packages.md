Royal Caribbean's wifi, called VOOM, costs roughly US$20–30 per device per day at recent prices, and the cheapest way to buy it is in the Cruise Planner before you sail. Since the line moved its fleet onto Starlink satellite internet, speeds are good enough for most people to stream and video call.

## Royal Caribbean wifi packages and prices
VOOM is sold per device for every day of the cruise. Royal Caribbean has offered a basic Surf tier for messaging, email and browsing, and a Surf + Stream tier that adds video streaming and calls. Tiers and names change, so check what's offered for your sailing. Multi-device plans lower the price per device, and on board you can usually log out of one device and in on another.

Prices vary by ship and date. The pre-cruise price is usually below the on-board rate, and sales in the weeks before sailing can cut it further.

| Who's travelling | What to buy | Approx. 7 nights (USD) | Roughly in CAD |
|---|---|---|---|
| Couple who check in once a day | 1 device, shared | $140–210 | C$190–295 |
| Couple both working or posting | 2 devices | $280–420 | C$380–585 |
| Family with two teens | 3–4 device plan | $420–700 | C$570–980 |

> **Quick math:** daily price × devices × nights, minus any multi-device discount, then × 1.35–1.40 for Canadian dollars. Buying a 7-night, two-device plan at a US$5 daily sale saving keeps about C$95 in your pocket.

## How fast is Royal Caribbean wifi?
Royal Caribbean has rolled out Starlink across its ships, and on most sailings browsing, messaging and video calls work comfortably. Expect slowdowns at peak times, such as sea-day afternoons on a full Oasis- or Icon-class ship, and occasional gaps in remote stretches or deep fjords on Alaska sailings. How the technology works is covered in [how cruise ship internet actually works](/articles/cruise-ship-internet-how-it-works/), and remote workers should read [can you work remotely from a cruise ship](/articles/can-you-work-remotely-cruise-ship/).

## What works without buying wifi
The Royal app runs on the ship's network for free. You can read the Cruise Compass, book shows and dining, and check your SeaPass account without a package. Messaging through the app has been available on many ships, sometimes for a fee, so check yours.

> **Good to know:** your Canadian phone can run up big charges at sea, where it connects to the ship's own cellular network at premium rates. In US ports such as Juneau or Miami it switches to land networks, where your carrier's US roaming plan applies. Turn off data roaming before you board and use wifi.

### Getting online, step by step
1. Check whether your fare or suite already includes VOOM.
2. Watch the Cruise Planner price in the weeks before sailing and buy on a sale.
3. Decide how many devices really need to be online at the same time.
4. Set your phone to airplane mode with wifi on as soon as you board.
5. Log in on the ship's network with your SeaPass details.

The general approach for Canadians is in [cruise wifi and phone roaming for Canadians](/articles/cruise-wifi-phone-roaming-canadians/).

Carnival and Norwegian are the lines most often weighed against Royal Caribbean. Royal Caribbean now costs about the same as Carnival per device and less than Norwegian's list price, and its fleet-wide Starlink rollout gives it some of the more reliable speeds at sea. Norwegian is likelier to bundle wifi into the fare. The full side-by-side for all eight lines is in [Cruise Wifi and Phone Roaming for Canadians](/articles/cruise-wifi-phone-roaming-canadians/).

## Getting wifi bundled instead of bought
For connected families, a sailing that includes VOOM can be worth several hundred dollars. Ask whether your dates carry a wifi promotion before you pay the deposit, and if not, buy in the Cruise Planner and keep checking for a sale.
