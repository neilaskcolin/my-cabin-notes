If Norwegian drops the price of your cruise before final payment, you can usually ask for the lower fare on the same ship, date and cabin category. The catch is that Norwegian sells cruises as bundles, so a lower fare is only a saving if it carries the same Free at Sea perks.

## Can you get a lower price on NCL after booking?
Usually, yes, until final payment, which on Norwegian falls about 120 days before sailing. The new fare must be one currently advertised for your exact sailing and category, and the request goes through whoever holds your booking. After final payment the window effectively closes.

Norwegian changes fares and promotions often, sometimes weekly, so a drop a few months after you book is common. Checking matters more on Norwegian than on lines with simpler pricing.

## Why the Free at Sea bundle matters more than the sticker
Most Norwegian fares include Free at Sea extras such as the Unlimited Open Bar package, specialty dining, wifi and shore excursion credit, and the mix rotates. A newer, cheaper fare might carry fewer perks or different ones.

| Illustrative example, couple | Current booking | New "lower" fare |
|---|---|---|
| Fare, 7-night balcony | US$3,600 | US$3,450 |
| Perks included | Drinks, dining, wifi, excursion credit | Drinks and wifi only |
| Extras you would now pay for | None | About US$400 |
| Real cost | US$3,600 | About US$3,850 |

On paper the new fare saves US$150. In practice it costs about US$250 more once you replace the specialty dinners and excursion credit. [Onboard credit vs a cash discount](/articles/cruise-onboard-credit-vs-cash-discount/) explains how to value extras like these.

## How to ask Norwegian to reprice, step by step
1. Save a screenshot of your current booking, including every Free at Sea perk and its service charge.
2. Price the identical sailing, ship and cabin category today, with the same number of guests.
3. Compare totals: fare, perks, perk service charges and any onboard credit.
4. If the new total is genuinely lower, ask for the reprice before final payment.
5. Check the revised invoice line by line, especially the perks, before accepting it.

> **Watch out:** repricing on a non-refundable deposit fare can involve fees or lost extras. Read the rules when you book, as explained in [cruise fare classes explained](/articles/cruise-fare-classes-refundable/).

## What about after final payment?
Once the balance is paid, Norwegian rarely passes on a lower fare. The realistic wins are upgrades: Norwegian's Upgrade Advantage bidding program lets you offer a price for a higher cabin category, and the [cabin upgrade bids explained](/articles/cabin-upgrades-bids-explained/) page covers how much to offer.

## Solo travellers, note
Norwegian's studio cabins are priced for one person, and their fares move on their own schedule, separately from double-occupancy cabins. Watch them separately, as covered in [solo cruising from Canada](/articles/solo-cruising-canada-supplements/).

## The Canadian check
Norwegian quotes Canadian residents in Canadian dollars at its own exchange rate. If you spot a lower US-dollar fare on a US site, convert it before concluding it is cheaper, and remember that a weaker loonie can make a CAD fare move even when the USD fare hasn't. ## Getting the whole-package comparison done
Working out whether a new NCL fare beats your current bundle takes a side-by-side of perks, not just price. Do it before the 120-day mark, and if a licensed cruise consultant holds your booking, ask them to run the comparison each time the fare moves.
