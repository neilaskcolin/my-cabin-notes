Bought on its own, Princess's Plus Beverage Package has recently cost roughly US$65–75 per person per day, plus an 18% service charge. Most guests who want drinks covered are better off with Princess Plus, around US$60 a day, which bundles the same package with wifi and crew appreciation.

## How much is the Princess drink package?
| Option | Recent price per person per day | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Plus Beverage Package (standalone) | US$65–75 + 18% | Drinks up to a per-drink cap |
| Princess Plus fare add-on | About US$60 | Drinks package, MedallionNet wifi, crew appreciation |
| Princess Premier fare add-on | Higher than Plus | Premier drinks with a higher cap, more wifi devices, extra perks |

In Canadian dollars, Princess Plus works out to roughly C$81–84 per person per day. Treat these as a guide and confirm for your sailing.

### What's included
Cocktails, spirits, wine by the glass, beer, specialty coffees, sodas, juices and bottled water, up to a per-drink price of around US$15 on the Plus package at recent policy. Pricier drinks cost the difference. There is also a daily limit on alcoholic drinks, and the specialty coffee comes into its own on a cold Glacier Bay morning.

### The both-adults rule
Princess requires all adults in a stateroom to buy the standalone package if one does. The Princess Plus fare is applied per booked guest.

## Is the Princess drink package worth it?
Bought alone, the package pays off at around five or six drinks per person per day, once the 18% is added. That is a lot of drinking on an Alaska sailing, where you may spend long port days ashore in Juneau, Skagway or Ketchikan.

Princess Plus is easier to justify, because part of the price covers things many guests buy anyway.

> **Quick math:** take the US$60 Princess Plus price, subtract about US$18 for crew appreciation and US$15–25 for a day of wifi. That leaves roughly US$17–27 a day for drinks, about two cocktails. If you would not otherwise buy wifi, the drinks share is closer to US$42, or three to four drinks.

For a couple on a 7-night sailing, Princess Plus comes to about US$840, roughly C$1,135–1,175. The standalone package for two at US$70 plus 18% is about US$1,155 before you have paid for wifi or gratuities.

## Who it suits, and who should skip it
Princess Plus tends to work for:
- Couples who want wifi and drink steadily from lunch onward
- Guests who like specialty coffees and a glass of wine with dinner every night
- Anyone who wants the onboard bill fixed in Canadian dollars before sailing

Skip it if you:
- Drink one or two glasses a day, mostly at dinner
- Plan full port days ashore and early nights
- Are happy to use your free wine allowance instead

## Cheaper alternatives
Light drinkers can pay per drink and bring one bottle of wine per adult free, with extra bottles allowed for a corkage fee, as set out in the [Princess alcohol policy](/articles/princess-alcohol-policy/). Bought on a CAD booking before sailing, any package is priced in Canadian dollars. Bought on board, it posts in US dollars, often with a foreign-transaction fee on Canadian cards, as explained in [paying in CAD vs USD for a cruise](/articles/paying-cad-vs-usd-cruise/).

## Getting Princess Plus bundled instead
Princess often runs promotions that include Princess Plus or discount it heavily. For a couple who would buy it anyway that is worth hundreds of dollars, so check your dates before paying for drinks separately.
